## Introduction

Kotor's Cats Museum is one of [Montenegro]({{ 'where-montenegro-keeps-its-strangest/' | relative_url }})'s smallest museums, yet its cultural influence far exceeds its physical size. Housed in a modest space inside Kotor's UNESCO-listed old town, it presents an eclectic private collection of cat-related books, prints, postcards, coins, advertisements, manuscripts and memorabilia dating mainly from the sixteenth century to around 1970. On the surface it is an eccentric specialist museum. More interestingly, it demonstrates how a private collector's project helped transform ordinary street cats into one of Kotor's strongest modern tourist symbols.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.catsmuseum.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: catsmuseum.org">[Cats Museum]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">catsmuseum.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Cats Museum Home</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Cats MuseumHome - Cats Museum...</span></span></span>

That distinction matters. Visitors often assume that Kotor has been known as "the city of cats" for centuries. Cats have indeed long lived around the historic port, but the museum itself openly argues that many of the stories now repeated to tourists are modern inventions or heavily embellished legends. Rather than preserving an ancient civic tradition unchanged through time, the museum has actively participated in creating the contemporary image of Kotor as a feline city.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.catsmuseum.org/cats-museum-cattaro/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: catsmuseum.org">[Cats Museum]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">catsmuseum.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Cats MuseumCats Museum – Cattaro - Cats Museum...</span></span></span>

## What the Cats Museum Actually Contains

Unlike a natural history museum, the Cats Museum is not about biology or local wildlife. It is built around the cultural history of cats in art and everyday life.

The collection includes:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Antique books and illustrated manuscripts.
* Prints, engravings and paintings.
* Historic postcards and greeting cards.
* Coins, medals and tokens featuring cats.
* Advertising artwork and commercial packaging.
* Newspapers, magazines and other printed ephemera.
* Small curiosities and memorabilia collected from several European traditions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.catsmuseum.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: catsmuseum.org">[Cats Museum]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">catsmuseum.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Cats Museum Home</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Cats MuseumHome - Cats Museum...</span></span></span>

</div>

According to the museum, much of the original collection developed after a substantial donation from Countess Francesca di Montereale Mantica, whose cat-themed collection formed the nucleus around which later acquisitions were added. The displays span several centuries and draw particularly from Italian, French, Austro-German and Spanish material, reflecting the international history of cat imagery rather than the history of Montenegro alone.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.catsmuseum.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: catsmuseum.org">[Cats Museum]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">catsmuseum.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Cats Museum Home</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Cats MuseumHome - Cats Museum...</span></span></span>

The museum therefore resembles an old-fashioned cabinet of curiosities organised around one subject. Individual objects are often modest, but together they reveal how cats have appeared in domestic decoration, advertising, satire, literature, religion and popular culture across Europe.

## From Private Collection to City Branding

The museum's greatest significance lies outside its display cases.

Its founders state that when the museum opened in 2013, the idea of dedicating a museum to cats was widely regarded as eccentric. They also argue that Kotor had not yet developed today's overwhelming commercial association with cats. Since then, cat imagery has spread across souvenir shops, postcards, magnets, cafés and visitor photography until it has become one of the city's defining tourist brands.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.catsmuseum.org/cats-museum-cattaro/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: catsmuseum.org">[Cats Museum]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">catsmuseum.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Cats MuseumCats Museum – Cattaro - Cats Museum...</span></span></span>

Whether every element of that claim can be measured precisely is less important than the broader mechanism it illustrates. The museum did not merely respond to an existing identity; it consciously promoted one.

Several factors reinforced each other:

<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.kotor.me/en/general-news/new-official-museums-of-kotor-web-site/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: kotor.me">[kotor]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">kotor.me</span><span class="citation-popover-title">NE W OFFICIAL MUSEUMS OF KOTOR WEB SITE &#124; Opstina Kotor</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">NE W OFFICIAL MUSEUMS OF KOTOR WEB SITE &#124; Opstina Kotor</span></span></span> or already had a conspicuous population of friendly street cats.

* Visitors naturally photographed the animals wandering through medieval streets.
* The museum supplied a coherent narrative linking cats to the city's identity.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HS87e2wHXCQ" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: youtube.com">[youtube.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">youtube.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Kotor: A City of Cats in Montenegro!</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">New Cat Capital? Today many visitors arrive expecting cats almost as much as medieval architecture, even though the city's UNESCO World Heritage status rests on its maritime and architectural heritage rather than its feline population.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.montenegro.travel/en/business/press/news/welcome-to-kotor-montenegro-s-medieval-town-that-s-a-cat-lover-s-dream" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: montenegro.travel">[montenegro.travel]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">montenegro.travel</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Welcome to Kotor, Montenegro’s medieval town that’s a cat lover’s dream &#124; Montenegro...</span></span></span>

## Historic Tradition or Modern Tourist Folklore?

This is where the museum becomes especially interesting within Montenegro's wider landscape of unusual collections.

Many travel articles repeat colourful stories explaining why Kotor is the "city of cats". Popular versions claim cats saved the city from plague by killing rats, protected Kotor during Ottoman sieges or have always enjoyed a special civic status stretching back through the Venetian period. Some explanations point more plausibly to the town's history as a busy Adriatic port, where ships commonly carried cats to control rodents before leaving animals behind in harbour towns.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/cats-museum" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: atlasobscura.com">[Atlas Obscura]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">atlasobscura.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Atlas Obscura Cats Museum in Kotor</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Atlas ObscuraCats Museum in KotorJune 27, 2017...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 27, 2017</span></span></span>

The museum itself takes a notably sceptical position. Its official account explicitly dismisses several of the most popular legends as fantasies without historical foundation. Rather than reinforcing romantic myths, it argues that these stories developed only after cats became a successful tourist theme.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.catsmuseum.org/cats-museum-cattaro/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: catsmuseum.org">[Cats Museum]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">catsmuseum.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Cats MuseumCats Museum – Cattaro - Cats Museum...</span></span></span>

That makes the museum unusual among visitor attractions. Instead of claiming an ancient, unbroken tradition, it openly acknowledges the modern process by which tourism reshapes local identity.

The distinction is important:

* **Historically well supported:** Kotor has long had visible street cats and a maritime environment where cats would have been useful for pest control.
* **Reasonably supported:** Local people have generally shown tolerance towards the animals, and feeding points and volunteer care have helped maintain their visibility in the old town.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://mymontenegro.org/eng/attractions/the-city-of-cats-kotor" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: mymontenegro.org">[My Montenegro]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">mymontenegro.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">My Montenegro Why Kotor is a city of cats? ## Why the Museum Matters Beyond Cat Lovers

For visitors uninterested in cats, the museum can initially seem like a novelty attraction. Its real value lies elsewhere.

It provides a rare example of a museum influencing the identity of its host city rather than simply interpreting it. Most museums preserve existing heritage. The Cats Museum has helped create a new layer of heritage by encouraging residents, businesses and visitors to view Kotor through a feline lens.

It also illustrates how modern tourist folklore develops. Once cat imagery became commercially successful, stories explaining that symbolism multiplied. Some became accepted almost as local history despite having weak documentary foundations. The museum's willingness to distinguish marketing from evidence makes it unusually self-aware within the world of niche museums.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.catsmuseum.org/cats-museum-cattaro/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: catsmuseum.org">[Cats Museum]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">catsmuseum.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Cats MuseumCats Museum – Cattaro - Cats Museum...</span></span></span>

Within Montenegro's network of unusual collections, this gives the Cats Museum a distinctive role. It is not the country's strangest collection because of bizarre objects or supernatural claims. Instead, it demonstrates how an eccentric private collection, careful branding and an already photogenic population of street cats combined to reshape how an internationally famous historic town presents itself to the world.
