## Introduction

The Saltmen of Zanjan are among Iran’s most unusual archaeological discoveries: human remains preserved naturally inside the Chehrabad salt mine, near Zanjan in north-western Iran. Unlike deliberately made mummies, these bodies were preserved by the environment itself. Salt-rich deposits removed moisture from the bodies and slowed decay, leaving behind evidence of ancient miners, their clothing, tools, health and working conditions. The discoveries matter because they turn a strange-looking archaeological curiosity into a detailed record of ordinary people involved in dangerous underground labour.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://saltmen-of-iran.com/index.php/en/research/archaeology" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: saltmen-of-iran.com">[saltmen-of-iran.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">saltmen-of-iran.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Chehrabad Saltmummy &amp; Saltmine Exploration Project</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">October 23, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 23, 2020</span></span></span>

The Saltmen are not a single mysterious body but a group of individuals discovered over several decades. Archaeologists have used the remains and associated objects to reconstruct episodes of salt mining from different periods of Iran’s past, including the Achaemenid, Parthian and Sasanian eras. Their value lies less in the mystery of preservation and more in the scientific information recovered from a rare combination of human remains, textiles, tools and a preserved mining environment.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.toiran.com/index.php/en/city-zanjan/museums/Archeology-Museum/7746" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: toiran.com">[ToIran, Tourism Platform]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">toiran.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">To Iran, Tourism Platform Archeology Museum &#124; To Iran, Tourism Platform</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">To Iran, Tourism Platform Archeology Museum &#124; To Iran, Tourism Platform</span></span></span>

## The discovery in Chehrabad salt mine

The first Saltman was discovered accidentally in 1993 when workers at the Chehrabad (Douzlākh) salt mine encountered human remains during mining activity. The find included a remarkably preserved head with hair and beard, part of a leather boot, clothing fragments and objects associated with mining life. Early investigation identified the individual as an ancient miner preserved within the salt deposits rather than a person intentionally buried through a funerary process.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://saltmen-of-iran.com/index.php/en/research/archaeology" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: saltmen-of-iran.com">[saltmen-of-iran.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">saltmen-of-iran.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Chehrabad Saltmummy &amp; Saltmine Exploration Project</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">October 23, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 23, 2020</span></span></span>

Further discoveries followed after additional remains were found in 2004. Rescue excavations in 2004 and 2005 revealed more human remains and, importantly, the archaeological context around them. Instead of isolated bodies, researchers began to understand the mine as a place where accidents had occurred and where workers, tools and everyday objects had become preserved together. Later international research projects brought together specialists in archaeology, mining history, anthropology, conservation and scientific analysis.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://saltmen-of-iran.com/index.php/en/research/archaeology" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: saltmen-of-iran.com">[saltmen-of-iran.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">saltmen-of-iran.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Chehrabad Saltmummy &amp; Saltmine Exploration Project</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">October 23, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 23, 2020</span></span></span>

The number of identified individuals has been refined through continued study. Earlier reports often referred to six Saltmen, while later research has suggested a minimum of eight individuals when fragmented remains and additional evidence are considered. This changing count reflects the difficulty of working with ancient remains recovered from a damaged mining environment rather than a conventional burial site.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.anthropology.uw.edu.pl/13/bne-13-03.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: anthropology.uw.edu.pl">[anthropology.uw.edu.pl]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">anthropology.uw.edu.pl</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Bioarchaeology of the Near East</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Bioarchaeology of the Near East</span></span></span>

The discoveries are now associated mainly with the Archaeology Museum of Zanjan, located in the historic Zolfaghari Mansion, while parts of the collection are also held by the National Museum of Iran in Tehran. The displays present the Saltmen alongside archaeological material from the mine, allowing visitors to see them not simply as preserved bodies but as evidence of ancient work and technology.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.toiran.com/index.php/en/city-zanjan/museums/Archeology-Museum/7746" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: toiran.com">[ToIran, Tourism Platform]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">toiran.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">To Iran, Tourism Platform Archeology Museum &#124; To Iran, Tourism Platform</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">To Iran, Tourism Platform Archeology Museum &#124; To Iran, Tourism Platform</span></span></span>

## What the preserved remains reveal


<div class="content-callout content-callout--key content-callout--takeaway" markdown="1">

The Saltmen provide rare evidence about the lives of ancient miners. Their clothing and equipment show that these were working individuals who entered the mine with practical gear rather than people preserved for ceremonial reasons. One well-preserved individual was found with clothing including a tunic, trousers, shoes, a belt and a fur cape, along with small containers and a knife. These discoveries allow researchers to study ancient textiles, personal equipment and working practices in unusual detail.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://saltmen-of-iran.com/index.php/en/research/archaeology" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: saltmen-of-iran.com">[saltmen-of-iran.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">saltmen-of-iran.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Chehrabad Saltmummy &amp; Saltmine Exploration Project</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">October 23, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 23, 2020</span></span></span>

</div>

The mine itself adds another layer of evidence. Archaeologists recovered tools linked to salt extraction, including mining implements, and studied how mining techniques changed over time. The associated remains of plants and food traces also provide clues about diet and the relationship between miners and the surrounding landscape. Together, these discoveries show a community of workers connected to a wider economic system rather than isolated victims of a disaster.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://saltmen-of-iran.com/index.php/en/research/archaeology" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: saltmen-of-iran.com">[saltmen-of-iran.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">saltmen-of-iran.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Chehrabad Saltmummy &amp; Saltmine Exploration Project</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">October 23, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 23, 2020</span></span></span>

Scientific examination has also revealed details about health and injury. Several Saltmen show trauma consistent with sudden mining accidents, supporting the interpretation that collapses or other underground events caused their deaths. The injuries are important because they connect the bodies directly to the risks of ancient mining rather than treating them as unexplained archaeological curiosities.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.antiquity.ac.uk/projgall/aali333" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: antiquity.ac.uk">[Antiquity Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">antiquity.ac.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Antiquity JournalAncient salt mining and salt men: the interdisciplinary Chehrabad Douzlakh project in north-western Iran &#124; Antiquity Jou...</span></span></span>

The remains have also contributed information about diet and disease. Research on one Saltman identified evidence of intestinal parasites, including tapeworm eggs, providing clues about ancient food practices and health conditions in Iran. ## Science behind the mystery

The unusual appearance of the Saltmen comes from natural mummification. Salt absorbs moisture, and dry conditions slow the activity of bacteria and other organisms responsible for decomposition. In Chehrabad, the salt environment created conditions that preserved soft tissue, hair, leather and textiles that would normally disappear after thousands of years.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://saltmen-of-iran.com/index.php/en/research/archaeology" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: saltmen-of-iran.com">[saltmen-of-iran.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">saltmen-of-iran.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Chehrabad Saltmummy &amp; Saltmine Exploration Project</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">October 23, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 23, 2020</span></span></span>

The preservation has allowed researchers to use methods that are rarely possible with ordinary archaeological remains. Studies have included radiocarbon dating to establish chronology, textile analysis to identify materials and manufacturing methods, isotope research to investigate aspects of life history, and biological analysis of human and animal remains. The international Chehrabad research programme has involved institutions including Iranian heritage authorities, the Deutsches Bergbau-Museum Bochum, Ruhr University Bochum, the University of Oxford and the University of Zurich.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.antiquity.ac.uk/projgall/aali333" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: antiquity.ac.uk">[Antiquity Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">antiquity.ac.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Antiquity JournalAncient salt mining and salt men: the interdisciplinary Chehrabad Douzlakh project in north-western Iran &#124; Antiquity Jou...</span></span></span>

A key point in interpreting the Saltmen is separating evidence from legend. Their preservation may appear extraordinary, but researchers do not need supernatural explanations to account for it. The available evidence points towards a combination of geological conditions, mining accidents and unusual survival of organic material. The real importance of the Saltmen is that nature created an accidental archive of ancient human activity.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://saltmen-of-iran.com/index.php/en/research/archaeology" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: saltmen-of-iran.com">[saltmen-of-iran.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">saltmen-of-iran.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Chehrabad Saltmummy &amp; Saltmine Exploration Project</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">October 23, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 23, 2020</span></span></span>

    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
