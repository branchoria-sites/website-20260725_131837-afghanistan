## Introduction

Falconry heritage in the [United Arab Emirates]({{ 'united-arab-emirates/' | relative_url }}) is unusual because its most important “collections” are not simply rows of historic objects behind glass. Instead, they combine preserved equipment, living birds, specialist knowledge and demonstrations of care, creating a rare opportunity to explore the long relationship between people and birds of prey. In the UAE, falconry is presented as a living cultural tradition rather than a relic of the past, making it one of the country's most distinctive examples of how museums and heritage institutions preserve human–animal bonds. UNESCO recognises falconry as an item of Intangible Cultural Heritage, highlighting not only hunting techniques but also mentoring, craftsmanship, conservation and the enduring partnership between falconers and their birds.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/falconry-a-living-human-heritage-01708" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ich.unesco.org">[UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ich.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Intangible Cultural Heritage Falconry, a living human heritage</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UNESCO Intangible Cultural HeritageFalconry, a living human heritage - UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage...</span></span></span>

Rather than focusing on trophies or exotic specimens, UAE falconry collections invite visitors to understand why a trained falcon became a trusted hunting companion, a symbol of identity and, today, an ambassador for wildlife conservation. This combination of artefacts, practical knowledge and living practice gives falconry heritage a distinctive place within the country's wider landscape of unusual collections.

## How Falcons Became Symbols Of UAE Heritage

Before modern development transformed the Emirates, falcons were valued for their practical role in helping desert communities hunt game during harsh seasons. Over generations, the relationship evolved into something that extended well beyond utility. Training a falcon required patience, consistency and detailed observation of the bird's behaviour, creating a partnership based on trust rather than domestication in the conventional sense. Experience Abu Dhabi notes that this relationship remains one of the country's defining cultural traditions and continues to be passed through families and specialist communities.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://visitabudhabi.ae/en/plan-your-trip/culture-and-traditions/falconry" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: visitabudhabi.ae">[Experience Abu Dhabi]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">visitabudhabi.ae</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Experience Abu Dhabi Falconry &#124; Culture and Tradition &#124; Experience Abu Dhabi</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Experience Abu Dhabi Falconry &#124; Culture and Tradition &#124; Experience Abu Dhabi</span></span></span>

UNESCO's description of falconry places equal emphasis on the emotional and ethical dimensions of the practice. Modern falconers are expected to understand bird welfare, habitat conservation and responsible care alongside traditional skills. The organisation specifically identifies the bond between falconer and bird, together with equipment makers, breeding centres and falcon hospitals, as essential parts of the living heritage rather than secondary additions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/falconry-a-living-human-heritage-01708" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ich.unesco.org">[UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ich.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Intangible Cultural Heritage Falconry, a living human heritage</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UNESCO Intangible Cultural HeritageFalconry, a living human heritage - UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage...</span></span></span>

This perspective helps explain why UAE heritage collections are unusual. Visitors are not simply looking at historical hunting equipment; they are seeing objects that only make sense when understood as part of a continuing relationship between humans and wild birds.

## Falcon Care Tools And Displays

One of the clearest examples of this approach is the museum incorporated into the Abu Dhabi Falcon Hospital. Unlike a conventional natural history museum, the displays combine historical equipment with the everyday realities of caring for living falcons.

Visitors encounter collections including:

## Desert Hunting Traditions And Identity

Falconry collections also preserve knowledge that cannot easily be displayed in a cabinet. Historic equipment gains meaning only when interpreted alongside desert hunting traditions, seasonal migration, wildlife knowledge and the etiquette surrounding falcon ownership.

Museum interpretation commonly explains that successful falconry depended on reading landscapes, weather and prey behaviour as much as controlling the bird itself. The equipment therefore represents practical adaptations to life in the desert rather than luxury possessions. A hood, glove or lure becomes evidence of an entire system of knowledge built around cooperation between human and raptor.

The UAE also presents falconry as an important element of national identity associated with the legacy of Sheikh Zayed bin Sultan Al Nahyan, who strongly promoted both traditional falconry and wildlife conservation. Modern interpretation therefore balances historical hunting practices with contemporary messages about sustainable management, captive breeding and habitat protection.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://visitabudhabi.ae/en/plan-your-trip/culture-and-traditions/falconry" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: visitabudhabi.ae">[Experience Abu Dhabi]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">visitabudhabi.ae</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Experience Abu Dhabi Falconry &#124; Culture and Tradition &#124; Experience Abu Dhabi</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Experience Abu Dhabi Falconry &#124; Culture and Tradition &#124; Experience Abu Dhabi</span></span></span>

Rather than celebrating hunting alone, today's heritage displays increasingly frame falconry as a cultural practice that evolved from survival into stewardship.

## Living Heritage Beyond Museum Objects

Falconry heritage in the UAE demonstrates that some collections cannot be separated from living practice. The knowledge survives because experienced falconers continue to train newcomers, specialist craftspeople still produce equipment, veterinarians develop advanced medical care, and birds remain active participants rather than preserved specimens.

This living dimension is central to UNESCO's recognition of falconry. The tradition is transmitted through mentoring, families, clubs and specialist institutions instead of relying solely on written records or museum catalogues. Supporting organisations—including falcon hospitals, breeding centres and conservation agencies—are treated as part of the heritage network because they preserve both cultural knowledge and healthy bird populations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/falconry-a-living-human-heritage-01708" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ich.unesco.org">[UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ich.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Intangible Cultural Heritage Falconry, a living human heritage</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UNESCO Intangible Cultural HeritageFalconry, a living human heritage - UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage...</span></span></span>

For visitors interested in unusual collections, this creates a distinctive experience. Instead of viewing a static display about extinct customs, they encounter a heritage system where historical artefacts, practical demonstrations, veterinary science and conservation work remain closely connected.

## Why Falconry Collections Matter Within The UAE's Curiosity Landscape

Compared with countries known for collections of occult objects, cryptids or eccentric cabinets of curiosities, the UAE offers a different kind of cultural fascination. Falconry collections reveal how ordinary-looking objects—a leather hood, a glove or a perch—carry stories about cooperation between humans and wild animals that have developed over centuries.

They also demonstrate how heritage can be preserved without freezing it in time. Museums and visitor centres document traditional craftsmanship while remaining linked to active practitioners, making the collections simultaneously historical, educational and contemporary.

Within the UAE's broader landscape of unusual heritage, falconry stands out because it preserves not simply objects but an enduring relationship. The most remarkable exhibit is not any individual artefact but the continuing partnership between falconer and bird, showing how cultural identity can be expressed through care, knowledge and mutual dependence as much as through material collections.

      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
