## Introduction

The [Central African Republic]({{ 'central-african-republic/' | relative_url }})'s most remarkable musical heritage is not preserved in a single museum gallery. Instead, it is scattered across carved instruments in Bangui, historic field recordings held by international archives, and living performance traditions that continue in forest communities. This makes the country's musical collections unusual from a heritage perspective: visitors may see an instrument in a museum, hear its sound in an overseas archive, and discover that its deepest meaning still survives in ceremonies rather than display cases.



<img src="{{ "/assets/images/Central_African_Repu_358788_musical_archives_ins_684f88-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="Sound Archives illustration 1" data-theme-src-dark="{{ "/assets/images/Central_African_Repu_358788_musical_archives_ins_684f88-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{ "/assets/images/Central_African_Repu_358788_musical_archives_ins_684f88-Illustration-1-light.svg" | relative_url }}" loading="eager" decoding="sync" fetchpriority="high">

For anyone interested in unusual collections, this creates a different kind of museum experience. The story is not simply about rare objects, but about how sound, craftsmanship and cultural memory have become separated by conflict, collecting history and international research, while remaining connected through documentation and living practice.

## When a Museum Collection Can Still Be Heard

Unlike paintings or sculptures, musical instruments were created to produce sound. A carved harp or ceremonial drum loses part of its meaning when it sits silently behind glass. In the Central African Republic, this challenge is especially visible because the surviving collections are split between physical artefacts and extensive sound recordings made during twentieth-century ethnomusicological expeditions.

The Barthélémy Boganda National Museum has long included traditional musical instruments among its ethnographic collections, alongside masks, hunting equipment and [ritual objects]({{ 'ritual-objects-fdf1d8/' | relative_url }}). These instruments represent many of the country's cultural groups rather than a single musical tradition. Damage and looting during the civil conflict disrupted access to much of the collection, making documentation and conservation as important as public display.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boganda_National_Museum" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Boganda National Museum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Boganda National Museum</span></span></span>

At the same time, researchers working in Central Africa made thousands of recordings that captured the sounds of these instruments in use. Those recordings now survive in archives and digital collections far beyond the country's borders, creating an unusual situation in which the soundscape has often proved easier to access than the original objects.

## Carved harps and drums as sculpture

Many traditional Central African instruments are striking works of carving in their own right. Their makers shaped wood not only for appearance but also for resonance, balance and durability, meaning that artistic decoration and acoustic design are inseparable.

Among the instruments associated with communities in the Central African Republic are:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* **Carved harps**, whose wooden frames often display carefully shaped necks, resonators and decorative carving while remaining functional musical tools.
* **Ceremonial drums**, carved from single tree trunks, whose proportions determine their distinctive sound as much as their visual appearance.
* **Musical bows and simple string instruments**, built from readily available natural materials yet capable of complex musical expression.
* **Horn and flute ensembles**, fashioned from bamboo, roots, antelope horns and other local materials, demonstrating remarkable craftsmanship despite their seemingly modest appearance.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://folkways.si.edu/central-african-republic-banda-polyphony/world/music/album/smithsonian" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: folkways.si.edu">[Smithsonian Folkways Recordings]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">folkways.si.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on si.edu.</span></span></span>

</div>

For museum visitors, these instruments occupy an unusual space between sculpture, engineering and ceremonial object. They are aesthetically impressive even before their musical purpose is understood, but their full significance only emerges when linked to recordings or performances.

## Aka polyphony and ceremonial performance

The country's best-known musical heritage is not an object at all but a living tradition. The polyphonic singing of the Aka people was inscribed by UNESCO on the Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity because of its exceptional complexity and its central role in community life.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/polyphonic-singing-of-the-aka-pygmies-of-central-africa-00082?RL=00082" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ich.unesco.org">[ICH UNESCO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ich.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ICH UNESCOPolyphonic singing of the Aka Pygmies of Central Africa</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage...</span></span></span>

Rather than existing as concert music, Aka polyphony accompanies important moments including hunting rituals, funerals and the establishment of new forest camps. Every participant contributes independent vocal lines that combine into continuously changing textures through improvisation rather than written notation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/polyphonic-singing-of-the-aka-pygmies-of-central-africa-00082?RL=00082" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ich.unesco.org">[ICH UNESCO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ich.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ICH UNESCOPolyphonic singing of the Aka Pygmies of Central Africa</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage...</span></span></span>

Although the voices attract most attention, the performances are also linked to specific instruments. UNESCO identifies the use of percussion, a local drum, a harp-like instrument and a single-string musical bow, each associated with particular occasions rather than serving as interchangeable accompaniment. These instruments are therefore best understood as components of ritual practice rather than museum specimens alone.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/polyphonic-singing-of-the-aka-pygmies-of-central-africa-00082?RL=00082" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ich.unesco.org">[ICH UNESCO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ich.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ICH UNESCOPolyphonic singing of the Aka Pygmies of Central Africa</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage...</span></span></span>

This relationship between object and performance explains why even an expertly preserved instrument cannot fully represent Aka musical culture without the songs, dances and social setting that give it meaning.

## Recordings, objects and archives abroad

A distinctive feature of Central African musical heritage is that some of its richest documentation is now held outside the country.

Beginning in the 1960s and 1970s, ethnomusicologist Simha Arom and other researchers made extensive field recordings across the Central African Republic. Their work documented Aka vocal traditions as well as Banda instrumental ensembles using carved drums, horns, flutes and other locally made instruments. Many of these recordings were released through UNESCO's Collection of Traditional Music and are now distributed digitally by Smithsonian Folkways, allowing listeners worldwide to hear performances that were originally captured in remote communities.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://folkways.si.edu/central-african-republic-banda-polyphony/world/music/album/smithsonian" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: folkways.si.edu">[Smithsonian Folkways Recordings]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">folkways.si.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on si.edu.</span></span></span>

Other recordings, photographs and documentation are preserved in specialist research archives such as the CNRS Centre for Research in Ethnomusicology in France. These collections combine audio, images and field notes, preserving details about performers, instruments and ceremonial contexts that might otherwise have disappeared.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://archives.crem-cnrs.fr/archives/collections/CNRSMH_E_1994_010_001/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: archives.crem-cnrs.fr">[archives.crem-cnrs.fr]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">archives.crem-cnrs.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Collection: CNRSMH_E_1994_010_001 - CREM-CNRS - Sound archives of the CNRS and the Musee de l&#x27;Homme. Research Centre of Ethnomusicology...</span></span></span>

This international dispersal has advantages and drawbacks. Overseas archives have often provided stable long-term preservation and digitisation, making historically important recordings publicly accessible. At the same time, the physical instruments, archival recordings and descendant communities are frequently separated by thousands of kilometres, making it difficult to experience them together.

## Why this matters in Central African Republic's curiosity landscape

For readers exploring unusual collections, the Central African Republic offers a reminder that the most intriguing archive may not consist of shelves of rare objects but of sounds that remain connected to living communities.

A carved harp in Bangui, a field recording preserved in Paris or Washington, and an Aka ceremony performed today are all parts of the same cultural record. None is complete without the others. The object reveals craftsmanship, the recording preserves sound, and the community maintains the knowledge that gives both their meaning.

That combination makes the country's dispersed musical heritage one of its most distinctive cultural collections: a museum that extends beyond its walls, where the archive can still be heard as well as seen.

    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
</script>
