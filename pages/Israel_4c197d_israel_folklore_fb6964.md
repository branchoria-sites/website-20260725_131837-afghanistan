## Introduction

[Israel]({{ 'where-israels-strangest-collections/' | relative_url }})’s hidden folklore collection is not a museum of ghosts or a cabinet of supernatural objects, but an archive of voices: thousands of stories recorded from communities whose beliefs, memories and everyday experiences might otherwise have disappeared. The central institution is the Israel Folktale Archives (IFA), founded in 1955 by Professor Dov Noy and now housed at the University of Haifa. It preserves more than 25,000 tales gathered from many cultural groups, making it one of the most important repositories of Jewish and Israeli oral storytelling traditions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://lib.haifa.ac.il/asai/en/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lib.haifa.ac.il">[lib.haifa.ac.il]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lib.haifa.ac.il</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ארכיון הסיפור העממי בישראל על שם דב נוי (אסע&quot;י</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ארכיון הסיפור העממי בישראל על שם דב נוי (אסע&quot;י</span></span></span>

## Collecting tales from diverse communities

The Israel Folktale Archives began during a period when Israel was receiving large numbers of immigrants from different regions. Dov Noy and his collaborators aimed to record oral traditions from newly arrived communities as well as long-established groups before changing lifestyles caused many storytelling traditions to fade. The archive was originally connected with Haifa’s Museum of Ethnology and Folklore before later becoming part of the University of Haifa.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://lib.haifa.ac.il/asai/en/about-ifa/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lib.haifa.ac.il">[lib.haifa.ac.il]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lib.haifa.ac.il</span><span class="citation-popover-title">About IFA – ארכיון הסיפור העממי בישראל על שם דב נוי (אסע&quot;י</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">About IFA – ארכיון הסיפור העממי בישראל על שם דב נוי (אסע&quot;י</span></span></span>

The collection’s unusual strength is its range. Rather than representing one national mythology, it captures many overlapping traditions. The archive includes stories from Jewish communities with roots across the Middle East, North Africa, Europe and Ethiopia, as well as narratives from Arab, Bedouin, Christian, Muslim and Druze communities living in Israel. UNESCO recognised the Israel Folktale Archives as part of the Memory of the World programme in 2017, highlighting its value as documentary heritage.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.unesco.org/fr/memory-world/israel-folktale-archives" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: unesco.org">[UNESCO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">IFA – Archives Israéliennes du Conte Populaire nommées en l’honneur de Dov Noy</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">IFA – Archives Israéliennes du Conte Populaire nommées en l’honneur de Dov Noy - Memory of the World...</span></span></span>

A key feature of the archive is that it preserves not only the plot of a story but also information about the storyteller and the circumstances of recording. This matters because folklore is not simply a collection of old tales; it is a record of who told them, why they mattered and how communities used stories to explain their world. The archive classifies stories by themes, storytellers, places, traditions and international folklore categories, allowing researchers to compare how similar ideas appear across different cultures.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ifa.haifa.ac.il/index.php/en/contact-us-sp-11296" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ifa.haifa.ac.il">[ifa.haifa.ac.il]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ifa.haifa.ac.il</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ארכיון הסיפור העממי בישראל על שם דב נוי (אסע&quot;י</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Contact Us...</span></span></span>

## Legends, spirits and cultural memory

Supernatural storytelling forms one of the most memorable parts of Israel’s folklore landscape. Many recorded tales involve encounters with unseen forces, religious figures, mysterious visitors or beings that exist between the human and spiritual worlds. These stories do not function as evidence of paranormal events; they are cultural records showing how communities understood danger, morality, faith and uncertainty.

Jewish folklore preserved in Israel contains many traditions involving angels, demons, spirits and miraculous events. Such stories often developed through centuries of religious interpretation and community storytelling rather than through a single fixed tradition. A tale about a supernatural encounter might explain a moral lesson, reinforce religious values or preserve memories of a particular place or person.

The same pattern appears across Israel’s many communities. Stories about holy people, hidden powers or mysterious events often connect the supernatural with everyday concerns: protecting a household, explaining unusual events, remembering ancestors or marking important religious moments. The supernatural element makes these tales fascinating, but their deeper importance lies in what they reveal about social values and collective memory.

The archive’s role is therefore closer to cultural archaeology than to proving legends. Researchers examine how stories change as they move between communities, languages and generations. A tale that appears in different regions may keep a similar structure while adapting its characters, locations or religious references to match the people telling it.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://lib.haifa.ac.il/asai/en/about-ifa/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lib.haifa.ac.il">[lib.haifa.ac.il]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lib.haifa.ac.il</span><span class="citation-popover-title">About IFA – ארכיון הסיפור העממי בישראל על שם דב נוי (אסע&quot;י</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">About IFA – ארכיון הסיפור העממי בישראל על שם דב נוי (אסע&quot;י</span></span></span>

## Why oral stories matter today


<div class="content-callout content-callout--key content-callout--takeaway" markdown="1">

Oral traditions are fragile collections. Unlike archaeological objects, they can disappear when a language declines, when older storytellers pass away or when younger generations stop learning traditional narratives. The Israel Folktale Archives preserve this intangible heritage by recording, organising and making stories available for research.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://lib.haifa.ac.il/asai/en/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lib.haifa.ac.il">[lib.haifa.ac.il]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lib.haifa.ac.il</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ארכיון הסיפור העממי בישראל על שם דב נוי (אסע&quot;י</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ארכיון הסיפור העממי בישראל על שם דב נוי (אסע&quot;י</span></span></span>

</div>

For modern audiences, the archive challenges the idea that folklore is only about ancient myths. Many stories reveal recent histories of migration, displacement and adaptation. A supernatural tale from an immigrant community may also preserve memories of a village, a family tradition or a way of understanding life before migration.

This makes Israel’s folklore archives unusual within the country’s wider landscape of mysterious and historic collections. A visitor searching for strange objects may find ancient manuscripts, archaeological discoveries or religious artefacts elsewhere, but the IFA preserves something less visible: the stories people carried with them. Its collection shows that legends, beliefs and supernatural narratives are not separate from history; they are part of how communities remember themselves.

The archive is also a reminder that folklore requires careful interpretation. A legend can be culturally significant without being historically verified. The value of these stories lies not only in whether a spirit, miracle or mysterious event happened, but in what the story reveals about fear, hope, identity and belonging. In that sense, Israel’s folklore archives are a museum of human imagination as much as a record of the past.

    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
