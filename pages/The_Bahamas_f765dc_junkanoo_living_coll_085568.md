## Introduction

Junkanoo costumes are designed to be temporary. They are built for movement, competition, music and a single dramatic appearance in the streets rather than for long-term display. Yet some of [the Bahamas]({{ 'where-the-bahamas-keeps-its-strangest/' | relative_url }})' most distinctive cultural collections are dedicated to preserving these fragile creations after the parade ends. Instead of treating them as disposable festival props, museums, community organisations and veteran Junkanoo artists preserve costumes, banners, instruments and working techniques as evidence of a living tradition that continues to evolve.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.bahamasmuseumofjunkanoo.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bahamasmuseumofjunkanoo.com">[Buy Junkanoo Tickets]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bahamasmuseumofjunkanoo.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Buy Junkanoo Tickets -The Bahamas Junkanoo Museum &#124; Bahamas&#x27; Top Attraction</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Buy Junkanoo Tickets -The Bahamas Junkanoo Museum &#124; Bahamas&#x27; Top Attraction</span></span></span>

## What happens when Junkanoo costumes stop moving?

The most striking feature of a Junkanoo costume is that it is incomplete without performance. Its full effect depends on dancing, spinning, music, lighting and the energy of hundreds of participants moving through the streets. Once the parade finishes, the costume loses the context for which it was designed.

Museums therefore preserve more than decorative objects. They preserve evidence of craftsmanship, competition and community identity. A costume on display represents months of collaborative labour by designers, builders, musicians and dancers who often work together in neighbourhood "shacks" or workshops throughout the year.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.bahamas.com/blog/making-of-a-junkanooer" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bahamas.com">[The Islands of The Bahamas]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bahamas.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Islands of The Bahamas Making of a Junkanooer</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Islands of The BahamasMaking of a Junkanooer - The Official Website of The BahamasJune 30, 2017...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 30, 2017</span></span></span>

Many museum displays also include photographs, video, instruments and oral histories so visitors can understand how apparently delicate paper constructions became spectacular moving artworks. Rather than pretending the museum version replaces the parade, curators generally present it as one chapter in the costume's life.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.bahamasmuseumofjunkanoo.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bahamasmuseumofjunkanoo.com">[Buy Junkanoo Tickets]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bahamasmuseumofjunkanoo.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Buy Junkanoo Tickets -The Bahamas Junkanoo Museum &#124; Bahamas&#x27; Top Attraction</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Buy Junkanoo Tickets -The Bahamas Junkanoo Museum &#124; Bahamas&#x27; Top Attraction</span></span></span>

## How Junkanoo costumes are made and used

Modern Junkanoo costumes are the result of months of design and experimentation. Themes may draw on Bahamian history, wildlife, mythology, architecture or international subjects, with every competing group interpreting its chosen theme differently.

Although techniques have changed over time, the tradition has long embraced whatever materials are available. Historical accounts describe early costumes incorporating natural materials, sponge, straw and scraps, while later generations increasingly used cardboard, crepe paper, wire, paste and lightweight framing. Today's costumes often combine traditional methods with modern materials while still relying heavily on hand-cut paper decoration.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://educulturebahamas.com/history-of-junkanoo/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: educulturebahamas.com">[educulturebahamas.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">educulturebahamas.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">History of Junkanoo – Educulture Bahamas Junkanoo Museum and Resource Centre</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">History of Junkanoo – Educulture Bahamas Junkanoo Museum and Resource Centre</span></span></span>

The making process is itself part of Bahamian heritage. Artists cut thousands of individual paper fringes, construct wire frames, paint surfaces and assemble elaborate wearable structures that must be both visually impressive and light enough to dance in for hours. The costume's success depends as much on engineering and movement as on appearance.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.bahamas.com/blog/making-of-a-junkanooer" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bahamas.com">[The Islands of The Bahamas]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bahamas.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Islands of The Bahamas Making of a Junkanooer</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Islands of The BahamasMaking of a Junkanooer - The Official Website of The BahamasJune 30, 2017...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 30, 2017</span></span></span>

## Museums, workshops and community archives

The Bahamas has developed several visitor-facing spaces that preserve Junkanoo beyond parade season, each with a slightly different purpose.

The **Bahamas Museum of Junkanoo** presents itself as a permanent home for collecting, preserving and communicating the festival's history. Rather than displaying costumes as isolated artworks, it explains the festival's origins, artistic techniques, social importance and continuing evolution. Its stated mission includes supporting Junkanoo artisans alongside preserving their work, recognising that knowledge is as valuable as surviving objects.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.bahamasmuseumofjunkanoo.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bahamasmuseumofjunkanoo.com">[Buy Junkanoo Tickets]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bahamasmuseumofjunkanoo.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Buy Junkanoo Tickets -The Bahamas Junkanoo Museum &#124; Bahamas&#x27; Top Attraction</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Buy Junkanoo Tickets -The Bahamas Junkanoo Museum &#124; Bahamas&#x27; Top Attraction</span></span></span>

**Educulture Bahamas** combines a small museum with demonstrations, historical interpretation and practical explanations of costume construction. Visitors encounter costume fragments, historical examples, musical instruments and presentations showing how techniques have changed across generations. The emphasis is educational rather than simply spectacular.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.bahamas.com/plan-your-trip/things-to-do/educulture-bahamas-ltd" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bahamas.com">[The Islands of The Bahamas]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bahamas.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Islands of The Bahamas Educulture Bahamas Ltd</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Islands of The BahamasEduculture Bahamas Ltd. - Explore The Bahamas - The Official Website of The Bahamas...</span></span></span>

## Preserving temporary art without freezing tradition

Conserving Junkanoo costumes presents unusual practical problems.

Unlike textiles designed for museums, parade costumes are often assembled from paper, glue and lightweight materials that fade, tear or become brittle with age. Large three-dimensional structures also require significant storage space, while many were never intended to survive beyond a competition season. Conservation therefore involves careful environmental control, repairs and, in some cases, preserving representative sections rather than entire costumes.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.degruyterbrill.com/document/doi/10.1515/9780822388296-027/html?lang=en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: degruyterbrill.com">[De Gruyter Brill]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">degruyterbrill.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">De Gruyter Brill DOCUMENT: Destroying While Preserving Junkanoo: The Junka</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">De Gruyter Brill DOCUMENT: Destroying While Preserving Junkanoo: The Junka</span></span></span>

There is also a deeper cultural question. Junkanoo changes every year. Groups compete through innovation, introducing new themes, techniques and visual ideas. If museums only preserve older masterpieces, they risk presenting the festival as something finished rather than continually reinvented.

For this reason, many Bahamian heritage organisations increasingly preserve:

* interviews with veteran designers and builders;
* demonstrations of costume-making techniques;
* musical traditions and instruments;
* photographs and video recordings of complete performances;

Within the Bahamas' wider landscape of unusual collections, Junkanoo museums occupy a distinctive place because they preserve objects that were never intended to become museum pieces.

A shipwreck artefact or colonial document survives by accident. A Junkanoo costume survives only through a deliberate decision to rescue something built for a few hours of public performance. This challenges conventional ideas about what museums collect. Instead of valuing permanence, these collections celebrate creativity, community effort and performance that would otherwise disappear almost completely.

That perspective has become even more significant as Junkanoo has gained wider international recognition as an important element of Bahamian cultural heritage.     if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
</script>
