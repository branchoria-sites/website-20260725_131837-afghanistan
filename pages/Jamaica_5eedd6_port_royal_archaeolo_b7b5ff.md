## Introduction

Port Royal is one of [Jamaica]({{ 'where-jamaicas-legends-meet-lost/' | relative_url }})’s most unusual archaeological sites because it is not simply a ruined town: it is a partially submerged 17th-century city preserved beneath the waters of Kingston Harbour. Destroyed by a powerful earthquake on 7 June 1692, the settlement that became famous through pirate legends also became one of the Caribbean’s most important underwater archaeology sites. Excavations have revealed streets, buildings, household objects and commercial spaces that capture a moment of colonial life interrupted by disaster.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1595/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: whc.unesco.org">[UNESCO World Heritage Centre]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">whc.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">World Heritage Centre The Archaeological Ensemble of 17th Century Port Royal</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UNESCO World Heritage CentreThe Archaeological Ensemble of 17th Century Port Royal - UNESCO World Heritage Centre...</span></span></span>

For visitors interested in strange histories and unusual collections, Port Royal offers something different from a conventional museum. Its “collection” is a landscape: a real town lost beneath the sea, with recovered objects and archaeological records showing how people lived, traded and worked before catastrophe struck. The site also challenges the popular image of Port Royal as only a pirate haven, revealing a more complicated story involving commerce, colonial power, enslaved labour and everyday urban life.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1595/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: whc.unesco.org">[UNESCO World Heritage Centre]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">whc.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">World Heritage Centre The Archaeological Ensemble of 17th Century Port Royal</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UNESCO World Heritage CentreThe Archaeological Ensemble of 17th Century Port Royal - UNESCO World Heritage Centre...</span></span></span>

## The 1692 earthquake and lost settlement

### How Port Royal disappeared beneath the sea

Before the disaster, Port Royal was a major English port in the Caribbean, positioned at the entrance to Kingston Harbour. Its deep-water harbour helped it become a centre of trade, naval activity and privateering during the late 17th century. The town’s wealth was reflected in its dense urban layout of houses, shops, warehouses, churches and defensive structures.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1595/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: whc.unesco.org">[UNESCO World Heritage Centre]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">whc.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">World Heritage Centre The Archaeological Ensemble of 17th Century Port Royal</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UNESCO World Heritage CentreThe Archaeological Ensemble of 17th Century Port Royal - UNESCO World Heritage Centre...</span></span></span>

On 7 June 1692, a severe earthquake struck Jamaica. Much of Port Royal collapsed as sections of the town were destroyed and submerged beneath water and sand. The disaster was not simply a case of buildings being washed away over centuries; parts of the settlement were preserved because they sank rapidly, leaving archaeological remains close to their original positions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1595/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: whc.unesco.org">[UNESCO World Heritage Centre]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">whc.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">World Heritage Centre The Archaeological Ensemble of 17th Century Port Royal</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UNESCO World Heritage CentreThe Archaeological Ensemble of 17th Century Port Royal - UNESCO World Heritage Centre...</span></span></span>

The survival of these remains is what makes Port Royal exceptional. Many archaeological sites show layers of rebuilding and gradual change, but Port Royal provides evidence from a single historical moment: a working town caught at the point of destruction. The underwater deposits preserved parts of the urban plan, structures and objects connected to daily activities.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://jamaicaportroyal.com/archaeology.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: jamaicaportroyal.com">[jamaicaportroyal.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">jamaicaportroyal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Archaeological Research in Port Royal</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Archaeological Research in Port Royal</span></span></span>

The disaster also changed how the town is remembered. Popular culture often describes Port Royal as a “pirate city”, but archaeology shows that it was also a functioning colonial port with merchants, artisans, administrators and residents from different social backgrounds. The UNESCO assessment of the site highlights both its maritime importance and its connections to the wider systems of colonial trade, including the trafficking of enslaved Africans.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1595/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: whc.unesco.org">[UNESCO World Heritage Centre]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">whc.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">World Heritage Centre The Archaeological Ensemble of 17th Century Port Royal</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UNESCO World Heritage CentreThe Archaeological Ensemble of 17th Century Port Royal - UNESCO World Heritage Centre...</span></span></span>

## Underwater archaeology and recovered objects

### A city preserved through marine archaeology

Port Royal became a landmark case in underwater archaeology, the study of human history through remains found beneath water. Archaeologists investigate not only individual objects but also their locations, relationships and surroundings. A bottle, tool or plate becomes much more informative when found inside a building, workshop or storage area where its original use can be reconstructed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://jnht.com/site_port_royal_underwater_archaeology.php" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: jnht.com">[jnht.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">jnht.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Jamaica National Heritage Trust</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Jamaica - Port Royal Underwater Archaeology...</span></span></span>

Early investigations began in the 19th and early 20th centuries, but major archaeological work expanded in the 1960s and later through cooperation between Jamaican heritage authorities, Texas A&M University and the Institute of Nautical Archaeology. The 1981–1990 Port Royal project focused on submerged areas around the commercial centre of the old town, including remains near Queen Street and High Street.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://jnht.com/site_port_royal_underwater_archaeology.php" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: jnht.com">[jnht.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">jnht.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Jamaica National Heritage Trust</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Jamaica - Port Royal Underwater Archaeology...</span></span></span>

Researchers found evidence of different kinds of buildings and businesses. Investigated structures included brick buildings and simpler constructions, with artefacts suggesting activities such as shoemaking, food preparation and trade. These discoveries transformed Port Royal from a legendary “sunken pirate town” into a detailed archaeological record of an actual Caribbean community.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://jamaicaportroyal.com/archaeology.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: jamaicaportroyal.com">[jamaicaportroyal.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">jamaicaportroyal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Archaeological Research in Port Royal</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Archaeological Research in Port Royal</span></span></span>

### What the recovered objects reveal

The objects recovered from Port Royal help recreate ordinary life rather than only dramatic stories of pirates. Archaeological collections include ceramics, glass bottles, metal tools, pewter objects, tobacco pipes and other household and commercial items. These finds show connections between local activity and international trade networks reaching across Europe, Asia and the wider Atlantic world.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1595/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: whc.unesco.org">[UNESCO World Heritage Centre]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">whc.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">World Heritage Centre The Archaeological Ensemble of 17th Century Port Royal</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UNESCO World Heritage CentreThe Archaeological Ensemble of 17th Century Port Royal - UNESCO World Heritage Centre...</span></span></span>

Some discoveries are memorable because they appear almost frozen in place. Archaeological investigations have documented objects preserved within building contexts, such as kitchen and household materials that remained where they were stored or used when disaster struck. Such finds are valuable because they provide clues about rooms, occupations and routines rather than existing as isolated museum pieces.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://jamaicaportroyal.com/archaeology.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: jamaicaportroyal.com">[jamaicaportroyal.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">jamaicaportroyal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Archaeological Research in Port Royal</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Archaeological Research in Port Royal</span></span></span>

Recovered artefacts have also supported specialist research. Studies based on Port Royal collections have examined materials including glass bottles, silver and pewter objects, tobacco pipes and tools, showing how the site has contributed to wider understanding of 17th-century Caribbean commerce and craftsmanship.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nautarch.tamu.edu/research/conservation-research-laboratory-crl/crl-publications/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nautarch.tamu.edu">[Nautical Archaeology Program – NAP]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nautarch.tamu.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Nautical Archaeology Program – NAPCRL Publications</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Nautical Archaeology Program – NAP...</span></span></span>

## Pirate myths versus colonial realities

### The city behind the legend

Port Royal’s reputation as a pirate stronghold is a major reason it attracts public fascination. Stories of privateers, treasure and sudden destruction have made it one of the Caribbean’s best-known historic locations. However, the archaeological record presents a broader picture: pirates were only one part of a much larger urban system.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1595/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: whc.unesco.org">[UNESCO World Heritage Centre]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">whc.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">World Heritage Centre The Archaeological Ensemble of 17th Century Port Royal</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UNESCO World Heritage CentreThe Archaeological Ensemble of 17th Century Port Royal - UNESCO World Heritage Centre...</span></span></span>

      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
