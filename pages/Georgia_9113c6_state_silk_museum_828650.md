## Introduction

The State Silk Museum in Tbilisi is one of [Georgia]({{ 'where-georgia-keeps-its-strangest/' | relative_url }})'s most unusual scientific collections because it treats silk as a complete biological and industrial ecosystem rather than as a luxury textile. At its heart is a remarkable cabinet of cocoons: thousands of preserved silk cocoons collected over more than a century, displayed alongside moths, silkworm specimens, dyes, laboratory equipment, machinery and archival material. Together they show how nineteenth-century scientists studied every stage of silk production, from breeding insects and cultivating mulberry trees to spinning thread and dyeing finished fabrics. Rather than a cabinet of curiosities built around oddities for their own sake, it is a cabinet of scientific observation whose sheer scale and diversity now make it feel wonderfully eccentric to modern visitors. The museum remains one of the world's oldest institutions devoted entirely to sericulture—the cultivation of silkworms—and its restored galleries allow visitors to experience this unusual research collection much as earlier generations of scientists and students did.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://silkmuseum.ge/en/about/museum" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: silkmuseum.ge">[აბრეშუმის სახელმწიფო მუზეუმი]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">silkmuseum.ge</span><span class="citation-popover-title">აბრეშუმის სახელმწიფო მუზეუმიmuseum history &#124; state silk museum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">აბრეშუმის სახელმწიფო მუზეუმიmuseum history &#124; state silk museum</span></span></span>

## How the sericulture station created the collection

The collection began in 1887 with the establishment of the Caucasian Sericulture Station, a regional research and education centre founded by biologist Nikolay Shavrov. Its purpose was practical rather than decorative. Silk production was an important agricultural industry across the Caucasus, and improving it required systematic study of silkworm health, breeding methods, disease prevention and silk quality. The station therefore combined laboratories, a library, teaching facilities and a museum under one roof, following contemporary European scientific models.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://silkmuseum.ge/en/about/museum" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: silkmuseum.ge">[აბრეშუმის სახელმწიფო მუზეუმი]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">silkmuseum.ge</span><span class="citation-popover-title">აბრეშუმის სახელმწიფო მუზეუმიmuseum history &#124; state silk museum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">აბრეშუმის სახელმწიფო მუზეუმიmuseum history &#124; state silk museum</span></span></span>

Instead of displaying rare objects simply to impress visitors, the museum accumulated working reference collections. Researchers assembled cocoons from different species and breeding lines, preserved every stage of the silkworm life cycle, gathered examples of mulberry cultivation, documented dye materials and collected industrial equipment used in silk manufacture. Many specimens were acquired through scientific exchanges and fieldwork across Europe, Asia and the Caucasus, allowing researchers to compare regional varieties directly.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://silkmuseum.ge/en/collection/4/silk-cocoons" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: silkmuseum.ge">[silkmuseum.ge]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">silkmuseum.ge</span><span class="citation-popover-title">აბრეშუმის სახელმწიფო მუზეუმიSilk cocoons &#124; collections &#124; state silk museum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">აბრეშუმის სახელმწიფო მუზეუმიSilk cocoons &#124; collections &#124; state silk museum</span></span></span>

This scientific purpose explains why the museum feels different from many textile museums. The displays are organised around processes and experimentation rather than fashion or luxury. Visitors encounter shelves that resemble a nineteenth-century natural history cabinet, where biological specimens, production tools and teaching materials sit side by side because they originally served the same educational mission.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://silkmuseum.ge/en/exhibition/50/museum-permanent-display-and-the-library" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: silkmuseum.ge">[აბრეშუმის სახელმწიფო მუზეუმი]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">silkmuseum.ge</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">October 9, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 9, 2024</span></span></span>

## Cocoons, moths, dyes and production machinery

The cocoon collection is the museum's defining feature. It contains around 5,000 species and varieties of silk cocoons dating from the nineteenth to the twenty-first centuries, representing specimens collected across the Caucasus, Europe and Asia. Differences that might seem insignificant at first glance—size, colour, shape and thread length—were scientifically important because they affected silk quality, productivity and breeding programmes. Today these variations create a visually striking display that resembles a natural history cabinet devoted entirely to silk.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://silkmuseum.ge/en/collection/4/silk-cocoons" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: silkmuseum.ge">[აბრეშუმის სახელმწიფო მუზეუმი]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">silkmuseum.ge</span><span class="citation-popover-title">აბრეშუმის სახელმწიფო მუზეუმიSilk cocoons &#124; collections &#124; state silk museum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">აბრეშუმის სახელმწიფო მუზეუმიSilk cocoons &#124; collections &#124; state silk museum</span></span></span>

The cocoons are only one element of a much larger scientific system. Permanent displays include:

* Preserved silkworm anatomy and complete life-cycle specimens.
* Adult silk moths used for breeding and identification.
* Natural and synthetic dyes illustrating changes in textile chemistry.
* Historic silk threads and skeins.

Seen together, these objects explain that silk production depended on far more than spinning thread. Successful sericulture required careful management of insects, plants, chemistry, engineering and transport. The museum therefore functions as a scientific map of an entire production network rather than a display of finished luxury goods.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://silkmuseum.ge/en/exhibition/50/museum-permanent-display-and-the-library" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: silkmuseum.ge">[აბრეშუმის სახელმწიფო მუზეუმი]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">silkmuseum.ge</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">October 9, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 9, 2024</span></span></span>

One particularly distinctive aspect is that many original display cabinets also survive. Designed by architect Aleksander Szymkiewicz for the museum's opening in the 1890s, the vitrines were purpose-built for scientific teaching and remain part of the exhibition today, making the presentation itself an important historical artefact.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://silkmuseum.ge/en/about/building" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: silkmuseum.ge">[აბრეშუმის სახელმწიფო მუზეუმი]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">silkmuseum.ge</span><span class="citation-popover-title">აბრეშუმის სახელმწიფო მუზეუმიbuilding &#124; state silk museum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">აბრეშუმის სახელმწიფო მუზეუმიbuilding &#124; state silk museum</span></span></span>

## What the restored museum offers visitors

Following a major restoration completed in 2024, visitors once again experience the museum as both a historic scientific institution and a contemporary exhibition space. Restoration preserved the nineteenth-century building while introducing updated displays that combine original collections with new interpretation and temporary exhibitions exploring the history of sericulture, scientific research and the wider Silk Road.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://silkmuseum.ge/en/news/9/opening-program" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: silkmuseum.ge">[აბრეშუმის სახელმწიფო მუზეუმი]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">silkmuseum.ge</span><span class="citation-popover-title">აბრეშუმის სახელმწიფო მუზეუმიOpening program &#124; menu.news &#124; state silk museum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">აბრეშუმის სახელმწიფო მუზეუმიOpening program &#124; menu.news &#124; state silk museum</span></span></span>

The permanent exhibition deliberately preserves the museum's identity as a research collection rather than transforming it into a conventional design museum. Biological specimens, laboratory tools, ethnographic material and industrial equipment remain central, while the adjoining historic library—holding around 20,000 books and periodicals in numerous languages—reinforces the sense that visitors are entering a former scientific campus rather than simply an exhibition gallery.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://silkmuseum.ge/en/exhibition/50/museum-permanent-display-and-the-library" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: silkmuseum.ge">[აბრეშუმის სახელმწიფო მუზეუმი]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">silkmuseum.ge</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">October 9, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 9, 2024</span></span></span>

For travellers interested in unusual museums, the appeal lies in this authenticity. The museum does not exaggerate mystery or spectacle. Instead, its fascination comes from seeing thousands of carefully catalogued cocoons, preserved insects and working scientific collections that reveal how a seemingly ordinary textile depended on meticulous biological research and international exchange.

## Why the cabinet matters in Georgia's curiosity landscape

Georgia has few museums devoted to the bizarre in the conventional sense. The State Silk Museum stands out because it transforms an agricultural industry into something that feels unexpectedly strange. Rows of subtly different cocoons, preserved moths, Victorian laboratory apparatus and century-old teaching displays create an atmosphere closer to a nineteenth-century cabinet of natural curiosities than a fashion museum.

Unlike collections built around folklore, paranormal claims or sensational objects, the Silk Museum's cabinet is unusual precisely because every specimen was assembled for practical scientific reasons. That original purpose has survived long after the regional silk industry declined, allowing today's visitors to encounter an intact world where biology, industry and education were brought together in extraordinary detail. Within Georgia's landscape of unusual collections, it is one of the clearest examples of how rigorous science can become as captivating as any cabinet of curiosities.

    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
