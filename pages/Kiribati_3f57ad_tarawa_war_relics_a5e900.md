## Introduction

Tarawa’s battlefield relics form one of the Pacific’s most unusual historical collections: not a conventional museum behind glass, but a landscape where the remains of war still sit among modern homes, roads and beaches. On Betio, the main battle site on Tarawa Atoll in [Kiribati]({{ 'where-kiribatis-strange-collections-tell/' | relative_url }}), visitors can encounter concrete bunkers, coastal guns, rusting tanks, wrecked amphibious vehicles and memorials connected to the 1943 Battle of Tarawa. These objects are unusual because their “display case” is the battlefield itself.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.kiribatitourism.gov.ki/kiribati-experiences/world-war-ii/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: kiribatitourism.gov.ki">[Tourism Authority of Kiribati (TAK)]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">kiribatitourism.gov.ki</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on kiribatitourism.gov.ki.</span></span></span>

The relics reveal more than a single military clash. They show how a small coral island became a crucial Pacific War position, how Japanese forces transformed the landscape into a defensive fortress, and how the devastating American assault changed later amphibious warfare. Unlike a traditional war museum, Tarawa’s collection remains incomplete, weathered and sometimes hidden, with some evidence still emerging from the ground decades later.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://wm.awm.gov.au/read/tarawa" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: wm.awm.gov.au">[wm.awm.gov.au]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">wm.awm.gov.au</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Visiting battle grounds: Tarawa &#124; WM</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Visiting battle grounds: Tarawa &#124; WM</span></span></span>

## The Battlefield Museum Hidden Across Tarawa

### The Battle of Tarawa and surviving remains

The relics scattered across Betio come from the Battle of Tarawa, fought from 20 to 23 November 1943 during the US campaign to capture Japanese-held islands in the central Pacific. The battle was part of Operation Galvanic and became one of the most heavily studied amphibious assaults of the Second World War because of the intensity of the fighting and the lessons it produced for later island campaigns.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://wm.awm.gov.au/read/tarawa" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: wm.awm.gov.au">[wm.awm.gov.au]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">wm.awm.gov.au</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Visiting battle grounds: Tarawa &#124; WM</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Visiting battle grounds: Tarawa &#124; WM</span></span></span>

Before the assault, Japanese forces had turned Betio into a heavily fortified position. They constructed concrete bunkers, pillboxes, gun emplacements, trenches and coastal defences designed to withstand naval bombardment. The surviving structures allow visitors to understand the physical reality behind historical descriptions: the island was not simply a beach landing site, but a compact defensive network built into a narrow strip of coral land.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://wm.awm.gov.au/read/tarawa" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: wm.awm.gov.au">[wm.awm.gov.au]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">wm.awm.gov.au</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Visiting battle grounds: Tarawa &#124; WM</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Visiting battle grounds: Tarawa &#124; WM</span></span></span>

The battlefield’s importance comes partly from its scale. Betio was a tiny location, yet thousands of troops fought over it in a matter of days. More than 1,000 US Marines and sailors were killed, while the Japanese garrison was almost entirely destroyed. The remains left behind therefore function as a concentrated record of a much larger Pacific conflict.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://wm.awm.gov.au/read/tarawa" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: wm.awm.gov.au">[wm.awm.gov.au]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">wm.awm.gov.au</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Visiting battle grounds: Tarawa &#124; WM</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Visiting battle grounds: Tarawa &#124; WM</span></span></span>

## Bunkers, guns, wreckage and memorial sites

Tarawa’s most recognisable relics are the large defensive structures and weapons that remain visible around Betio. Heavy coastal guns, including the famous 8-inch guns installed by the Japanese, still stand as reminders of the island’s role as a fortified naval position. Concrete command posts and bunkers demonstrate how Japanese engineers adapted the island’s limited space into overlapping defensive zones.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://wm.awm.gov.au/read/tarawa" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: wm.awm.gov.au">[wm.awm.gov.au]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">wm.awm.gov.au</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Visiting battle grounds: Tarawa &#124; WM</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Visiting battle grounds: Tarawa &#124; WM</span></span></span>

Other remains are scattered between land and sea. At low tide, visitors may see the remains of amphibious vehicles, tanks and other wartime equipment that were abandoned during the assault. Rusting machinery on the shoreline creates a rare form of battlefield archaeology, where the boundary between land, ocean and historical artefact is constantly changing.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.kiribatitourism.gov.ki/kiribati-experiences/world-war-ii/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: kiribatitourism.gov.ki">[Tourism Authority of Kiribati (TAK)]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">kiribatitourism.gov.ki</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on kiribatitourism.gov.ki.</span></span></span>

The relics also include memorial landscapes. The Battle of Tarawa Memorial honours those killed during the fighting, while Japanese and other memorial sites reflect the different communities connected to the battle. The island therefore preserves competing memories of the conflict: military achievement, national sacrifice, civilian disruption and the long aftermath of war.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.marforpac.marines.mil/Media-Room/Pacific-Marines-Stories/Article/Article/530097/marines-remember-tarawa-heroes/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: marforpac.marines.mil">[marforpac.marines.mil]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">marforpac.marines.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Marines remember Tarawa heroes &gt; U.S. Marine Corps Forces, Pacific &gt; Article Population Page...</span></span></span>

One of the most striking aspects of Tarawa is that these remains are not always separated from ordinary life. A bunker may stand near modern buildings, and historic military structures can appear beside everyday community spaces. This creates a very different experience from visiting a purpose-built battlefield park: the past remains physically present within a living island environment.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://wm.awm.gov.au/read/tarawa" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: wm.awm.gov.au">[wm.awm.gov.au]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">wm.awm.gov.au</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Visiting battle grounds: Tarawa &#124; WM</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Visiting battle grounds: Tarawa &#124; WM</span></span></span>

## How landscapes become historical collections

Tarawa’s relics show how a place itself can become a collection. Museums usually select, preserve and label objects, but battlefield landscapes preserve relationships between objects: a gun emplacement facing the sea, a bunker protecting a beach approach, or a damaged vehicle marking where an assault failed. The meaning comes from location as much as from the object itself.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ibiblio.org/hyperwar/USMC/USMC-C-Tarawa/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ibiblio.org">[Ibiblio]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ibiblio.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Across the Reef: The Marine Assault of Tarawa</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Across the Reef: The Marine Assault of Tarawa...</span></span></span>

This makes Tarawa unusual within Kiribati’s heritage landscape. The country’s cultural collections often focus on living traditions, craftsmanship and community knowledge, while the battlefield remains represent a different kind of memory: a physical archive of international conflict preserved unintentionally through geography and climate. The relics are not curiosities because they are strange objects, but because they transform an entire island into a historical exhibit.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.kiribatitourism.gov.ki/kiribati-experiences/world-war-ii/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: kiribatitourism.gov.ki">[Tourism Authority of Kiribati (TAK)]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">kiribatitourism.gov.ki</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on kiribatitourism.gov.ki.</span></span></span>

The landscape also raises difficult questions about preservation. Tropical weather, coastal erosion and development gradually affect wartime remains. At the same time, the continuing discovery of human remains shows that Tarawa is not only an archaeological site but also a place connected to unresolved personal histories. The US Defence POW/MIA Accounting Agency’s Tarawa Project has used archaeological investigation, historical research and identification techniques to locate and identify missing servicemen from the battle.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://dpaa-mil.sites.crmforce.mil/Projects_WWII_Tarawa_InfoSheet" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dpaa-mil.sites.crmforce.mil">[dpaa-mil.sites.crmforce.mil]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dpaa-mil.sites.crmforce.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">DPA A Tarawa Info Sheet</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">DPA A Tarawa Info Sheet</span></span></span>

For visitors, the significance of Tarawa’s relics lies in their honesty. They are not polished exhibits designed to recreate history; they are fragments of a real battlefield still embedded in a modern community.         .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
