## Introduction

Ngundeng Bong's ceremonial rod is one of the most symbolically charged objects associated with [South Sudan]({{ 'where-are-south-sudans-strangest-objects/' | relative_url }}). It is neither an ordinary museum piece nor simply an archaeological artefact. For many Nuer people it is a sacred relic linked to one of the region's most influential prophets; for historians it is a colonial trophy removed by British forces; and for modern politicians it has become an object whose possession carries powerful symbolic weight. Unlike many returned cultural treasures that end up in public museums, the rod's current location and public accessibility remain uncertain, making it an unusual case where a nationally significant object continues to exist at the intersection of religion, heritage and contemporary politics.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nyamile.com/south-sudan-riek-machar-and-the-prophets-rod/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nyamile.com">[nyamile.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nyamile.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">South Sudan: Riek Machar and the prophet’s rod &#124; Nyamilepedia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">March 12, 2014...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 12, 2014</span></span></span>

Within South Sudan's landscape of unusual collections and contested heritage, the rod stands out precisely because it is not a stable museum exhibit. Its story raises questions about who should control sacred objects, whether deeply religious artefacts belong in display cases, and how colonial-era trophies can acquire entirely new political meanings after repatriation.

## Who Controls the Prophet's Rod?

### Ngundeng Bong and the rod's sacred meaning

Ngundeng Bong (c.1830–1906) was a Nuer prophet, religious leader and peace advocate whose influence extended well beyond his own community. His songs, prophecies and religious authority remain important to many followers today. Among the objects most closely associated with him was his ceremonial rod, known as the *dang*, a staff fashioned from tamarind root and wrapped with copper wire. According to tradition it was used during ceremonies, blessings and public acts of authority rather than as an ordinary walking stick.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nyamile.com/south-sudan-riek-machar-and-the-prophets-rod/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nyamile.com">[nyamile.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nyamile.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">South Sudan: Riek Machar and the prophet’s rod &#124; Nyamilepedia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">March 12, 2014...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 12, 2014</span></span></span>

Belief surrounding the rod goes far beyond its physical form. Many Nuer traditions regard it as carrying the prophet's spiritual authority, making it comparable to a sacred regalia rather than an historical curiosity. Stories describe the rod as playing a role in battles, miracles and prophecies, although these accounts belong to religious tradition rather than verifiable historical record. Because of this sacred status, discussions about its ownership have always involved theology and custom as much as heritage law.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://apnews.com/article/e2300925846a694731e7627bb7a2f04e" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: apnews.com">[AP News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">apnews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The stick, believed by many to possess mystical power, is associated with a prophecy foretelling the rise of a messianic Nuer leader—trai...</span></span></span>

For visitors interested in unusual cultural heritage, this distinction is important. The rod is significant not because it is visually spectacular but because belief invests it with extraordinary meaning. Its cultural power comes from what communities think it represents rather than from its craftsmanship alone.

## From colonial trophy to celebrated return

The rod left South Sudan after British colonial forces attacked Ngundeng's religious centre at Wec Deang following resistance associated with the prophet's successors. Colonial officers removed the staff as a military trophy during campaigns in the late 1920s, treating it as evidence of imperial victory rather than as a sacred object.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nyamile.com/south-sudan-riek-machar-and-the-prophets-rod/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nyamile.com">[nyamile.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nyamile.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">South Sudan: Riek Machar and the prophet’s rod &#124; Nyamilepedia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">March 12, 2014...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 12, 2014</span></span></span>

For decades the object remained in Britain in private hands rather than becoming a well-known museum exhibit. Historian Douglas H. Johnson later traced it, purchased it from the descendants of a former colonial official and worked to arrange its return. In May 2009 he personally carried the rod back to Southern Sudan, then still an autonomous region before independence. The arrival prompted large public celebrations in Juba, with thousands gathering to witness what many regarded as the homecoming of a spiritually important national treasure.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://sudantribune.com/article/31058" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: sudantribune.com">[Sudan Tribune]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">sudantribune.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Sudan Tribune Nuer prophet’s rod finally arrives in Juba</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Sudan TribuneNuer prophet’s rod finally arrives in Juba - Sudan TribuneMay 17, 2009...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 17, 2009</span></span></span>

The return was celebrated not only as restitution of a cultural object but also as a symbolic reversal of colonial dispossession. President Salva Kiir described the rod's return as a symbol of resistance against oppression, illustrating how an object originally taken as an imperial trophy had become a national emblem.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://sudantribune.com/article/31096" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: sudantribune.com">[Sudan Tribune]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">sudantribune.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Sudan Tribune Kiir says Ngundeng&#x27;s dang a symbol of resistance</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Sudan TribuneKiir says Ngundeng&#x27;s dang a symbol of resistance - Sudan TribuneMay 21, 2009...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 21, 2009</span></span></span>

Yet the celebrations also exposed disagreements. Members of Ngundeng's extended family and different community representatives disputed who should formally receive and safeguard the relic, revealing that repatriation did not automatically settle questions of legitimate ownership.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://sudantribune.com/article/31080" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: sudantribune.com">[Sudan Tribune]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">sudantribune.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Sudan Tribune Vice President of S. ## Why display, ownership and access remain disputed

Most returned heritage objects eventually enter museums or national collections with documented conservation and public access. Ngundeng's rod followed a different path.

Instead of becoming part of an established museum, the rod came into the possession of South Sudan's then Vice-President Riek Machar, himself a prominent Nuer politician. Historians note that Machar presented the rod to visiting community leaders as a significant cultural and religious object, while critics argued that its custody blurred the boundary between sacred authority and political symbolism.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://apnews.com/article/e2300925846a694731e7627bb7a2f04e" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: apnews.com">[AP News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">apnews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The stick, believed by many to possess mystical power, is associated with a prophecy foretelling the rise of a messianic Nuer leader—trai...</span></span></span>

The controversy is amplified by Ngundeng's enduring prophecies. Some followers interpret prophetic traditions as foretelling the rise of a future Nuer leader with characteristics they associate with Machar, while many others reject such political readings or caution against treating religious traditions as literal predictions. Scholars emphasise that the prophecies have multiple interpretations and should not be reduced to modern electoral politics.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://apnews.com/article/e2300925846a694731e7627bb7a2f04e" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: apnews.com">[AP News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">apnews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The stick, believed by many to possess mystical power, is associated with a prophecy foretelling the rise of a messianic Nuer leader—trai...</span></span></span>

This has transformed the rod from a returned heritage object into an active political symbol. Rather than representing only the past, it continues to influence perceptions of legitimacy, leadership and spiritual authority within South Sudan's contemporary political landscape.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://apnews.com/article/e2300925846a694731e7627bb7a2f04e" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: apnews.com">[AP News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">apnews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The stick, believed by many to possess mystical power, is associated with a prophecy foretelling the rise of a messianic Nuer leader—trai...</span></span></span>

## Why it remains an unusual heritage object

For anyone exploring South Sudan's unusual collections and heritage, the rod is remarkable because it resists conventional museum categories.

It is simultaneously:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* a sacred religious relic for many believers;
* a colonial trophy whose removal reflects imperial collecting practices;
* a repatriated cultural object celebrated as a victory for historical justice;
* a politically sensitive emblem connected with competing claims to authority; and
* an artefact that is not reliably available for public viewing despite its national importance.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://sudantribune.com/article/31653" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: sudantribune.com">[sudantribune.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">sudantribune.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Sudan Tribune South Sudan celebrates return of Ngundeng’s daang</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Sudan TribuneSouth Sudan celebrates return of Ngundeng’s daang - Sudan Tribune...</span></span></span>

</div>

Unlike many famous returned artefacts that become permanent museum centrepieces, the rod occupies an uncertain space between private custodianship, religious tradition and state symbolism. That ambiguity is precisely what makes it one of South Sudan's most distinctive heritage stories.

## Can visitors see the rod today?

Reliable public information about the rod's present location is limited. There is no evidence that it forms part of a permanent public exhibition or that visitors can routinely view it in a museum. Reports over the years have indicated that it remained under the custody of Riek Machar, but recent accounts acknowledge that its precise location and accessibility are unclear, particularly amid South Sudan's continuing political instability.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://apnews.com/article/e2300925846a694731e7627bb7a2f04e" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: apnews.com">[AP News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">apnews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The stick, believed by many to possess mystical power, is associated with a prophecy foretelling the rise of a messianic Nuer leader—trai...</span></span></span>

For travellers, this means the prophet's rod is best understood as a nationally important but effectively inaccessible relic. Its significance lies less in its visibility than in the extraordinary debates surrounding who should possess it, how a sacred object should be cared for, and whether some artefacts are too spiritually and politically charged ever to become ordinary museum exhibits.

        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
