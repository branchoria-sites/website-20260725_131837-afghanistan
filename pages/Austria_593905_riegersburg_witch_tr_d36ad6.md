## Introduction

Riegersburg Castle in Styria is home to [Austria]({{ 'why-does-austria-preserve-such-strange/' | relative_url }})'s best-known permanent exhibition on witchcraft accusations and persecution, but its purpose is often misunderstood. Visitors expecting a museum that argues witches possessed supernatural powers will find the opposite. The exhibition uses court records, historical context and multimedia displays to explain how fear, rumour, religious belief, political authority and flawed legal processes combined to create the idea of the witch as a criminal. Rather than celebrating occult traditions, it examines one of the darkest chapters in Austrian history and asks how ordinary people became victims of extraordinary accusations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.dieriegersburg.at/en/museen/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dieriegersburg.at">[Burg Riegersburg]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dieriegersburg.at</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Burg Riegersburg Museen</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Burg RiegersburgMuseen - Burg Riegersburg...</span></span></span>

## How the Riegersburg exhibition began

The Witch Museum traces its origins to the hugely successful Styrian Provincial Exhibition **"Witches and Sorcerers"**, held at Riegersburg in 1987. Around 360,000 people visited the temporary exhibition, making it one of the most successful provincial exhibitions ever organised in Styria. Rather than dismantling everything afterwards, part of the exhibition remained and became the permanent Witch Museum in 1988.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.dieriegersburg.at/en/museen/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dieriegersburg.at">[Burg Riegersburg]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dieriegersburg.at</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Burg Riegersburg Museen</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Burg RiegersburgMuseen - Burg Riegersburg...</span></span></span>

A major redesign in 2017 shifted the museum even further towards contemporary historical interpretation. Today's exhibition combines documentary material with video, sound installations and interpretive displays designed to explain the mentality of the seventeenth century while encouraging visitors to recognise how exclusion, scapegoating and moral panic can still appear in modern societies. The official interpretation explicitly links historical persecution with broader themes of discrimination rather than presenting witchcraft as a supernatural reality.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.dieriegersburg.at/en/museen/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dieriegersburg.at">[Burg Riegersburg]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dieriegersburg.at</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Burg Riegersburg Museen</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Burg RiegersburgMuseen - Burg Riegersburg...</span></span></span>

This approach distinguishes Riegersburg from attractions that market themselves through haunted legends or paranormal experiences. The exhibition is educational rather than sensational, using the language of social history instead of ghost stories.

## What early modern witch trials did to the accused

The exhibition concentrates on documented legal persecution in Styria during the seventeenth and early eighteenth centuries. According to the museum, around 300 people accused of witchcraft or sorcery were prosecuted in the region during this period. Those targeted were not exclusively social outsiders. People of varying backgrounds could find themselves accused through neighbourly disputes, local rivalries, rumours, personal misfortune or denunciations made under pressure.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.dieriegersburg.at/en/museen/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dieriegersburg.at">[Burg Riegersburg]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dieriegersburg.at</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Burg Riegersburg Museen</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Burg RiegersburgMuseen - Burg Riegersburg...</span></span></span>

Rather than asking whether magic worked, the museum asks why communities believed accusations that now seem implausible. Visitors encounter evidence showing how judicial procedures accepted confessions extracted under coercion, how rumours accumulated into criminal cases, and how religious and political authorities reinforced fears instead of questioning them. The result was a legal system capable of producing executions despite the absence of reliable evidence for supernatural crimes.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.dieriegersburg.at/en/museen/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dieriegersburg.at">[Burg Riegersburg]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dieriegersburg.at</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Burg Riegersburg Museen</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Burg RiegersburgMuseen - Burg Riegersburg...</span></span></span>

One reason the exhibition remains effective is that it treats the accused as historical individuals instead of mythical figures. Their experiences illustrate how institutions can transform suspicion into official guilt when critical standards of evidence collapse.

## The case of Katharina Paldauf

The exhibition's best-known individual story is that of **Katharina Paldauf**, wife of the Riegersburg castle administrator. She became associated with the nickname "Flower Witch" because unusually successful flowers growing around the castle were interpreted by some contemporaries as evidence of supernatural assistance rather than gardening skill or favourable conditions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.dieriegersburg.at/en/museen/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dieriegersburg.at">[Burg Riegersburg]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dieriegersburg.at</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Burg Riegersburg Museen</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Burg RiegersburgMuseen - Burg Riegersburg...</span></span></span>

Her inclusion demonstrates an important point made throughout the museum: accusations often emerged from everyday observations that were given extraordinary explanations. Prosperity, illness, failed harvests, personal conflicts or unexpected events could all become "evidence" once communities accepted the assumption that witches existed.

The exhibition therefore treats Paldauf not as proof of magic but as evidence of how ordinary circumstances could be reinterpreted through fear and suspicion. Her story provides a concrete example of the wider mechanisms behind witchcraft prosecutions in Styria.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.dieriegersburg.at/en/museen/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dieriegersburg.at">[Burg Riegersburg]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dieriegersburg.at</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Burg Riegersburg Museen</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Burg RiegersburgMuseen - Burg Riegersburg...</span></span></span>

## Where documented persecution ends and folklore begins

Riegersburg deliberately separates documented history from later folklore. The museum presents surviving trial records, regional history and contemporary scholarship as evidence for what actually happened, while recognising that legends about witches developed afterwards and continue to influence popular culture.

This distinction matters because the historical record demonstrates the existence of witch trials, imprisonment and executions, but it does not demonstrate the existence of supernatural witchcraft itself. Modern historians overwhelmingly explain European witch persecutions through interacting social, religious, political and legal factors rather than by accepting accusations as factual descriptions of magical activity.

The museum's interpretation reflects that scholarly consensus. Visitors are encouraged to understand why people believed in witches without being asked to accept those beliefs as true. In this respect, the exhibition functions less as a museum of magic than as a museum about belief, justice and collective fear.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.dieriegersburg.at/en/museen/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dieriegersburg.at">[Burg Riegersburg]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dieriegersburg.at</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Burg Riegersburg Museen</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Burg RiegersburgMuseen - Burg Riegersburg...</span></span></span>

## What visitors should expect today

The Witch Museum forms one part of the larger Riegersburg Castle museum complex alongside exhibitions on the castle's history and its historic weapons collection. Audio guides and guided tours place the witch exhibition within the broader history of seventeenth-century Styria, connecting witch persecutions with warfare, social upheaval and everyday life rather than isolating them as mysterious episodes.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.dieriegersburg.at/en/museen/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dieriegersburg.at">[Burg Riegersburg]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dieriegersburg.at</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Burg Riegersburg Museen</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Burg RiegersburgMuseen - Burg Riegersburg...</span></span></span>

Modern multimedia installations avoid creating a horror attraction. Instead, visitors encounter:

Among Austria's unusual museums, Riegersburg occupies a distinctive position because it transforms a subject often surrounded by myth into an evidence-based examination of historical persecution. They leave having learned more about legal systems, prejudice, social panic and the consequences of treating suspicion as proof.

That makes the Witch Museum significant beyond its regional history.     return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
</script>
