## Introduction

The Museum of Tabanca is one of [Cape Verde]({{ 'where-cape-verde-keeps-its-strangest/' | relative_url }})'s most distinctive heritage collections because it attempts to preserve something that cannot simply be placed in a display case. Its musical instruments, ceremonial staffs, banners, crowns, drums and ritual objects only acquire their full meaning when used by living communities during annual processions, religious observances and communal celebrations. Rather than presenting relics from a vanished culture, the museum documents an active tradition that continues to be practised, especially on the islands of Santiago and Maio, where local associations maintain ceremonies centred on patron saints, music and collective identity. This makes the museum unusual within Cape Verde's landscape of curious collections: it is as much a meeting place between communities and visitors as it is a repository of historic objects.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ipc.cv/en/museus/museu-da-tabanca/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ipc.cv">[Instituto do Patrimnio Cultural]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ipc.cv</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on ipc.cv.</span></span></span>

## What Tabanca is and where it is practised

Tabanca is a community organisation and ceremonial tradition that blends Catholic devotion with African-derived musical practices, local social organisation and mutual support. Each Tabanca group has its own leadership, ceremonial hierarchy and patron saint, with festivals combining religious observance, processions, feasting and highly distinctive musical performances. Although forms of the tradition have existed elsewhere, it survives most strongly today on Santiago and Maio, where annual celebrations remain important parts of local cultural life.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ipc.cv/en/patromonio-imaterial/tabanca/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ipc.cv">[Instituto do Patrimnio Cultural]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ipc.cv</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Instituto do Patrimnio Cultural Tabanca Instituto do Patrimnio Cultural</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Instituto do Patrimnio Cultural Tabanca Instituto do Patrimnio Cultural</span></span></span>

The celebrations are not staged as historical re-enactments for tourists. Preparations begin weeks in advance as communities raise funds, repair costumes and instruments, organise shared meals and prepare ceremonial spaces. Rituals include prayers, processions, flag ceremonies, drum performances and communal gatherings that reinforce neighbourhood ties as much as religious devotion. In 2019 the Cape Verdean state formally recognised the Tabanca manifestation as National Intangible Cultural Heritage after extensive community-based documentation, acknowledging that its significance lies in living practice rather than in artefacts alone.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ipc.cv/en/patromonio-imaterial/tabanca/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ipc.cv">[Instituto do Patrimnio Cultural]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ipc.cv</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Instituto do Patrimnio Cultural Tabanca Instituto do Patrimnio Cultural</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Instituto do Patrimnio Cultural Tabanca Instituto do Patrimnio Cultural</span></span></span>

This distinction matters when considering the Museum of Tabanca. Unlike a conventional ethnographic museum displaying objects from a discontinued culture, it documents traditions whose custodians are still active participants rather than historical subjects.

## How instruments and ceremonial objects function

The museum's permanent exhibition includes musical instruments, ceremonial costumes, banners, crosses, crowns, staffs, ritual objects, items associated with communal meals and objects connected with batuque performance. Individually they may appear simple, but each belongs to a carefully ordered sequence of ceremonies.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ipc.cv/en/museus/museu-da-tabanca/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ipc.cv">[Instituto do Patrimnio Cultural]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ipc.cv</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on ipc.cv.</span></span></span>

Several categories of objects illustrate how the tradition operates:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--checklist" markdown="1">

* **Drums** provide the rhythmic foundation for ceremonies, accompanying prayers, dances and processions rather than serving merely as entertainment.
* **Conch-shell and horn instruments** announce gatherings and processional moments, functioning as audible signals that draw communities together.
* **Flags, staffs and ceremonial regalia** identify individual Tabanca groups and represent authority within the ritual hierarchy.
* **Crosses and devotional objects** demonstrate the close relationship between Catholic observance and local ceremonial practice.
* **Costumes and crowns** distinguish ceremonial roles such as kings, queens and court officials during festivals.
* **Objects connected with communal meals** remind visitors that hospitality and shared food are central parts of the celebration rather than incidental social events.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ipc.cv/en/museus/museu-da-tabanca/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ipc.cv">[Instituto do Patrimnio Cultural]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ipc.cv</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on ipc.cv.</span></span></span>

These artefacts should therefore be understood as working equipment rather than decorative folklore. Their value comes from repeated use during annual ceremonies, where sound, movement and collective participation transform them from museum pieces into active components of ritual life.

One striking example is the sequence of ceremonies known as the *toque de salva*, in which ritual drumming, the presentation of the flag, ceremonial dances, prayers and processional acts occur in a prescribed order. The museum preserves many of the physical objects involved, but their full meaning becomes clear only when viewed as part of this wider ceremonial sequence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ipc.cv/en/patromonio-imaterial/tabanca/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ipc.cv">[Instituto do Patrimnio Cultural]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ipc.cv</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Instituto do Patrimnio Cultural Tabanca Instituto do Patrimnio Cultural</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Instituto do Patrimnio Cultural Tabanca Instituto do Patrimnio Cultural</span></span></span>

## Can a museum preserve a living ritual?

The Museum of Tabanca was originally established in 2000 during efforts to rescue a tradition then considered vulnerable to decline. In 2010 it moved from Assomada to Ch de Tanque, a community closely associated with active Tabanca practice. The relocation reflected an important curatorial choice: instead of isolating the collection within an urban cultural institution, it placed the museum inside a community where the tradition continues to be performed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ipc.cv/en/museus/museu-da-tabanca/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ipc.cv">[Instituto do Patrimnio Cultural]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ipc.cv</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on ipc.cv.</span></span></span>

This community-centred approach shapes the museum's purpose. According to the Cultural Heritage Institute, the collection was assembled through fieldwork undertaken in several phases during the 1990s and 2000s across different communities on Santiago. Rather than collecting spectacular curiosities, researchers documented everyday ceremonial equipment that might otherwise have disappeared as groups modernised or older members passed away.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ipc.cv/en/museus/museu-da-tabanca/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ipc.cv">[Instituto do Patrimnio Cultural]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ipc.cv</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on ipc.cv.</span></span></span>

The museum also positions itself as an educational centre. School groups, community organisations and Tabanca associations are intended audiences alongside tourists, emphasising that preservation depends upon transmitting knowledge as well as conserving objects. This reflects a broader understanding of intangible heritage, where songs, performance techniques, ceremonial roles and community memory are inseparable from the physical artefacts that support them.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ipc.cv/en/museus/museu-da-tabanca/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ipc.cv">[Instituto do Patrimnio Cultural]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ipc.cv</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on ipc.cv.</span></span></span>

## Preservation, heritage status and community control

The Museum of Tabanca illustrates an important principle in heritage preservation: community traditions cannot simply be frozen behind glass. Instruments wear out, costumes require repair, and ceremonial objects continue to circulate through annual celebrations. Preservation therefore means balancing documentation with continued use.

Cape Verde's recognition of Tabanca as National Intangible Cultural Heritage strengthened this approach by acknowledging that safeguarding depends upon practitioners themselves. Official inventories and museum collections record material culture, but local associations remain responsible for organising festivals, maintaining ceremonial hierarchies, transmitting musical knowledge and preserving ritual practice.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ipc.cv/en/patromonio-imaterial/tabanca/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ipc.cv">[Instituto do Patrimnio Cultural]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ipc.cv</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Instituto do Patrimnio Cultural Tabanca Instituto do Patrimnio Cultural</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Instituto do Patrimnio Cultural Tabanca Instituto do Patrimnio Cultural</span></span></span>

Visitors should also be aware that the museum's accessibility has varied in recent years, with the Cultural Heritage Institute noting periods of temporary closure. Even so, its significance extends beyond opening hours. It represents an unusual type of collection in which preservation is measured not only by what survives in storage or display cases, but by whether drums continue to sound, processions continue to form and communities continue to recognise the objects as part of their own living tradition.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ipc.cv/en/museus/museu-da-tabanca/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ipc.cv">[Instituto do Patrimnio Cultural]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ipc.cv</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on ipc.cv.</span></span></span>

      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
