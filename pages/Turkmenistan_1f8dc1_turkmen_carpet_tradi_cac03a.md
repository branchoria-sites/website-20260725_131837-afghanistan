## Introduction

Turkmen carpets are more than decorative textiles: they are woven records of identity, memory, and social meaning. In [Turkmenistan]({{ 'where-turkmenistans-strangest/' | relative_url }}), a carpet can preserve clues about the community that produced it, the environment around the weaver, and the occasions for which it was made. The tradition is unusual within the country’s heritage landscape because an everyday household object becomes a cultural archive, carrying patterns that connect craftsmanship with ancestry and belonging. UNESCO recognised traditional Turkmen carpet making as an element of Intangible Cultural Heritage in 2019, describing carpets as objects whose colours and patterns reflect tribal identity, local surroundings, and important moments such as births, weddings, prayers, and mourning rituals.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/traditional-turkmen-carpet-making-art-in-turkmenistan-01486" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ich.unesco.org">[UNESCO ICH]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ich.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ICHTraditional turkmen carpet making art in Turkmenistan</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UNESCO ICHTraditional turkmen carpet making art in Turkmenistan - UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage...</span></span></span>

For visitors interested in unusual collections and cultural curiosities, Turkmen carpets offer a different kind of mystery from archaeological artefacts or folklore objects. Their “hidden stories” are not based on secret codes or supernatural claims, but on the way generations of makers embedded knowledge into repeated forms, weaving techniques, and symbolic designs. Museum displays in Turkmenistan present carpets both as works of art and as evidence of social history.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://museum.gov.tm/en/node/114" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: museum.gov.tm">[State Museum of Turkmenistan]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">museum.gov.tm</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">State Museum of TurkmenistanState Museum of the State Cultural Centre of Turkmenistan - THE STATE MUSEUM OF THE STATE CULTURAL CENTRE OF...</span></span></span>

## Patterns, symbols, and regional identity

A Turkmen carpet is often recognised by its geometric organisation and repeated central motifs known as carpet designs or patterns. These designs are closely associated with different Turkmen communities and historical groups, with UNESCO noting that characteristic patterns relate to the five main Turkmen tribes. The result is a visual language in which shape, colour, and arrangement can indicate cultural connections rather than simply aesthetic choices.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/traditional-turkmen-carpet-making-art-in-turkmenistan-01486" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ich.unesco.org">[UNESCO ICH]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ich.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ICHTraditional turkmen carpet making art in Turkmenistan</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UNESCO ICHTraditional turkmen carpet making art in Turkmenistan - UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage...</span></span></span>

The most recognisable feature of many Turkmen carpets is the repeated medallion-like pattern commonly called the gul. These large geometric motifs dominate the field of many carpets and are often associated with particular regional traditions. However, their meanings should be treated carefully. Modern explanations sometimes describe individual motifs as having fixed symbolic meanings, but carpet traditions are living practices: meanings can vary between makers, communities, periods, and contexts. A pattern may function simultaneously as a marker of identity, a traditional design inherited from earlier generations, and a visually balanced element of decoration.

The relationship between design and place is one of the strongest themes in Turkmen carpet heritage. UNESCO notes that local flora, fauna, and environmental surroundings influence combinations of threads, colours, and designs. This means carpets preserve not only human craftsmanship but also a sense of landscape: the colours and forms reflect the world familiar to the people who produced them.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.unesco.org/archives/multimedia/document-4934" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: unesco.org">[UNESCO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Traditional Turkmen Carpet Making Art In Turkmenistan &#124; Intangible Heritage</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Traditional Turkmen Carpet Making Art In Turkmenistan &#124; Intangible Heritage - UNESCO Multimedia Archives...</span></span></span>

Some connections between carpet imagery and older Central Asian traditions remain subjects of interpretation. Turkmenistan’s cultural institutions have highlighted similarities between carpet-like geometric decoration and ancient archaeological material, including Bronze Age finds from sites such as [Gonur-Depe]({{ 'gonur-depe/' | relative_url }}) and other settlements. Such comparisons are culturally interesting, but they do not prove that every modern carpet motif has an unbroken meaning stretching back thousands of years. Instead, they show how geometric visual traditions have repeatedly appeared in the region’s artistic history.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://turkmenistan.gov.tm/en/post/13747/patterns-on-the-fabric-of-history-an-ode-to-carpets" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: turkmenistan.gov.tm">[Turkmenistan Government]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">turkmenistan.gov.tm</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Turkmenistan Government Patterns on the Fabric of History: An Ode to Carpets</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ernmentPatterns on the Fabric of History: An Ode to CarpetsDecember 29, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 29, 2019</span></span></span>

## Craftsmanship across generations

The unusual strength of Turkmen carpet tradition comes from the combination of technical skill and family transmission. Carpet making has historically been a craft learned through practice, with knowledge of weaving, wool preparation, colouring, and design passed between generations. UNESCO describes the tradition as being maintained through community participation and family-based transmission of skills.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/traditional-turkmen-carpet-making-art-in-turkmenistan-01486" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ich.unesco.org">[UNESCO ICH]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ich.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ICHTraditional turkmen carpet making art in Turkmenistan</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UNESCO ICHTraditional turkmen carpet making art in Turkmenistan - UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage...</span></span></span>

The process begins long before the final pattern appears. Wool must be prepared, spun, and coloured, while the weaver must maintain the correct tension and density of knots. The quality of a carpet depends on factors such as weaving density, colour harmony, edge precision, and the arrangement of patterns. The State Museum of the State Cultural Centre of Turkmenistan describes these qualities as important measures of traditional carpet craftsmanship.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://museum.gov.tm/en/node/114" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: museum.gov.tm">[State Museum of Turkmenistan]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">museum.gov.tm</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">State Museum of TurkmenistanState Museum of the State Cultural Centre of Turkmenistan - THE STATE MUSEUM OF THE STATE CULTURAL CENTRE OF...</span></span></span>

Carpets were also woven for different social purposes rather than only as household decoration. Some were used inside homes and tents, while others decorated walls, horses, camels, or special spaces. Certain carpets were connected with major life events, including childbirth, weddings, religious practice, and mourning. This practical role explains why carpets became powerful symbols: they accompanied people through important stages of life.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/traditional-turkmen-carpet-making-art-in-turkmenistan-01486" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ich.unesco.org">[UNESCO ICH]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ich.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ICHTraditional turkmen carpet making art in Turkmenistan</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UNESCO ICHTraditional turkmen carpet making art in Turkmenistan - UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage...</span></span></span>

The tradition also reveals a tension between preservation and change. A museum object can freeze a carpet at a particular historical moment, while living craft traditions continue to adapt to new markets, tourism, and contemporary tastes. The challenge for heritage institutions is to present carpets not simply as old objects, but as skills and knowledge that continue to exist through makers.

## Museum displays and living traditions

The main public showcase for Turkmen carpet heritage is the National Carpet Museum in Ashgabat, which was established as a specialist institution dedicated to preserving and displaying Turkmen carpets. Its collections include historic carpets and related woven objects, demonstrating how carpets functioned across domestic, ceremonial, and artistic settings.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkmen_Carpet_Museum" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Turkmen Carpet Museum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Turkmen Carpet Museum</span></span></span>

Museum displays transform carpets from household objects into historical documents. A visitor can see differences between regional styles, compare patterns across periods, and understand how a textile can communicate identity without written language. The State Museum of the State Cultural Centre of Turkmenistan also presents carpets as examples of inherited artistic knowledge, describing them as objects that record cultural memory through design.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://museum.gov.tm/en/node/114" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: museum.gov.tm">[State Museum of Turkmenistan]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">museum.gov.tm</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">State Museum of TurkmenistanState Museum of the State Cultural Centre of Turkmenistan - THE STATE MUSEUM OF THE STATE CULTURAL CENTRE OF...</span></span></span>

Large ceremonial carpets have also become part of modern national presentation. Turkmenistan has used carpet exhibitions and public celebrations to promote the craft internationally, including events linked to the recognition of carpet making as intangible heritage. These displays serve both cultural and national purposes: they celebrate craftsmanship while presenting carpets as symbols of unity and continuity.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://turkmenistan.un.org/en/28686-traditional-turkmen-carpet-making-art-turkmenistan-inscribed-list-intangible-cultural" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: turkmenistan.un.org">[The United Nations in Turkmenistan]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">turkmenistan.un.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The United Nations in TurkmenistanTraditional Turkmen carpet making art in Turkmenistan inscribed on the List of Intangible Cultural Heri...</span></span></span>

For a curious visitor, the most interesting aspect of Turkmen carpets is therefore not simply their beauty. Their significance lies in the layers of meaning attached to them: a carpet may be a work of art, a family inheritance, a marker of regional identity, a commercial object, and a museum artefact at the same time. In Turkmenistan’s wider landscape of unusual heritage collections, carpets stand out because they preserve history not through ruins or relics, but through patterns still being created.

        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
