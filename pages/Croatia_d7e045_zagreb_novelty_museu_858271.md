## Introduction

Zagreb has become one of Europe's most distinctive cities for small, concept-driven museums. Rather than competing with large national collections, many of its best-known attractions are built around emotions, participation, sensory tricks or reconstructed experiences. Visitors can move from reading anonymous stories of heartbreak to navigating optical illusions, exploring artist-created folklore worlds or walking through a simulated night out ending in a hangover. Together, these venues blur the line between museum, installation, theatre and interactive entertainment.

What makes Zagreb unusual is not simply the number of novelty museums but the way they transform everyday experiences into curated collections. Ordinary possessions, memories and cultural myths become exhibits, while visitors are often invited to contribute stories, solve puzzles or become part of the display themselves. This has helped establish the city as [Croatia]({{ 'where-croatia-keeps-its-strangest/' | relative_url }})'s centre for experimental museum culture, although it also raises questions about where museums end and commercial attractions begin.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.infozagreb.hr/en/explore-zagreb/culture/museums/museum-of-broken-relationships" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: infozagreb.hr">[infozagreb.hr]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">infozagreb.hr</span><span class="citation-popover-title">museum of broken relationships</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">museum of broken relationships</span></span></span>

## Why Zagreb Has So Many Unusual Museums

Unlike traditional museums built around archaeological finds or fine art, many of Zagreb's independent museums begin with a single idea rather than an established collection. A breakup, a drinking story, an optical illusion or a body of folklore becomes the organising principle from which exhibits are assembled.

Several factors have encouraged this approach:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Many venues occupy relatively small premises in the historic centre, making highly focused themes practical.
* Private founders can develop exhibitions much faster than large public institutions.
* Interactive displays encourage social media sharing and word-of-mouth tourism.
* International visitors increasingly seek memorable experiences lasting an hour rather than full-day museum visits.

</div>

The result is a museum landscape where interpretation often matters more than the rarity of individual objects. Visitors are paying for a carefully designed emotional or sensory experience as much as for access to collections.

## Personal Objects as Public Collections

The clearest example is the Museum of Broken Relationships, which reverses almost every convention of collecting.

Instead of preserving valuable artefacts, it preserves ordinary possessions whose importance comes entirely from personal experience. A toy, letter, household object or piece of clothing becomes meaningful because of the anonymous story attached to it. The museum originated as a travelling exhibition before establishing a permanent home in Zagreb, with donations arriving from around the world rather than from a single national collection.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.infozagreb.hr/en/explore-zagreb/culture/museums/museum-of-broken-relationships" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: infozagreb.hr">[Infozagreb]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">infozagreb.hr</span><span class="citation-popover-title">museum of broken relationships</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">museum of broken relationships</span></span></span>

This approach changes the visitor's role. Rather than admiring rare objects, people read intimate accounts of love, separation, bereavement and family conflict. The emotional narrative becomes the primary exhibit, while the physical object acts almost as supporting evidence.

The museum also illustrates an important shift in contemporary museology. It functions simultaneously as:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* a participatory archive built from public donations;
* an artwork exploring memory and loss;
* a therapeutic ritual for some contributors;
* a tourist attraction built around emotional storytelling.

</div>

Its international reputation demonstrates that deeply personal experiences can attract audiences just as effectively as famous historical collections. At the same time, critics sometimes question whether such narrative installations should be considered museums in the traditional sense or a distinct form of curated art experience. The debate itself has become part of the attraction's identity.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.infozagreb.hr/en/museums-maybe/museum-of-broken-relationships-en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: infozagreb.hr">[infozagreb.hr]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">infozagreb.hr</span><span class="citation-popover-title">museum of broken relationships en</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Museum of Broken Relationships...</span></span></span>

## Immersion, Games and Reconstructed Settings

Many of Zagreb's newer attractions place visitors inside constructed environments rather than asking them to observe displays from a distance.

The Museum of Hangovers presents perhaps the most theatrical example. Rather than displaying historical artefacts relating to alcohol, it reconstructs the experience of a night out through themed rooms, interactive games, real submitted stories and educational material about alcohol and impaired driving. Its founders explicitly describe the museum as showing the full social and cultural picture of drinking rather than celebrating or condemning it.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://museumofhangovers.com/about-us/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: museumofhangovers.com">[Museum of Hangovers]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">museumofhangovers.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Museum of Hangovers About Us</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Museum of HangoversAbout Us - The Story Behind the Museum of Hangovers...</span></span></span>

The attraction demonstrates how experience increasingly replaces collection. Visitors encounter:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* reconstructed environments representing different stages of a night out;
* physical challenges designed to simulate impaired perception;
* anonymously contributed hangover stories;
* educational exhibits addressing alcohol's consequences.

</div>

Here the collection consists less of physical objects than of shared experiences assembled into a coherent narrative.

A different model appears in the Museum of Illusions, the original institution that later expanded into an international franchise. Optical illusions, perspective rooms, holograms and hands-on puzzles encourage visitors to experiment with perception instead of passively viewing exhibits. Although educational principles from psychology and visual science underpin many displays, the presentation prioritises participation and entertainment.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://muzejiluzija.com/en/about-us/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: muzejiluzija.com">[Muzej Iluzija Zagreb]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">muzejiluzija.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Muzej Iluzija Zagreb About us &#124; Museum of Illusions Zagreb</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Muzej Iluzija Zagreb About us &#124; Museum of Illusions Zagreb</span></span></span>

Meanwhile, the Museum of Lost Tales occupies another position within the same landscape. Created by illustrator Zdenko Bašić, it transforms Croatian folklore into immersive artistic environments populated by imagined creatures, witches, spirits and legendary beings. Rather than claiming to reconstruct historical belief exactly, it openly presents an artist's interpretation inspired by traditional stories, allowing visitors to enter an atmospheric world where folklore becomes spatial rather than purely literary.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.infozagreb.hr/en/explore-zagreb/culture/museums/museum-of-broken-relationships" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: infozagreb.hr">[Infozagreb]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">infozagreb.hr</span><span class="citation-popover-title">museum of broken relationships</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">museum of broken relationships</span></span></span>

## Where Museums End and Entertainment Begins

Zagreb's novelty museums deliberately blur institutional categories.

Some possess characteristics associated with conventional museums:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--phrase-pills" markdown="1">

* curated collections;
* documented objects;
* long-term preservation;
* interpretive texts;
* educational ambitions.

</div>

Others resemble themed attractions through their emphasis on games, immersive rooms, photo opportunities or theatrical design.

Rather than seeing this as a sharp division, Zagreb demonstrates a spectrum. At one end stand institutions such as the Museum of Broken Relationships, whose donated objects form a genuine archive despite their unconventional subject. At the other are sensory attractions where interaction itself is the principal exhibit. Between these lie hybrid spaces combining artistic installation, local folklore, education and commercial tourism.

This ambiguity sometimes attracts criticism from museum professionals who question whether highly experiential attractions dilute the meaning of the word "museum". Yet visitor expectations have also changed. Many travellers actively seek memorable, emotionally engaging experiences rather than encyclopaedic displays, and Zagreb's independent museums have responded directly to that demand.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.11386" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: arxiv.org">[arxiv.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">arxiv.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">arXiv Interpersonalizing Intimate Museum Experiences</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Interpersonalizing Intimate Museum ExperiencesNovember 23, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 23, 2020</span></span></span>

## Why They Matter in Croatia's Curiosity Culture

Within Croatia's broader landscape of unusual collections, Zagreb occupies a distinctive niche. Elsewhere in the country, curious attractions often focus on preserved historical objects, religious relics, archaeological discoveries or eccentric collections. Zagreb instead specialises in museums where the central exhibit is memory, emotion, perception or participation.

This shift reflects wider developments in contemporary museum practice. Personal testimony becomes as valuable as physical artefacts; visitors become contributors rather than observers; and carefully designed experiences can preserve aspects of everyday life that traditional collecting often overlooks. Although these venues are frequently compact and privately operated, they have given Zagreb an international reputation for inventive museum concepts that continue to influence how cultural attractions are imagined, marketed and experienced.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.11398" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: arxiv.org">[arxiv.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">arxiv.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">arXiv Designing for Interpersonal Museum Experiences</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">arXiv Designing for Interpersonal Museum Experiences</span></span></span>

    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
</script>
</section>
