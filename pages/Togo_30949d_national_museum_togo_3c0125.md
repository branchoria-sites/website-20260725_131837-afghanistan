## Introduction

The National Museum of Togo in Lomé is the country’s main historical museum, preserving a mixture of archaeological remains, ethnographic objects, photographs, documents and everyday artefacts that show how Togolese societies changed over time. Unlike the more visually dramatic living collections associated with spiritual markets or ritual practice, the museum’s importance lies in its role as a carefully curated record of identity, memory and historical transition. It brings together objects connected with traditional life, European contact, colonial rule, the struggle for independence and the formation of modern Togo.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://togotourisme.tg/site/le-musee-national/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: togotourisme.tg">[Togo Tourisme]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">togotourisme.tg</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Togo Tourisme Le Musée national</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Togo TourismeLe Musée national - Togo Tourisme...</span></span></span>

For visitors interested in unusual collections, the museum is compelling because many ordinary-looking objects — tools, musical instruments, household vessels, clothing and craft items — become historical evidence. They reveal how people worked, worshipped, traded and adapted to political changes. The collection also reflects a wider question in African museums: how should objects shaped by colonial encounters be preserved and interpreted after independence?<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://togotourisme.tg/site/le-musee-national/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: togotourisme.tg">[Togo Tourisme]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">togotourisme.tg</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Togo Tourisme Le Musée national</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Togo TourismeLe Musée national - Togo Tourisme...</span></span></span>

## The Objects That Tell Togo’s Hidden History

### Ethnographic collections and memory

The National Museum of Togo is an ethnographic and historical museum housed at the Palais des Congrès in Lomé. Its collections include objects from different communities of Togo, presenting material culture rather than isolated “curiosities”. The museum displays items such as traditional crafts, musical instruments, pottery, clothing, woodwork and other objects connected with daily life.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://togotourisme.tg/site/le-musee-national/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: togotourisme.tg">[Togo Tourisme]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">togotourisme.tg</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Togo Tourisme Le Musée national</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Togo TourismeLe Musée national - Togo Tourisme...</span></span></span>

What makes these collections significant is the way they preserve evidence of activities that are often poorly represented in written histories. A carved object, woven item or farming tool can reveal information about skills, local economies and social organisation. A musical instrument can point towards traditions of performance and community gatherings, while household objects can show how people stored food, transported water or organised domestic life.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Togo_National_Museum" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Togo National Museum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Togo National Museum</span></span></span>

These objects are not “mysteries” in the sense of unexplained artefacts. Their curiosity comes from their ability to preserve vanished routines and forgotten forms of knowledge. A visitor seeing a simple tool or vessel is effectively seeing a fragment of a much larger social history.

The museum’s collections also sit alongside Togo’s wider heritage of spiritual and ritual objects. International ethnographic collections contain objects from Togo connected with ritual practices, including items made from materials such as shells, cloth, gourds and leather. ## Colonial encounters and independence

The National Museum’s historical collections are unusual because they do not stop at traditional culture. They also preserve evidence of the dramatic political changes that shaped modern Togo. The museum includes documents and photographs connected with early European contact, colonial rule, the independence movement and the leaders of independent Togo.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://togotourisme.tg/site/le-musee-national/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: togotourisme.tg">[Togo Tourisme]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">togotourisme.tg</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Togo Tourisme Le Musée national</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Togo TourismeLe Musée national - Togo Tourisme...</span></span></span>

This makes the museum a place where different layers of history meet. A traditional object may represent centuries of local knowledge, while a colonial-era photograph may reveal how outsiders attempted to classify and govern the same societies. The contrast is important because colonial records were often created from European viewpoints, sometimes reflecting the assumptions and priorities of colonial administrations rather than local communities. Research projects examining colonial-era images of Togo have highlighted the need to reinterpret such material through local perspectives.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://kolonialfotografie.com/en/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: kolonialfotografie.com">[Kolonialfotografie]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">kolonialfotografie.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Startseite...</span></span></span>

The museum’s historical section also includes material relating to the slave trade on the Gulf of Guinea and Togo’s colonial experience. These items connect local history with wider Atlantic and regional networks of forced migration, commerce and political power.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://togotourisme.tg/site/le-musee-national/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: togotourisme.tg">[Togo Tourisme]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">togotourisme.tg</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Togo Tourisme Le Musée national</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Togo TourismeLe Musée national - Togo Tourisme...</span></span></span>

The colonial legacy also affects museums beyond Togo’s borders. Research into German colonial-era collections in European museums has examined hundreds of objects and photographs from the period when Togo was under German colonial rule between 1884 and 1916. Such research has increased attention on provenance — the question of where objects came from, how they were acquired and whether communities of origin should have a greater role in their interpretation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.proveana.de/en/project/provenance-colonial-era-collections-togo-museum-fur-volkerkunde-dresden-and-grassi-museum" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: proveana.de">[Proveana]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">proveana.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Provenance of colonial-era collections from Togo in the Museum für Völkerkunde Dresden and the GRASSI Museum für Völkerkunde zu L...</span></span></span>

For the National Museum of Togo, this history gives the collection an important role: it is not simply preserving old objects, but helping create a national narrative after colonial rule.

## Everyday objects as cultural evidence

The most memorable aspect of the museum’s collections is that many objects are not spectacular in appearance. Their importance comes from the stories attached to them.

A carved wooden item, a woven basket or a musical instrument may appear modest compared with royal treasures or famous archaeological discoveries, but these objects preserve the practical knowledge of communities. They show how people adapted to their environment, passed skills between generations and expressed identity through craftsmanship.

This approach creates a useful contrast with Togo’s living spiritual and marketplace traditions. Places such as ritual markets present objects that remain active within belief systems, while the National Museum places objects into a historical framework. One preserves continuing practice; the other preserves memory and interpretation. Together, they show two different ways that unusual collections can exist: as living cultural systems and as carefully arranged historical records.

The museum’s strength is therefore not based on rarity alone. Its collection matters because it turns everyday materials into evidence of larger historical changes — migration, trade, colonialism, independence and cultural continuity.

    var url = template.replace('{query}', encodeQuery(query || '')).replace('{query_plain}', String(query || ''));
    var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ? '&' : '?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
