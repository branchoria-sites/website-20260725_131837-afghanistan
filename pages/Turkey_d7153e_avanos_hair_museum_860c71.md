## Introduction

Avanos Hair Museum, also known as the Galip Körükçü Hair Museum, is one of [Turkey]({{ 'where-turkeys-strangest-collections-hide/' | relative_url }})’s most unusual personal collections: a cave-like room in the Cappadocian town of Avanos filled with thousands of locks of hair donated by visitors. Unlike a conventional museum built around rare objects, it is a collection created through participation. Each sample represents a person who chose to leave a small physical trace behind, turning a simple material into a record of encounters, memories and travel. The collection began in 1979 under potter Galip Körükçü and later gained international attention, including recognition in Guinness World Records for its size.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.avanos.gov.tr/galip-korukcu-sac-muzesinin-resmi-acilisi-gerceklestirildi" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: avanos.gov.tr">[Avanos Kaymakamlığı]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">avanos.gov.tr</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Avanos KaymakamlığıT.C. The museum’s importance within Turkey’s strange-collection landscape comes less from the scientific value of the hair itself and more from the unusual way the collection was formed. It sits between a tourist attraction, a private archive and a folk tradition of keeping personal mementoes. Visitors are not only observers; they become possible contributors to the story of the collection.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://culturalheritageonline.com/places/chez-galip-hair-museum/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: culturalheritageonline.com">[CulturalHeritageOnline - CHO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">culturalheritageonline.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Cultural Heritage Online</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">CHOChez Galip Hair Museum - CulturalHeritageOnline - CHOJune 4, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 4, 2026</span></span></span>

## The cave collection and its origins

### A potter’s memory became a public collection

The Hair Museum grew out of the pottery world of Avanos rather than from a formal museum project. Galip Körükçü, a master potter whose workshop became known as Chez Galip, created the collection beneath his pottery studio. Avanos itself has a long tradition of ceramics, but this particular collection developed from a personal gesture rather than from archaeological or artistic collecting.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://chezgalip.com/galip-korukcu/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: chezgalip.com">[Chez Galip]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">chezgalip.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Chez Galip Galip Körükçü – Chez Galip ®</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Chez Galip Galip Körükçü – Chez Galip ®</span></span></span>

The commonly repeated origin story describes a woman leaving Avanos who gave Körükçü a lock of her hair as a keepsake. According to the museum’s own account and later travel reports, this small act of remembrance inspired other female visitors to leave their own locks, gradually transforming the workshop space into a growing collection.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://chezgalip.com/hair-museum-avanos/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: chezgalip.com">[Chez Galip]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">chezgalip.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Chez Galip Hair Museum Avanos – Chez Galip ®</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Chez Galip Hair Museum Avanos – Chez Galip ®</span></span></span>

The result is unusual because the museum’s “objects” are not selected for age, rarity or craftsmanship. A strand of hair from one visitor has little conventional museum importance. Its meaning comes from association: who left it, why they left it and how thousands of individual contributions created a shared story.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.hindustantimes.com/travel/visit-a-unique-hair-museum-and-leave-a-lock-in-turkey/story-VYEgis94khBMEaYN0LCvKP.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hindustantimes.com">[Hindustan Times]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hindustantimes.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Hindustan Times Visit a unique hair museum, and leave a lock in Turkey &#124; Travel</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Hindustan TimesVisit a unique hair museum, and leave a lock in Turkey &#124; TravelFebruary 1, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 1, 2020</span></span></span>

### A collection built from participation

Reports about the museum describe a cave room where walls and surfaces are covered with donated hair samples, often accompanied by notes identifying the donors. The collection is estimated by its operators to contain more than 16,000 samples from women around the world, although the exact total is difficult to verify independently because it is a privately managed attraction rather than a research institution with a public catalogue.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://chezgalip.com/hair-museum-avanos/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: chezgalip.com">[Chez Galip]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">chezgalip.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Chez Galip Hair Museum Avanos – Chez Galip ®</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Chez Galip Hair Museum Avanos – Chez Galip ®</span></span></span>

This makes Avanos Hair Museum different from many unusual museums. Traditional collections usually begin with an owner, institution or government deciding what deserves preservation. Here, the collection expanded because visitors voluntarily added themselves to it. The collecting process became part of the attraction.

In this sense, the museum resembles other forms of personal remembrance: autograph books, pilgrimage souvenirs, memorial objects and visitor-created archives. The individual contribution is small, but the accumulated effect creates a collective record of human participation.

## Why visitors donated personal traces

### Hair as a symbol of identity and memory

Hair has a long connection with personal identity. It grows from the body, changes with time and is closely associated with appearance and self-image. This helps explain why a lock of hair can feel more personal than an ordinary souvenir. At Avanos, the donation is not valuable because of the material itself; it is valuable because it represents a moment when someone chose to leave evidence of their presence.

The museum’s appeal comes from this unusual exchange. A visitor enters as a tourist but can leave as part of the collection. The act creates a direct relationship between the traveller and the place, which is one reason the museum has remained memorable despite its unconventional subject matter.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.hindustantimes.com/travel/visit-a-unique-hair-museum-and-leave-a-lock-in-turkey/story-VYEgis94khBMEaYN0LCvKP.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hindustantimes.com">[Hindustan Times]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hindustantimes.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Hindustan Times Visit a unique hair museum, and leave a lock in Turkey &#124; Travel</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Hindustan TimesVisit a unique hair museum, and leave a lock in Turkey &#124; TravelFebruary 1, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 1, 2020</span></span></span>

### The story behind the donation matters as much as the object

Many curiosity collections are really collections of stories disguised as collections of things. Avanos Hair Museum follows this pattern. The hair strands are visually striking, but the deeper attraction is the narrative: thousands of people have contributed something personal to a room hidden beneath a pottery workshop.

The collection therefore works more like a social archive than a historical archive. It does not document a major political event, scientific discovery or artistic movement. Instead, it preserves small acts of connection between visitors and a particular place.

There is also a practical element to the tradition. The museum encourages visitors who wish to participate to leave a sample, while the collection’s organisers have used the story of donated hair as part of the wider experience of visiting the pottery workshop.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://chezgalip.com/hair-museum-avanos/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: chezgalip.com">[Chez Galip]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">chezgalip.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Chez Galip Hair Museum Avanos – Chez Galip ®</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Chez Galip Hair Museum Avanos – Chez Galip ®</span></span></span>

## The museum’s place in strange collections

Avanos Hair Museum occupies an unusual category within Turkey’s curiosity culture. It is not an archaeological museum, a folklore institution or a scientific display. It is a privately created collection where tourism, craftsmanship and personal storytelling overlap.

Its Guinness World Records recognition helped transform a local curiosity into an international attraction, but the record alone does not explain its appeal. A room containing thousands of hair samples could easily become a simple novelty. What gives it cultural interest is the collecting method: ordinary people voluntarily transformed themselves into part of the exhibit.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.avanos.gov.tr/galip-korukcu-sac-muzesinin-resmi-acilisi-gerceklestirildi" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: avanos.gov.tr">[Avanos Kaymakamlığı]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">avanos.gov.tr</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Avanos KaymakamlığıT.C. AVANOS KAYMAKAMLIĞI</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Galip Körükçü Saç Müzesi’nin Resmi Açılışı GerçekleştirildiMay 21, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 21, 2026</span></span></span>

The museum also raises questions about what counts as a collection worth preserving. Conventional museums often focus on objects with historical, artistic or scientific importance. Avanos suggests another approach: collections can preserve experiences, emotions and participation. The hair is not a historical artefact in the usual sense, but the accumulated donations reveal something about tourism, memory and the human desire to leave a mark.

For visitors exploring Turkey’s unusual heritage, the Hair Museum is therefore best understood not simply as a room of strange objects, but as a portrait of collecting itself. It shows how a personal gesture can grow into a public curiosity, and how an everyday material can become meaningful when thousands of people attach their own stories to it.

    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket];
    if (!cfg) return '#';
    var template = String(cfg.url_template || 'https://www.ebay.com/sch/i.html?_nkw={query}');
    var url = template.replace('{query}', encodeQuery(query || '')).replace('{query_plain}', String(query || ''));
    var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ? '&' : '?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
