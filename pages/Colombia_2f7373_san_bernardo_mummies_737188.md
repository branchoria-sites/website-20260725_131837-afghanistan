## Introduction

The mummies of San Bernardo are unusual not because they are ancient, but because they are modern people whose bodies dried naturally after burial instead of decomposing in the expected way. Discovered during cemetery exhumations in the 1960s, the preserved dead gradually became one of [Colombia]({{ 'inside-colombias-strangest-museums-and/' | relative_url }})'s strangest heritage attractions. Yet the central mystery remains unresolved: no single explanation has been accepted for why some bodies became naturally mummified while others did not. Official accounts, forensic opinion, local tradition and family memories all offer different answers, and each explains only part of the phenomenon. Rather than proving a supernatural event or a scientific certainty, San Bernardo presents an intriguing case where environmental conditions, burial practices and local belief overlap without producing a definitive solution.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cundinamarca.gov.co/noticias/el-misterio-de-las-momias-naturales-en-el-municipio-de-san-bernardo" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cundinamarca.gov.co">[cundinamarca.gov.co]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cundinamarca.gov.co</span><span class="citation-popover-title">El misterio de las momias naturales en el municipio de San Bernardo</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">July 22, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 22, 2025</span></span></span>

## What Preserved the Mummies of San Bernardo?

### How the Mummies Were Discovered

The phenomenon came to public attention after cemetery workers exhumed a body in 1963 and found that it had not decomposed. Instead, the deceased still retained skin, hair and much of their clothing. As more graves were reopened over subsequent years, similar discoveries convinced local people that this was not an isolated accident but a recurring feature of the cemetery.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cundinamarca.gov.co/noticias/el-misterio-de-las-momias-naturales-en-el-municipio-de-san-bernardo" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cundinamarca.gov.co">[cundinamarca.gov.co]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cundinamarca.gov.co</span><span class="citation-popover-title">El misterio de las momias naturales en el municipio de San Bernardo</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">July 22, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 22, 2025</span></span></span>

Unlike the famous mummies of Egypt or the Andes, these bodies were never intentionally embalmed. They belonged to ordinary residents buried according to modern Catholic customs. Their preservation therefore immediately raised an obvious question: what had interrupted the normal process of decay?

Today a selection of the best-preserved bodies is displayed in the José Arquímedes Castro Mausoleum. The museum presents them not as archaeological relics but as part of the town's recent history, with many visitors recognising that the individuals once had living relatives in the community. This close connection between display and memory distinguishes San Bernardo from most mummy collections around the world.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cundinamarca.gov.co/noticias/el-misterio-de-las-momias-naturales-en-el-municipio-de-san-bernardo" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cundinamarca.gov.co">[cundinamarca.gov.co]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cundinamarca.gov.co</span><span class="citation-popover-title">El misterio de las momias naturales en el municipio de San Bernardo</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">July 22, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 22, 2025</span></span></span>

## Drying Vaults, Diets and Other Explanations

Despite decades of interest, there is still no universally accepted explanation for the natural mummification.

The strongest scientific ideas focus on **rapid dehydration rather than mysterious preservation**. [Human remains]({{ 'human-remains-28c5aa/' | relative_url }}) decay because bacteria, insects and moisture break down soft tissue. If a body dries quickly enough, bacterial activity is greatly reduced and natural mummification can occur.

Several factors have therefore been proposed:

* **Burial vault conditions.** Many researchers consider the cemetery's above-ground or sealed burial vaults the most plausible factor. Air circulation, limited insect access and favourable microclimatic conditions may have removed moisture from the bodies before extensive decay began. Some anthropologists have compared the process to slow natural dehydration rather than artificial embalming.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://caracol.com.co/2026/04/04/por-que-momifican-a-las-personas-en-colombia-este-es-el-pueblo-que-tiene-un-museo-de-momias/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: caracol.com.co">[Caracol Radio]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">caracol.com.co</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">April 4, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 4, 2026</span></span></span>
* **Climate and local environment.** Official descriptions point to combinations of temperature, relatively low humidity, soil characteristics and local environmental conditions that may inhibit decomposition. However, none of these factors has been demonstrated conclusively through comprehensive scientific study.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cundinamarca.gov.co/noticias/el-misterio-de-las-momias-naturales-en-el-municipio-de-san-bernardo" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cundinamarca.gov.co">[cundinamarca.gov.co]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cundinamarca.gov.co</span><span class="citation-popover-title">El misterio de las momias naturales en el municipio de San Bernardo</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">July 22, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 22, 2025</span></span></span>
* **Diet.** Perhaps the town's most famous explanation is that regular consumption of local foods—especially guatila (chayote) and balú (a locally grown bean)—helped preserve the bodies after death. This remains a popular local belief, but there is no convincing scientific evidence supporting it. Similar foods are eaten elsewhere in Colombia without producing naturally mummified cemeteries.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.noticiascaracol.com/colombia/el-pueblo-de-colombia-donde-los-muertos-se-convierten-en-momias-que-misterio-hay-detras-rg10?_amp=true" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: noticiascaracol.com">[Noticias Caracol]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">noticiascaracol.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Noticias CaracolEl pueblo de Colombia donde los muertos se convierten en momias: ¿qué misterio hay detrás? - NoticiasCaracol...</span></span></span>

One important point often overlooked is that **not every body becomes mummified**. The selective nature of the preservation suggests that several conditions probably need to occur together. If diet alone were responsible, far more burials would show identical preservation. Likewise, if climate alone explained everything, similar cemeteries would be common throughout the region.

The absence of detailed long-term forensic investigation means the exact combination of temperature, airflow, humidity, coffin construction, burial vault design and individual biological factors remains uncertain. Researchers therefore generally describe San Bernardo as a case of natural mummification whose precise mechanism has yet to be fully established.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://caracol.com.co/2026/04/04/por-que-momifican-a-las-personas-en-colombia-este-es-el-pueblo-que-tiene-un-museo-de-momias/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: caracol.com.co">[com.co]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">caracol.com.co</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">April 4, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 4, 2026</span></span></span>

## Why Folklore Still Matters

Scientific uncertainty has allowed local beliefs to remain an important part of the story.

Some residents interpret the preserved bodies as evidence that particularly virtuous people were protected after death. Others regard the phenomenon simply as one of God's mysteries rather than something science must necessarily explain. During religious commemorations, the mummies continue to occupy a place within local remembrance rather than existing solely as museum exhibits. Flowers, prayers and family visits reinforce that these are still viewed as relatives, not anonymous specimens.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cundinamarca.gov.co/noticias/el-misterio-de-las-momias-naturales-en-el-municipio-de-san-bernardo" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cundinamarca.gov.co">[cundinamarca.gov.co]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cundinamarca.gov.co</span><span class="citation-popover-title">El misterio de las momias naturales en el municipio de San Bernardo</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">July 22, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 22, 2025</span></span></span>

These beliefs are not presented as scientific explanations, but they help explain why the town treats the mummies with a mixture of curiosity, reverence and civic pride. The attraction's atmosphere is therefore very different from a commercial horror display. Local identity and religious tradition remain central to how the preserved bodies are interpreted.

## From Family Dead to Local Heritage

The transformation of the mummies into public heritage has been gradual rather than planned.

Initially the preserved bodies simply presented cemetery workers with an unexpected practical problem. Over time, however, repeated discoveries encouraged the municipality to preserve selected examples instead of reburying them. The José Arquímedes Castro Mausoleum eventually became both a museum and a memorial, helping establish San Bernardo as one of Colombia's most distinctive destinations for unusual heritage. Official tourism material now emphasises respectful remembrance alongside scientific curiosity rather than sensational mystery.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cundinamarca.gov.co/noticias/el-misterio-de-las-momias-naturales-en-el-municipio-de-san-bernardo" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cundinamarca.gov.co">[cundinamarca.gov.co]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cundinamarca.gov.co</span><span class="citation-popover-title">El misterio de las momias naturales en el municipio de San Bernardo</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">July 22, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 22, 2025</span></span></span>

This change also introduced ethical questions familiar to museums displaying human remains. The people exhibited are not distant archaeological figures but comparatively recent townspeople. Academic work examining the site has shown that relatives, museum staff, visitors and local authorities sometimes understand the mummies differently: as family members, cultural heritage, tourist attractions, religious symbols or scientific curiosities. Those perspectives do not always align, making San Bernardo an important case study in how communities negotiate ownership of the dead.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://redcol.minciencias.gov.co/Record/UNIANDES2_af1fe4c545b055b4863d12a26477ac77/Description" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: redcol.minciencias.gov.co">[Redcol]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">redcol.minciencias.gov.co</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on minciencias.gov.co.</span></span></span>

## Why the Mystery Endures

The continuing fascination of San Bernardo lies less in claims of the paranormal than in the lack of a complete explanation. Natural mummification itself is a recognised process and has occurred in dry caves, deserts, frozen landscapes and other unusual environments around the world. What makes San Bernardo distinctive is that it occurred repeatedly in a modern municipal cemetery without deliberate preservation, and under conditions that have not been fully reproduced or explained.

For visitors, the mummies therefore represent two intertwined stories. One is scientific: an unresolved question about exactly how natural preservation occurred. The other is cultural: how a small Colombian town transformed the unexpected preservation of its own dead into a place of remembrance, heritage and enduring curiosity.
