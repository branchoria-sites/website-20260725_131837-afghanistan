## Introduction

Gishora is one of [Burundi]({{ 'where-burundi-keeps-its-strangest/' | relative_url }})'s most distinctive heritage sites because it preserves a collection that is meant to be heard as much as seen. Rather than displaying royal drums behind glass, the Gishora Royal Drum Sanctuary near Gitega keeps them alive through regular ceremonial performance, traditional architecture and the hereditary knowledge of the drummers who care for them. This makes it less like a conventional museum and more like a living collection, where the objects, the performers and the rituals remain inseparable. Burundi's famous royal drumming tradition was recognised by UNESCO in 2014 as Intangible Cultural Heritage, reflecting the importance of preserving not only the drums themselves but also the music, dance, poetry and ceremonial practices surrounding them.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ich.unesco.org/en/decisions/9.COM/10.10" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ich.unesco.org">[ICH UNESCO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ich.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ICH UNESCODecision of the Intergovernmental Committee: 9.CO M 10.10</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Decision of the Intergovernmental Committee: 9.COM 10.10 - UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage...</span></span></span>

## Why Gishora's Drums Are More Than Music

The royal drums at Gishora were historically symbols of political authority rather than ordinary musical instruments. In the former Kingdom of Burundi, ceremonial drums announced major state occasions including royal accessions, victories, peace ceremonies and other events connected with the monarchy. Their sound represented the authority of the king and the unity of the kingdom rather than public entertainment.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://gishoradrumsanctuary.com/about-roya-drums/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gishoradrumsanctuary.com">[gishoradrumsanctuary.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gishoradrumsanctuary.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">About Roya Drums</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Gishora Drum Sanctuary BurundiJuly 28, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 28, 2025</span></span></span>

The sanctuary itself reinforces this meaning. Situated on a hill roughly seven kilometres from Gitega, Gishora recreates the layout of a royal residence, with the sacred drum house at its centre. Visitors encounter architecture, performance space and ceremonial objects as parts of one integrated cultural landscape rather than isolated exhibits. Burundi's tourism authority describes it as one of the country's best-known cultural sites where visitors can experience traditional performances within a reconstructed royal compound.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://tourisme.gov.bi/en/cultural-tourism/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: tourisme.gov.bi">[Tourisme Burundi]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">tourisme.gov.bi</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Tourisme Burundi Cultural Tourism – Tourism</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Tourisme Burundi Cultural Tourism – Tourism</span></span></span>

Unlike many collections where preservation means limiting physical contact, Gishora deliberately allows the collection to remain active. The drums are maintained through continued use according to established traditions rather than being treated as silent historical artefacts.

## The Drums and the Former Monarchy

Tradition associates the sanctuary with King Mwezi Gisabo, one of Burundi's last independent monarchs, who established the site during the nineteenth century after consolidating his authority. The royal drums housed there became enduring symbols of kingship and national identity. Although Burundi abolished its monarchy in the twentieth century, the sanctuary survived as a place where this aspect of royal culture could continue outside its original political setting.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://gishoradrumsanctuary.com/about-roya-drums/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gishoradrumsanctuary.com">[gishoradrumsanctuary.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gishoradrumsanctuary.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">About Roya Drums</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Gishora Drum Sanctuary BurundiJuly 28, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 28, 2025</span></span></span>

This distinction matters because Gishora is not a museum of royal possessions in the ordinary sense. The significance of the drums comes from their ceremonial role. Their size, placement and handling all carry symbolic weight, and historically not everyone was permitted to play them. Their importance rested as much in social rules and ritual authority as in craftsmanship.

For visitors interested in unusual collections, this makes Gishora distinctive within Burundi. The "collection" consists not simply of preserved objects but of relationships between objects, architecture, hereditary custodians and ceremonial practice.

## How Performance Preserves the Collection

### Living conservation rather than static display

The most unusual feature of Gishora is its method of preservation. Instead of freezing tradition in a historical reconstruction, the sanctuary maintains continuity by regularly performing it.

Groups of specialist drummers perform highly coordinated routines combining:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--phrase-pills" markdown="1">

* powerful ensemble drumming
* synchronised movement
* traditional dance
* heroic poetry
* ceremonial songs

</div>

UNESCO's inscription specifically recognises this combination of music, movement and oral tradition as the cultural element being safeguarded, rather than the drums alone.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ich.unesco.org/en/decisions/9.COM/10.10" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ich.unesco.org">[ICH UNESCO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ich.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ICH UNESCODecision of the Intergovernmental Committee: 9.CO M 10.10</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Decision of the Intergovernmental Committee: 9.COM 10.10 - UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage...</span></span></span>

The custodians of the sanctuary pass techniques between generations, ensuring that specialised rhythms, performance etiquette and ceremonial knowledge continue alongside maintenance of the physical instruments. In this sense, the performers function much like conservators in a museum, except that their principal conservation tool is repeated practice rather than climate-controlled storage.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://gishoradrumsanctuary.com/heritage/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gishoradrumsanctuary.com">[gishoradrumsanctuary.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gishoradrumsanctuary.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Gishora Drum Sanctuary BurundiJuly 28, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 28, 2025</span></span></span>

### Why the collection cannot be separated from its performers

Removing the drums from performance would preserve the wood but lose much of their cultural meaning. Their significance depends on rhythm, choreography, oral knowledge and collective memory.

This is why UNESCO classifies Burundi's royal drumming as intangible cultural heritage. The protected heritage includes:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--phrase-stack" markdown="1">

* performance techniques
* ceremonial knowledge
* inherited teaching traditions
* associated songs and poetry
* the cultural meaning attached to the drums

</div>

The physical instruments remain important, but they represent only one part of a much larger cultural system.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ich.unesco.org/en/decisions/9.COM/10.10" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ich.unesco.org">[ICH UNESCO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ich.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ICH UNESCODecision of the Intergovernmental Committee: 9.CO M 10.10</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Decision of the Intergovernmental Committee: 9.COM 10.10 - UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage...</span></span></span>

## Tourism, Ceremony and Living Heritage

For modern visitors, Gishora occupies an unusual space between heritage attraction, ceremonial site and educational centre.

A typical visit combines a guided explanation of the sanctuary with a live performance by traditional drummers. Rather than observing anonymous exhibits, visitors encounter practitioners who explain how the drums functioned within Burundi's royal traditions and demonstrate techniques that remain actively taught. The sanctuary's operators emphasise respectful interpretation rather than theatrical re-enactment, presenting the site as a place where historical practice continues in adapted form.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://gishoradrumsanctuary.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gishoradrumsanctuary.com">[gishoradrumsanctuary.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gishoradrumsanctuary.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Welcome to Gishora Drum Sanctuary -Burundi Royal Drums</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Welcome to Gishora Drum Sanctuary -Burundi Royal Drums</span></span></span>

At the same time, Gishora illustrates one of the central tensions in living heritage. Public performances help generate tourism, visibility and financial support for cultural preservation, but organisers must also ensure that traditions are not reduced to entertainment divorced from their historical meaning. UNESCO's recognition reflects this balance by highlighting safeguarding, transmission and community participation rather than performance for visitors alone.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ich.unesco.org/en/decisions/9.COM/10.10" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ich.unesco.org">[ICH UNESCO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ich.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ICH UNESCODecision of the Intergovernmental Committee: 9.CO M 10.10</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Decision of the Intergovernmental Committee: 9.COM 10.10 - UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage...</span></span></span>

Within Burundi's landscape of unusual collections, Gishora therefore stands apart. It is not a curiosity cabinet filled with rare objects, nor an ethnographic museum preserving a vanished past. Instead, it demonstrates how a collection can survive because it continues to be performed, taught and understood by the community that inherited it. The drums remain remarkable not because they are old, but because they still fulfil the cultural role that has given them meaning for generations.

    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
