## Introduction

[Djibouti]({{ 'where-djibouti-keeps-its-strangest/' | relative_url }}) is in the middle of a significant change in how it manages its archaeological heritage. For many years, excavated artefacts were largely held by research institutions, stored after archaeological missions, or managed through fragmented arrangements with limited public visibility. Today, government policy is moving towards a coordinated national system in which archaeological discoveries are inventoried, conserved, legally protected and, eventually, displayed through the National Museum and related heritage institutions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.journalofficiel.dj/arabe/texte-juridique/loi-n208-an-25-9eme-l-relatif-au-patrimoine-culturel-national-de-la-republique-de-djibouti/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: journalofficiel.dj">[eJO - Journal Officiel de Djibouti]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">journalofficiel.dj</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">eJO - Journal Officiel de DjiboutiLoi n° 208/AN/25/9ème L relatif au patrimoine culturel national de la République de Djibouti. - eJOJanu...</span></span></span>

For anyone interested in unusual collections, this transition is important because the story is less about spectacular museum galleries than about building the foundations of one. Rather than discovering a finished cabinet of curiosities, visitors are witnessing the creation of a national archaeological collection whose priorities are conservation, documentation and state stewardship before large-scale public exhibition.

## What the National Museum legally controls

The turning point came with the creation of the National Museum of Djibouti as an autonomous public institution in 2021. The founding decree gave the museum responsibility for collecting, conserving, researching and exhibiting material connected with the country's history and civilisations. One of its most important provisions transferred archaeological collections previously held by the Centre for Studies and Research of Djibouti into the museum's care, beginning the process of bringing scattered holdings under one national institution.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.journalofficiel.dj/arabe/texte-juridique/loi-n208-an-25-9eme-l-relatif-au-patrimoine-culturel-national-de-la-republique-de-djibouti/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: journalofficiel.dj">[eJO - Journal Officiel de Djibouti]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">journalofficiel.dj</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">eJO - Journal Officiel de DjiboutiLoi n° 208/AN/25/9ème L relatif au patrimoine culturel national de la République de Djibouti. - eJOJanu...</span></span></span>

This represents a major governance change rather than simply opening another museum building. Instead of archaeological objects remaining primarily within research collections, the state established a legal framework in which:

* archaeological collections become part of a national museum system;
* conservation and documentation become permanent institutional responsibilities;
* exhibitions are expected to grow from professionally managed collections rather than temporary research displays; and
* archaeological material can be managed alongside historical, artistic and ethnographic collections within a single public institution.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.journalofficiel.dj/arabe/texte-juridique/loi-n208-an-25-9eme-l-relatif-au-patrimoine-culturel-national-de-la-republique-de-djibouti/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: journalofficiel.dj">[eJO - Journal Officiel de Djibouti]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">journalofficiel.dj</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">eJO - Journal Officiel de DjiboutiLoi n° 208/AN/25/9ème L relatif au patrimoine culturel national de la République de Djibouti. - eJOJanu...</span></span></span>

The approach reflects a shift from project-based archaeology towards long-term collection management. Excavation is no longer treated as the end of archaeological work; preserving, cataloguing and interpreting finds becomes an equally important responsibility.

## How archaeological finds are conserved

Djibouti's newer heritage legislation greatly expands the state's authority over archaeological discoveries. The 2025 national cultural heritage law defines archaeological finds—including objects recovered from authorised excavations, accidental discoveries and even illicit excavations—as protected cultural property belonging under state supervision.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.journalofficiel.dj/arabe/texte-juridique/loi-n208-an-25-9eme-l-relatif-au-patrimoine-culturel-national-de-la-republique-de-djibouti/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: journalofficiel.dj">[eJO - Journal Officiel de Djibouti]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">journalofficiel.dj</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">eJO - Journal Officiel de DjiboutiLoi n° 208/AN/25/9ème L relatif au patrimoine culturel national de la République de Djibouti. - eJOJanu...</span></span></span>

The law establishes several linked mechanisms intended to build a permanent national collection.

First, archaeological excavations require official authorisation. Excavation is treated as scientific research rather than private collecting, with approval involving both cultural and research authorities.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.journalofficiel.dj/arabe/texte-juridique/loi-n208-an-25-9eme-l-relatif-au-patrimoine-culturel-national-de-la-republique-de-djibouti/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: journalofficiel.dj">[eJO - Journal Officiel de Djibouti]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">journalofficiel.dj</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">eJO - Journal Officiel de DjiboutiLoi n° 208/AN/25/9ème L relatif au patrimoine culturel national de la République de Djibouti. - eJOJanu...</span></span></span>

Second, archaeological objects recovered through excavations are placed under state custody. The Ministry responsible for culture assumes responsibility for their conservation while working with national research centres on scientific study. This aims to prevent important discoveries disappearing into dispersed institutional storage or private ownership.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.journalofficiel.dj/arabe/texte-juridique/loi-n208-an-25-9eme-l-relatif-au-patrimoine-culturel-national-de-la-republique-de-djibouti/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: journalofficiel.dj">[eJO - Journal Officiel de Djibouti]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">journalofficiel.dj</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">eJO - Journal Officiel de DjiboutiLoi n° 208/AN/25/9ème L relatif au patrimoine culturel national de la République de Djibouti. - eJOJanu...</span></span></span>

Third, preventive archaeology becomes part of infrastructure planning. Major construction projects are expected to include archaeological assessment before development proceeds, helping identify and protect sites that might otherwise be destroyed without documentation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.journalofficiel.dj/arabe/texte-juridique/loi-n208-an-25-9eme-l-relatif-au-patrimoine-culturel-national-de-la-republique-de-djibouti/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: journalofficiel.dj">[eJO - Journal Officiel de Djibouti]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">journalofficiel.dj</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">eJO - Journal Officiel de DjiboutiLoi n° 208/AN/25/9ème L relatif au patrimoine culturel national de la République de Djibouti. - eJOJanu...</span></span></span>

The legislation also recognises that conservation extends beyond physical storage. It creates the legal basis for a national digital platform intended to inventory, document and publicise cultural heritage, including archaeological collections. Digital cataloguing is designed to improve long-term collection management while making information about heritage more accessible even when individual objects are not yet on permanent display.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.journalofficiel.dj/arabe/texte-juridique/loi-n208-an-25-9eme-l-relatif-au-patrimoine-culturel-national-de-la-republique-de-djibouti/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: journalofficiel.dj">[eJO - Journal Officiel de Djibouti]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">journalofficiel.dj</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">eJO - Journal Officiel de DjiboutiLoi n° 208/AN/25/9ème L relatif au patrimoine culturel national de la République de Djibouti. - eJOJanu...</span></span></span>

## Why building collections matters

For a country with relatively few established museums, creating a national archaeological collection has consequences beyond preserving individual artefacts.

Many of Djibouti's internationally significant archaeological resources—including prehistoric rock art, ancient settlement sites and burial landscapes—have long been better known through academic publications than through museum interpretation. A stronger national collection provides the infrastructure needed to connect excavation, conservation, education and public exhibition within Djibouti itself rather than relying primarily on foreign research projects.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.journalofficiel.dj/arabe/texte-juridique/loi-n208-an-25-9eme-l-relatif-au-patrimoine-culturel-national-de-la-republique-de-djibouti/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: journalofficiel.dj">[eJO - Journal Officiel de Djibouti]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">journalofficiel.dj</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">eJO - Journal Officiel de DjiboutiLoi n° 208/AN/25/9ème L relatif au patrimoine culturel national de la République de Djibouti. - eJOJanu...</span></span></span>

The legal framework also strengthens protection against illicit trafficking. By explicitly defining archaeological discoveries as protected state heritage and setting penalties for unauthorised excavations or failure to surrender discovered objects, the legislation reinforces national ownership of material recovered from archaeological sites.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.journalofficiel.dj/arabe/texte-juridique/loi-n208-an-25-9eme-l-relatif-au-patrimoine-culturel-national-de-la-republique-de-djibouti/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: journalofficiel.dj">[eJO - Journal Officiel de Djibouti]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">journalofficiel.dj</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">eJO - Journal Officiel de DjiboutiLoi n° 208/AN/25/9ème L relatif au patrimoine culturel national de la République de Djibouti. - eJOJanu...</span></span></span>

For unusual collections enthusiasts, this means future displays are likely to emerge from professionally documented archaeological assemblages with clear provenance rather than isolated curiosities whose origins are uncertain.

## Why public access remains uncertain

Although the legal framework is now much clearer, public access remains the least developed part of the system.

The National Museum has an official mandate to conserve, research and exhibit collections, but reliable public information about permanent archaeological galleries, opening schedules, collection catalogues or regular visitor services remains limited. The institutional emphasis has so far been on establishing governance, transferring collections and strengthening conservation capacity rather than creating large public exhibitions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.journalofficiel.dj/arabe/texte-juridique/loi-n208-an-25-9eme-l-relatif-au-patrimoine-culturel-national-de-la-republique-de-djibouti/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: journalofficiel.dj">[eJO - Journal Officiel de Djibouti]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">journalofficiel.dj</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">eJO - Journal Officiel de DjiboutiLoi n° 208/AN/25/9ème L relatif au patrimoine culturel national de la République de Djibouti. - eJOJanu...</span></span></span>

The 2025 heritage law makes public access an explicit museum responsibility, requiring recognised museums to conserve, study and make their collections accessible while supporting research and public knowledge. Developing exhibition spaces, interpretation, conservation laboratories and digital catalogues is a gradual process that typically follows the legal reforms.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.journalofficiel.dj/arabe/texte-juridique/loi-n208-an-25-9eme-l-relatif-au-patrimoine-culturel-national-de-la-republique-de-djibouti/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: journalofficiel.dj">[eJO - Journal Officiel de Djibouti]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">journalofficiel.dj</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">eJO - Journal Officiel de DjiboutiLoi n° 208/AN/25/9ème L relatif au patrimoine culturel national de la République de Djibouti. - eJOJanu...</span></span></span>

As a result, travellers should think of Djibouti's archaeological collections as being in transition. The country's most significant achievement at present is not the existence of a vast public archaeological museum but the creation of the legal and institutional foundations from which one can steadily develop.
