## Introduction

[Sri Lanka]({{ 'where-sri-lanka-keeps-its-strangest/' | relative_url }})'s most unusual public collections are not always found in conventional museums. Some of the country's most striking displays are maintained by universities, where teaching collections of dissected human bodies, skeletons, preserved organs and thousands of animal specimens are used for medical education and biological research while also being opened, under controlled conditions, to schools and members of the public. Rather than presenting the macabre for entertainment, these institutions aim to explain anatomy, evolution, biodiversity and disease through carefully curated scientific collections.

For visitors interested in the country's stranger side, these museums offer a rare opportunity to see the intersection of science, education and public engagement. They stand apart from commercial curiosity attractions because the specimens remain part of active teaching and research programmes, with public access shaped by ethical rules and academic priorities rather than spectacle.

## What visitors encounter in the Peradeniya anatomy museum

The best-known visitor-facing anatomy collection is the Museum of Human Anatomy at the University of Peradeniya's Faculty of Medicine. Opened in 2018, it was created primarily for medical and allied health education but was also designed to welcome schools and the wider public by prior arrangement.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://med.pdn.ac.lk/departments/anatomy/museum.php" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: med.pdn.ac.lk">[med.pdn.ac.lk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">med.pdn.ac.lk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on pdn.ac.lk.</span></span></span>

Visitors find a modern gallery rather than a traditional Victorian-style anatomy room. Human anatomical specimens are displayed in illuminated glass cases with explanatory labels presented in Sinhala, Tamil and English. The collection includes:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--example" markdown="1">

* Dissected specimens from every major region of the human body.
* Complete skeletal material used to explain bone structure.
* Cross-sectional body slices that help demonstrate modern medical imaging such as CT and MRI.
* Embryological specimens showing normal human development alongside examples of congenital abnormalities.
* Anatomical illustrations and artwork that place scientific study within the history of medicine.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://med.pdn.ac.lk/departments/anatomy/museum.php" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: med.pdn.ac.lk">[med.pdn.ac.lk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">med.pdn.ac.lk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on pdn.ac.lk.</span></span></span>

</div>

The emphasis throughout is educational. Instead of encouraging shock, displays explain how organs relate to one another, how disease alters anatomy and how medical students learn from donated human remains. The museum explicitly describes itself as a place to improve public understanding of the human body rather than as a collection of medical curiosities.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://med.pdn.ac.lk/departments/anatomy/museum.php" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: med.pdn.ac.lk">[med.pdn.ac.lk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">med.pdn.ac.lk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on pdn.ac.lk.</span></span></span>

Its existence also reflects a broader shift in anatomy education. Modern medical schools increasingly complement dissection laboratories with permanent museum displays that allow students to revisit complex structures long after practical classes have finished. For non-specialists, these preserved specimens provide an unusually detailed view of the human body that is rarely available outside medical institutions.

## How university zoological collections preserve animals

Sri Lanka's university zoological museums provide a different form of curiosity. Their collections preserve the country's wildlife—and selected overseas species—in forms ranging from skeletons and taxidermy mounts to specimens stored in preserving fluid.

One of the largest public collections is the Zoological Museum at the University of Sri Jayewardenepura. The museum houses more than 8,000 specimens representing Sri Lankan and exotic animals preserved as wet specimens, dry preparations, skins and skeletons. Although built for undergraduate teaching and scientific research, it actively welcomes school groups, university students and members of the public on weekdays.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://science.sjp.ac.lk/zoo/services-and-facilities/museum/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: science.sjp.ac.lk">[science.sjp.ac.lk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">science.sjp.ac.lk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Museum – Department of Zoology</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Museum – Department of Zoology</span></span></span>

Visitors encounter collections organised by major animal groups rather than by dramatic themes. Highlights include:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Amphibians and reptiles preserved for comparative anatomy.
* Freshwater, marine and brackish-water fish collections.
* Bird skins, mounted specimens and skeletal material.
* Mammalian skeletons and taxidermy preparations.
* Extensive insect collections and other invertebrates preserved using both traditional and modern museum techniques.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://science.sjp.ac.lk/zoo/services-and-facilities/museum/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: science.sjp.ac.lk">[science.sjp.ac.lk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">science.sjp.ac.lk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Museum – Department of Zoology</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Museum – Department of Zoology</span></span></span>

</div>

Because the museum continues to receive specimens from research projects and authorised donations, it functions as a living scientific collection rather than a static exhibition. Curators also train students in museum conservation, specimen preparation and preservation techniques, illustrating how biological collections are maintained for future generations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://science.sjp.ac.lk/zoo/services-and-facilities/museum/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: science.sjp.ac.lk">[science.sjp.ac.lk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">science.sjp.ac.lk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Museum – Department of Zoology</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Museum – Department of Zoology</span></span></span>

A similarly important resource exists at the University of Colombo's Zoological Museum, whose collections exceed 9,000 specimens. The museum traces its history back roughly a century and welcomes public visitors, offering guided educational tours that focus on natural history rather than entertainment. Its collections include marine invertebrates, insects, mammals, birds, reptiles and skeletal material used in teaching and research.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://museum.zoology.cmb.ac.lk/visit-us/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: museum.zoology.cmb.ac.lk">[museum.zoology.cmb.ac.lk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">museum.zoology.cmb.ac.lk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Visit Us – Zoological Museum of the University of Colombo</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Visit Us – Zoological Museum of the University of Colombo</span></span></span>

## Public access, ethics and the difference between education and spectacle

These collections differ fundamentally from commercial displays of preserved bodies or sensational "oddity" museums.

Human anatomical specimens at Peradeniya are displayed within a medical faculty under professional supervision. Access is controlled, and the university requires advance permission for public visits, reflecting the ethical responsibilities attached to human remains. The specimens exist because of medical education and anatomical donation, not because they were collected as curiosities.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://med.pdn.ac.lk/departments/anatomy/museum.php" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: med.pdn.ac.lk">[med.pdn.ac.lk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">med.pdn.ac.lk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on pdn.ac.lk.</span></span></span>

The zoological museums follow similar principles. Although visitors can see preserved animals at close range, the specimens support scientific identification, biodiversity research and teaching. Many have been obtained through authorised scientific work, natural deaths, research collections or official donations rather than being acquired for display alone. Museum staff continue to catalogue, conserve and expand the collections as part of ongoing academic programmes.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://science.sjp.ac.lk/zoo/services-and-facilities/museum/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: science.sjp.ac.lk">[science.sjp.ac.lk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">science.sjp.ac.lk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Museum – Department of Zoology</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Museum – Department of Zoology</span></span></span>

For visitors accustomed to commercial "body worlds" exhibitions or private cabinets of curiosities, Sri Lanka's university museums therefore feel quite different. They are quieter, more restrained and strongly contextualised. Labels focus on anatomy, evolution, ecology and conservation rather than on provoking emotional reactions.

Photography policies also reflect this educational approach. The University of Sri Jayewardenepura's Zoological Museum permits non-commercial photography without flash, while the University of Colombo's Zoological Museum similarly allows photography under specified conditions. The anatomy museum, however, places greater emphasis on controlled access because of the sensitive nature of its human collections.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://science.sjp.ac.lk/zoologymuseum/visit-us/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: science.sjp.ac.lk">[sjp.ac.lk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">science.sjp.ac.lk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">VISI T US – Zoology Museum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">VISI T US – Zoology Museum</span></span></span>

## Why these collections matter within Sri Lanka's curiosity landscape

Sri Lanka has relatively few institutions dedicated solely to medical history or natural-history curiosities. Instead, universities have become custodians of some of the country's most remarkable preserved collections.

These museums broaden the idea of what counts as a "strange" attraction. Their fascination comes not from folklore or mystery but from direct encounters with real anatomy and biodiversity. Human organs prepared for teaching, complete animal skeletons, preserved reptiles, embryological specimens and comparative anatomical displays reveal aspects of life that most people rarely see.

Placed alongside Sri Lanka's ritual mask museums, folk collections and religious displays, these academic museums demonstrate another strand of the country's curiosity culture: one grounded in scientific observation, careful preservation and public education. They remind visitors that collections of bodies and bones can inspire wonder without sacrificing accuracy, dignity or ethical responsibility.

    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
</script>
