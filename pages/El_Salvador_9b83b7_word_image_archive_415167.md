## Introduction

The Museum of the Word and Image (MUPI) in San Salvador is unusual not because it displays bizarre objects, but because it rescues voices that were once meant to disappear. Hidden radio broadcasts, handwritten letters, photographs, refugee embroidery, paintings and personal testimonies have been transformed into a public archive that challenges official silence. Rather than collecting curiosities for their own sake, the museum preserves evidence of lives lived in secrecy, exile and conflict, making fragile memories into tangible museum objects. Within [El Salvador]({{ 'where-el-salvador-keeps-its-strangest/' | relative_url }})'s landscape of unusual collections, MUPI stands out as a counter-archive: a place where material once concealed for survival now forms one of the country's richest public records of cultural memory.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://museo.com.sv/?product-page=1" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: museo.com.sv">[museo.com.sv]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">museo.com.sv</span><span class="citation-popover-title">MUP I – Museo de la palabra y la imagen</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">MUPI – Museo de la palabra y la imagenJuly 15, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 15, 2026</span></span></span>

## Radio Venceremos and the recovered counter-archive

The heart of MUPI grew directly from the archive of Radio Venceremos, the clandestine broadcaster that transmitted from guerrilla-held territory during the Salvadoran Civil War. Founded by journalist Carlos Henríquez Consalvi, known by the nom de guerre "Santiago", the station documented military events, reported civilian experiences and challenged government accounts while constantly moving equipment to avoid detection. After the 1992 peace accords, instead of allowing these recordings to scatter into private collections or disappear altogether, Consalvi used them as the foundation for a new museum dedicated to preserving historical memory.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio_Venceremos" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[wikipedia.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Radio Venceremos</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Radio Venceremos</span></span></span>

What visitors encounter is not simply an exhibition about a radio station. Original recording equipment, photographs of broadcasters working under wartime conditions, audio recordings and documentary material demonstrate how information itself became a contested resource. Listening stations and reconstructed broadcasting spaces make clear that these broadcasts were produced under extraordinary circumstances, often from mountain camps that could be abandoned within minutes if discovered.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio_Venceremos" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Radio Venceremos</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Radio Venceremos</span></span></span>

The archive's importance extends beyond military history. Universities and research institutions have partnered with MUPI to digitise the Radio Venceremos recordings because they preserve voices, accents, music, humour and eyewitness testimony that rarely appear in official documents. The broadcasts are now used by historians, linguists and human-rights researchers as primary evidence of everyday life during the conflict rather than merely as political propaganda.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://curriculum.llilasbenson.utexas.edu/sources/sources-mupi-radio-venceremos/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: curriculum.llilasbenson.utexas.edu">[utexas.edu]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">curriculum.llilasbenson.utexas.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Radio Venceremos Audio Collection (Primary Sources) – Curriculum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Radio Venceremos Audio Collection (Primary Sources) – Curriculum</span></span></span>

## Salarrué's spiritual art and invented worlds

The museum's collections move far beyond war. One of its most distinctive archives centres on the writer and artist Salvador Salazar Arrué, better known as Salarrué. While celebrated for his literature, he also produced an extensive body of paintings inspired by spiritual philosophy, visionary symbolism and imagined landscapes that occupy an unusual place within Salvadoran art.

Rather than presenting only finished artworks, MUPI preserves Salarrué's wider creative world. Manuscripts, unpublished writings, correspondence, personal objects, sketchbooks and a substantial personal library reveal how literature, painting and spiritual thought informed one another. The archive contains over a thousand letters alongside original manuscripts and artworks, allowing visitors to trace creative processes rather than simply admire completed pieces. UNESCO's Latin American Memory of the World programme has recognised the significance of this archive as documentary heritage.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://museo.com.sv/salarrue-2/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: museo.com.sv">[museo.com.sv]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">museo.com.sv</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Salarrué – MUPI</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Salarrué – MUPI</span></span></span>

This makes the collection especially unusual within El Salvador's museum landscape. Salarrué's paintings often depict mystical or dreamlike environments that resist straightforward interpretation. Displayed alongside his notebooks and correspondence, they become evidence of an individual intellectual universe rather than isolated works of art. The museum therefore treats imagination itself as an archival subject worthy of preservation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://museo.com.sv/salarrue-2/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: museo.com.sv">[museo.com.sv]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">museo.com.sv</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Salarrué – MUPI</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Salarrué – MUPI</span></span></span>

## Embroidery, exile and testimony as museum objects

Perhaps the museum's most unexpected collections are its embroidered testimonies created by Salvadoran women living in refugee camps during the civil war. Instead of written memoirs, these textile works record villages, displacement, violence, daily routines and hopes for return through stitched images and carefully chosen colours.

Seen in a gallery, the embroideries resemble folk art. Read alongside accompanying oral histories, however, they become documentary evidence. They preserve memories of forced migration and survival in forms that many participants found safer, more accessible or more culturally meaningful than written testimony. The museum has treated these textiles not as decorative craft but as historical documents carrying first-person witness accounts.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.lib.utexas.edu/about/news/embroidered-testimonies-salvadoran-civil-war-refugees-accessible-online" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lib.utexas.edu">[utexas.edu]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lib.utexas.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">September 26, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 26, 2019</span></span></span>

Digital collaborations have expanded access to this material without separating it from its context. Joint projects with international academic partners have photographed, catalogued and presented the embroidered works alongside interviews and historical information, allowing viewers to understand both the artistic techniques and the experiences encoded within each piece.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.lib.utexas.edu/about/news/embroidered-testimonies-salvadoran-civil-war-refugees-accessible-online" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lib.utexas.edu">[lib.utexas.edu]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lib.utexas.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">September 26, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 26, 2019</span></span></span>

The same philosophy shapes other collections throughout MUPI. Photographs of indigenous communities, posters from international solidarity campaigns, personal letters, manuscripts, family photographs and everyday objects are interpreted as evidence of social movements and individual lives rather than simply as historical illustrations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Museo_de_la_Palabra_y_la_Imagen" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Museo de la Palabra y la Imagen</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Museo de la Palabra y la Imagen</span></span></span>

## Why these rescued voices matter


<div class="content-callout content-callout--key content-callout--takeaway" markdown="1">

Unlike conventional history museums that rely heavily on official government records, MUPI deliberately preserves material created outside state institutions or hidden from them. Many of its most important objects survived because individuals protected them in exile, carried them across borders or concealed them during years of conflict. The museum's long-running campaign against what it calls the "chaos of forgetting" continues to encourage members of the public to donate or lend documents, photographs and recordings before they are lost.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://museo.com.sv/colabora/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: museo.com.sv">[museo.com.sv]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">museo.com.sv</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Colabora – MUPI</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Colabora – MUPI</span></span></span>

</div>

This approach gives the museum a distinctive place within El Salvador's culture of unusual collections. Visitors do not come to see spectacular relics or mysterious artefacts. Instead, they encounter rescued voices: broadcasts that once travelled secretly across mountains, paintings born from spiritual imagination, and embroidered cloth that quietly records experiences of exile and survival. Together, these collections demonstrate that memory itself can become one of a country's most remarkable museum objects.

    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
</script>
