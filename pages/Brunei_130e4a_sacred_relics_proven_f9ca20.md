## Introduction

[Brunei]({{ 'where-brunei-keeps-its-strangest/' | relative_url }})'s most unusual public collection is not a cabinet of curiosities but a carefully curated display of Islamic relics whose significance rests as much on faith and tradition as on historical documentation. The Sultan Haji Hassanal Bolkiah Islamic Treasury Hall (Balai Khazanah Islam Sultan Haji Hassanal Bolkiah) brings together Qur'anic manuscripts, pilgrimage textiles, historic Islamic objects and a gallery centred on items presented as being associated with the Prophet Muhammad.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.bkishhb-en.gov.bn/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bkishhb-en.gov.bn">[Government of Brunei Darussalam]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bkishhb-en.gov.bn</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ernment of Brunei DarussalamBKISHHB &#124; Balai Khazanah Islam Sultan Haji Hassanal Bolkiah, Prime Minister&#x27;s OfficeJuly 11, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 11, 2026</span></span></span>

For visitors interested in unusual collections, the key question is not whether every object can be scientifically authenticated. Instead, it is how a modern museum presents sacred objects whose religious importance often exceeds what conventional provenance research can prove. The Treasury Hall deliberately combines conservation, scholarship and devotional heritage, making it one of Brunei's most distinctive cultural institutions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.bkishhb-en.gov.bn/about-us/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bkishhb-en.gov.bn">[Government of Brunei Darussalam]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bkishhb-en.gov.bn</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ernment of Brunei Darussalam About Us &#124; BKISHHB</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ernment of Brunei DarussalamAbout Us &#124; BKISHHB...</span></span></span>

## Manuscripts, textiles and pilgrimage objects

Much of the Treasury Hall resembles a traditional museum of Islamic art and manuscript culture rather than a reliquary. Its galleries contain historic Qur'an manuscripts from different regions, Islamic coins, calligraphy, scholarly manuscripts in Arabic, Persian and Malay (Jawi), and textiles connected with the pilgrimage to Mecca.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.bkishhb-en.gov.bn/1299-2/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bkishhb-en.gov.bn">[Government of Brunei Darussalam]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bkishhb-en.gov.bn</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ernment of Brunei Darussalam Explore English &#124; BKISHHB</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ernment of Brunei DarussalamExplore English &#124; BKISHHB...</span></span></span>

Among the better documented objects are:

* Manuscripts illustrating centuries of Islamic scholarship across the wider Muslim world.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.bkishhb-en.gov.bn/gallery-1/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bkishhb-en.gov.bn">[bkishhb-en.gov.bn]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bkishhb-en.gov.bn</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ernment of Brunei Darussalam Gallery 1 &#124; BKISHHB</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ernment of Brunei DarussalamGallery 1 &#124; BKISHHB...</span></span></span>

## The relic gallery and claims of sacred association

The Treasury Hall's most distinctive space is Gallery 3, dedicated to what the museum describes as the Noble Hair of the Prophet Muhammad. According to the official gallery description, the display includes a hair presented as belonging to the Prophet alongside other objects associated with early Islamic history, including a fragment of the Prophet's cloak (*burda*), replicas of his sword, footprints, slippers and walking staff.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.bkishhb-en.gov.bn/gallery-3/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bkishhb-en.gov.bn">[Government of Brunei Darussalam]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bkishhb-en.gov.bn</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ernment of Brunei Darussalam Gallery 3 &#124; BKISHHB</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ernment of Brunei DarussalamGallery 3 &#124; BKISHHB...</span></span></span>

The museum itself is careful in how it frames these objects. Some are presented as relics, while others are explicitly identified as replicas. This distinction matters because visitors may otherwise assume that every object on display is an original artefact from the seventh century.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.bkishhb-en.gov.bn/gallery-3/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bkishhb-en.gov.bn">[Government of Brunei Darussalam]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bkishhb-en.gov.bn</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ernment of Brunei Darussalam Gallery 3 &#124; BKISHHB</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ernment of Brunei DarussalamGallery 3 &#124; BKISHHB...</span></span></span>

The Noble Hair receives special treatment within the institution. At various times it has been displayed only during particular public viewing periods, attracting large visitor numbers and functioning as both a museum exhibit and an object of religious reverence. The official museum continues to announce dedicated opening periods for the gallery.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.bkishhb-en.gov.bn/event/the-opening-of-the-noble-hair-of-the-prophet-%EF%B7%BA-gallery/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bkishhb-en.gov.bn">[Government of Brunei Darussalam]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bkishhb-en.gov.bn</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ernment of Brunei DarussalamThe Opening of the Noble Hair of the Prophet ﷺ Gallery &#124; BKISHHB...</span></span></span>

## Belief, provenance and careful museum language

Understanding the collection requires recognising that religious provenance differs from archaeological provenance.

For archaeological finds, provenance normally means a documented chain showing where an object was discovered, how it was excavated and how it entered a collection. Sacred relics often follow a different tradition. Their significance may rest on centuries of custodianship, religious testimony or inherited belief rather than uninterrupted documentary records that satisfy modern historical standards.

The Treasury Hall generally avoids presenting every claim as scientifically verified fact. Instead, its language emphasises religious heritage, preservation and Islamic civilisation while identifying some accompanying objects as reproductions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.bkishhb-en.gov.bn/gallery-3/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bkishhb-en.gov.bn">[Government of Brunei Darussalam]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bkishhb-en.gov.bn</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ernment of Brunei Darussalam Gallery 3 &#124; BKISHHB</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ernment of Brunei DarussalamGallery 3 &#124; BKISHHB...</span></span></span>

For historians, questions naturally arise about the documented chain of custody for relics associated with the Prophet Muhammad. Such questions are not unique to Brunei. Across the Islamic world, objects attributed to the Prophet—including hairs, cloaks, swords and personal belongings—are preserved in several institutions, and historians often note that complete documentary provenance extending back to the seventh century is exceptionally difficult to establish. Museums therefore distinguish between devotional tradition and what can be demonstrated through surviving historical evidence.

For believers, however, the value of these relics is not necessarily dependent upon modern forensic verification. Their importance lies in their role within Islamic memory, reverence and cultural continuity. The museum reflects this perspective by presenting the relics within a broader narrative of Islamic scholarship and heritage rather than as archaeological discoveries requiring laboratory authentication.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.bkishhb-en.gov.bn/about-us/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bkishhb-en.gov.bn">[Government of Brunei Darussalam]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bkishhb-en.gov.bn</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ernment of Brunei Darussalam About Us &#124; BKISHHB</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ernment of Brunei DarussalamAbout Us &#124; BKISHHB...</span></span></span>

    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
</script>
</section>
