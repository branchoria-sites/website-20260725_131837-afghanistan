## Introduction

The EFKS Museum at Malua is one of [Samoa]({{ 'where-samoa-keeps-its-strangest-stories/' | relative_url }})’s most distinctive religious collections because it presents missionary history through the living faith of the Congregational Christian Church of Samoa (EFKS). Rather than separating archaeology, folklore and religion into different galleries, the museum deliberately weaves them together. Visitors encounter missionary artefacts, church history, contemporary Samoan art and a powerful narrative that links the arrival of Christianity in 1830 with an older tradition: the prophecy attributed to the warrior goddess Nafanua, which church tradition interprets as foretelling the coming of the Christian faith.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.samoa.travel/plan-book/activities/efks-museum" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: samoa.travel">[samoa.travel]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">samoa.travel</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Travel EFKS Museum &#124; Samoa Culture and Tradition &#124; Pacific Island</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EFKS Museum &#124; Samoa Culture and Tradition &#124; Pacific Island...</span></span></span>

Instead, it is a church-operated institution that preserves historical objects while openly interpreting them through EFKS theology and collective memory. Understanding that dual role helps visitors appreciate both what the museum displays and how it explains Samoa's religious past.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.samoa.travel/plan-book/activities/efks-museum" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: samoa.travel">[samoa.travel]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">samoa.travel</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Travel EFKS Museum &#124; Samoa Culture and Tradition &#124; Pacific Island</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EFKS Museum &#124; Samoa Culture and Tradition &#124; Pacific Island...</span></span></span>

## Why the museum resembles a ship's hull

The museum's architecture immediately signals that visitors are entering a story rather than merely a building. Its most striking feature is a section designed to resemble the hull of the ship associated with the arrival of the London Missionary Society missionary John Williams. This symbolic vessel faces the coast, visually connecting the museum to the historic sea routes by which Christianity reached Samoa.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.samoa.travel/plan-book/activities/efks-museum" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: samoa.travel">[Samoa Travel]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">samoa.travel</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Travel EFKS Museum &#124; Samoa Culture and Tradition &#124; Pacific Island</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EFKS Museum &#124; Samoa Culture and Tradition &#124; Pacific Island...</span></span></span>

The surrounding design reinforces this narrative. Covered walkways incorporate traditional Samoan forms and carvings, while statues and decorative panels reference ancestral stories. The result is an architectural dialogue between pre-Christian Samoa and Christian Samoa rather than an attempt to erase one in favour of the other. The museum therefore presents conversion as a continuation of Samoan history instead of a complete break with it.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cccs.org.ws/index.php/efks-museum/129-efks-museum-about-us" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cccs.org.ws">[CCCS]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cccs.org.ws</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EFKS Museum - About us...</span></span></span>

At the entrance stands a statue of Nafanua, one of Samoa's best-known legendary figures. According to church tradition, she foretold that a new religion would one day arrive and replace the old order. The museum positions this prophecy as the opening chapter of the Christian story that unfolds inside the galleries. Official EFKS descriptions explicitly identify John Williams' arrival at Sapapali'i in 1830 as the fulfilment of that prophecy.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.samoa.travel/plan-book/activities/efks-museum" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: samoa.travel">[samoa.travel]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">samoa.travel</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Travel EFKS Museum &#124; Samoa Culture and Tradition &#124; Pacific Island</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EFKS Museum &#124; Samoa Culture and Tradition &#124; Pacific Island...</span></span></span>

## John Williams and the memory of conversion

The museum is devoted less to the biography of John Williams than to what his arrival represents in Samoan church memory. Williams appears as the missionary whose voyage marks the beginning of a national religious transformation, while the emphasis remains firmly on the response of Samoan communities that adopted, adapted and spread Christianity across the islands.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cccs.org.ws/index.php/news-events/117-from-samoan-church-london-missionary-society-lms-to-congregational-christian-church-samoa-cccs" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cccs.org.ws">[CCCS]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cccs.org.ws</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">FROM SAMOAN CHURCH (LONDON MISSIONARY SOCIETY-LMS) TO CONGREGATIONAL CHRISTIAN CHURCH SAMOA (CCCS)...</span></span></span>

Displays associated with the early mission include missionary-era artefacts, church records, historical objects and artworks alongside more recent cultural material. Rather than presenting missionaries as isolated heroes, the museum places them within the wider institutional history of the EFKS and the Malua theological community, which became one of the Pacific's most influential centres for training indigenous ministers and missionaries.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.lonelyplanet.com/points-of-interest/efks-museum/1529290" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lonelyplanet.com">[lonelyplanet.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lonelyplanet.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Lonely Planet EFKS Museum &#124; &#x27;Upolu, Samoa &#124; Attractions</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lonely PlanetEFKS Museum &#124; &#x27;Upolu, Samoa &#124; Attractions - Lonely Planet...</span></span></span>

This perspective reflects the EFKS understanding of its own history. The church traces its origins to the London Missionary Society while emphasising the development of a self-governing Samoan church that eventually became independent of direct missionary control. The museum therefore commemorates both foreign missionaries and the generations of Samoan ministers, teachers and congregations who shaped Christianity into a distinctly Samoan institution.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cccs.org.ws/index.php/news-events/117-from-samoan-church-london-missionary-society-lms-to-congregational-christian-church-samoa-cccs" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cccs.org.ws">[CCCS]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cccs.org.ws</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">FROM SAMOAN CHURCH (LONDON MISSIONARY SOCIETY-LMS) TO CONGREGATIONAL CHRISTIAN CHURCH SAMOA (CCCS)...</span></span></span>

## Faith tradition, folklore and historical evidence

The museum's most intriguing feature is the way it presents prophecy alongside documented history. Historians can verify the arrival of John Williams in 1830 and the rapid expansion of Christianity through missionary records, Samoan accounts and church archives. The prophecy of Nafanua, however, belongs to oral tradition rather than documentary history. It survives through stories recorded after Christian conversion and has been interpreted in different ways by scholars and believers.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://tmatic.travel/en/view/story/john-williams-memorial_ddIxa5o/en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: tmatic.travel">[Tmatic]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">tmatic.travel</span><span class="citation-popover-title">John Williams Memorial – Audio guide by Museum of Sāmoa &#124; tmatic.travel</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">John Williams Memorial – Audio guide by Museum of Sāmoa &#124; tmatic.travel...</span></span></span>

For the EFKS, this distinction is not a contradiction. The prophecy functions as part of a theological interpretation of history rather than as a claim that can be tested using the same methods as shipping logs or missionary correspondence. Within the museum, folklore becomes evidence of divine preparation, while historical documents demonstrate how that perceived fulfilment unfolded in practice.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cccs.org.ws/index.php/efks-museum/129-efks-museum-about-us" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cccs.org.ws">[CCCS]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cccs.org.ws</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EFKS Museum - About us...</span></span></span>

Academic researchers generally approach the relationship more cautiously. Many note that traditions concerning Nafanua were preserved and reshaped within Christian Samoa, making it difficult to reconstruct exactly how the prophecy was understood before European missionaries arrived. The museum does not dwell on those historiographical debates, preferring instead to present the narrative accepted within the EFKS faith community.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://repository.divinity.edu.au/divinityserver/api/core/bitstreams/46d65d53-46ce-4dcf-ab65-94786a473c6e/content" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: repository.divinity.edu.au">[repository.divinity.edu.au]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">repository.divinity.edu.au</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Microsoft Word</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">COMBINED FIRST PAGES...</span></span></span>

For visitors, this means the collection should be read on two levels. It preserves genuine historical material connected with nineteenth-century missions and the development of one of the Pacific's most influential Protestant churches. At the same time, it is a devotional interpretation of national history in which prophecy, memory and documentary evidence are intentionally brought together to express the church's understanding of Samoa's conversion.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.samoa.travel/plan-book/activities/efks-museum" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: samoa.travel">[samoa.travel]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">samoa.travel</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Travel EFKS Museum &#124; Samoa Culture and Tradition &#124; Pacific Island</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EFKS Museum &#124; Samoa Culture and Tradition &#124; Pacific Island...</span></span></span>

      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
