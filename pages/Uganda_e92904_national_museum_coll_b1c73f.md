## Introduction

The [Uganda]({{ 'where-ugandas-strangest-collections/' | relative_url }}) National Museum is the country’s closest equivalent to a traditional “cabinet of curiosities”: not because it was created to display the bizarre, but because it brings together objects from very different worlds — ancient tools, fossils, royal regalia, musical instruments, wildlife specimens, photographs, and archives. For visitors interested in strange history, the museum’s appeal lies in the way ordinary objects become unusual when placed in historical context: a drum can represent royal authority, a stone tool can connect to human origins, and a ritual object can reveal changing ideas about belief and power.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://museums.tourism.go.ug/about-us/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: museums.tourism.go.ug">[Uganda Museums]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">museums.tourism.go.ug</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Uganda Museums About Us</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Uganda MuseumsAbout Us - Uganda Museums...</span></span></span>

Founded in 1908, the museum developed from a colonial-era collection into Uganda’s national heritage institution. Today it holds more than 200,000 artefacts, specimens, and archival records covering archaeology, palaeontology, ethnography, natural history, and historical documentation. Its collections do not function as a “museum of oddities” in the sensational sense; instead, they preserve the unusual intersections between science, memory, spirituality, politics, and everyday life in Uganda.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://museums.tourism.go.ug/about-us/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: museums.tourism.go.ug">[Uganda Museums]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">museums.tourism.go.ug</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Uganda Museums About Us</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Uganda MuseumsAbout Us - Uganda Museums...</span></span></span>

## From colonial collection to national archive

The Uganda National Museum’s history reflects a wider transformation in African museums: from institutions shaped by colonial collecting practices into places where nations interpret their own histories. The museum began with a small collection associated with early colonial administration and later developed into a research and public education institution. Its permanent building on Kira Road opened in the 1950s, becoming the home of galleries covering ethnography, history, archaeology, natural history, musical instruments, and temporary exhibitions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://museums.tourism.go.ug/about-us/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: museums.tourism.go.ug">[Uganda Museums]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">museums.tourism.go.ug</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Uganda Museums About Us</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Uganda MuseumsAbout Us - Uganda Museums...</span></span></span>

One of the most distinctive parts of the museum’s history is its local nickname, **Enyumba y'Amayembe**, often translated as the “House of Fetishes”. The name emerged because visitors associated the museum with displays of spiritually significant objects and ritual materials. Uganda Museums notes that the nickname became part of the institution’s identity and was even believed to have helped protect the museum during periods of political instability and looting.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://museums.tourism.go.ug/about-us/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: museums.tourism.go.ug">[Uganda Museums]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">museums.tourism.go.ug</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Uganda Museums About Us</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Uganda MuseumsAbout Us - Uganda Museums...</span></span></span>

The phrase also reveals the changing language of museums. Colonial collectors often used terms such as “fetish” to describe African religious objects, but modern interpretation generally treats these items as evidence of complex systems of belief, craftsmanship, authority, and social organisation rather than as curiosities or magical objects. The museum’s collections therefore preserve not only objects themselves but also the history of how those objects have been viewed and interpreted.

The institution also sits within ongoing global debates about colonial-era collections. Questions about ownership, provenance, and the movement of cultural objects have affected museums worldwide, including African collections held abroad. Uganda has participated in restitution discussions, including the return of 39 traditional artefacts from the University of Cambridge in 2023, showing how museum collections continue to be connected to questions of memory and cultural authority.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://apnews.com/article/b5e2eb50e56477872fd2afff8c0a3055" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: apnews.com">[AP News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">apnews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The items, including tribal regalia and pottery, were handed over to Ugandan officials on Saturday and will be on loan for an initial per...</span></span></span>

## Royal objects, archaeology, and natural specimens

### The objects that make the museum unusual

The Uganda National Museum’s strongest curiosity appeal comes from the variety of objects displayed together. A visitor can encounter evidence of prehistoric human activity, objects linked to kingdoms, examples of traditional technology, and specimens from Uganda’s extraordinary biodiversity within the same institution.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://museums.tourism.go.ug/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: museums.tourism.go.ug">[Uganda Museums]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">museums.tourism.go.ug</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Uganda Museums Home</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Uganda MuseumsHome - Uganda Museums...</span></span></span>

**Royal collections and ceremonial objects** are among the museum’s most distinctive holdings. The Ethnology and Cultural Heritage Collection includes royal regalia such as drums, stools, spears, ceremonial garments, crafts, and musical instruments. Some objects remain connected to living cultural traditions, including community stewardship arrangements involving Buganda Palace.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://museums.tourism.go.ug/collections/ethnology-cultural-heritage-collection/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: museums.tourism.go.ug">[Uganda Museums]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">museums.tourism.go.ug</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Uganda Museums Ethnology &amp; Cultural Heritage Collection</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Uganda MuseumsEthnology &amp; Cultural Heritage Collection - Uganda Museums...</span></span></span>

A particularly memorable example is the Buganda royal drum, including the long ceremonial drum known as the **Engalabi**. Such objects are not simply decorative artefacts; they represent political authority, ceremony, and the historical organisation of kingdoms. A drum in this setting becomes a record of power and identity rather than merely a musical instrument.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://museums.tourism.go.ug/collections/ethnology-cultural-heritage-collection/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: museums.tourism.go.ug">[Uganda Museums]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">museums.tourism.go.ug</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Uganda Museums Ethnology &amp; Cultural Heritage Collection</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Uganda MuseumsEthnology &amp; Cultural Heritage Collection - Uganda Museums...</span></span></span>

**Musical instruments** provide another unusual window into Uganda’s cultural diversity. The museum highlights more than 200 traditional instruments representing more than 30 ethnic groups, including instruments used for performance, storytelling, ceremony, and social communication.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://museums.tourism.go.ug/plan-your-visit/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: museums.tourism.go.ug">[Uganda Museums]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">museums.tourism.go.ug</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Uganda Museums Plan Your Visit</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Uganda MuseumsPlan Your Visit - Uganda Museums...</span></span></span> These objects are especially valuable because they show how sound and craftsmanship become forms of historical evidence.

**Archaeological and palaeontological collections** move the museum’s story far beyond recent human history. The archaeology and palaeontology holdings include around 4,200 objects, including fossils, stone tools, and pottery fragments covering periods from deep prehistory to later archaeological eras.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://museums.tourism.go.ug/collections/archaeological-palaeontology-collection/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: museums.tourism.go.ug">[Uganda Museums]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">museums.tourism.go.ug</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Uganda Museums Archaeological &amp; Palaeontology Collection</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Uganda MuseumsArchaeological &amp; Palaeontology Collection - Uganda Museums...</span></span></span> One example is an Acheulean hand axe from the Lake Victoria region, a reminder that Uganda’s landscape has been part of the story of early human technology for hundreds of thousands of years.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://museums.tourism.go.ug/collections/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: museums.tourism.go.ug">[Uganda Museums]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">museums.tourism.go.ug</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Uganda Museums Collections Archive</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Uganda MuseumsCollections Archive - Uganda Museums...</span></span></span>

The **natural history collections** add another layer to the museum’s curiosity character. Preserved specimens, geological materials, and displays of Uganda’s wildlife connect the country’s cultural history with its ecological history. The museum presents natural objects not as isolated scientific samples but as part of a broader story about landscapes, species, and human relationships with nature.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://museums.tourism.go.ug/plan-your-visit/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: museums.tourism.go.ug">[Uganda Museums]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">museums.tourism.go.ug</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Uganda Museums Plan Your Visit</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Uganda MuseumsPlan Your Visit - Uganda Museums...</span></span></span>

## Why the museum feels like a curiosity cabinet

Traditional European curiosity cabinets often brought together rare, scientific, artistic, and mysterious objects in a single collection. The Uganda National Museum creates a similar feeling, but for different reasons. Its “curiosities” are not chosen because they are strange in isolation; they become fascinating because each object carries multiple meanings.

A royal drum can be studied as an object of craftsmanship, a symbol of authority, and a piece of political history. A fossil can be examined as a scientific specimen and as evidence of Uganda’s place in debates about human evolution. A ritual object can reveal beliefs, social structures, and the changing relationship between communities and museums.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://museums.tourism.go.ug/collections/ethnology-cultural-heritage-collection/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: museums.tourism.go.ug">[Uganda Museums]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">museums.tourism.go.ug</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Uganda Museums Ethnology &amp; Cultural Heritage Collection</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Uganda MuseumsEthnology &amp; Cultural Heritage Collection - Uganda Museums...</span></span></span>

This mixture makes the museum particularly interesting for visitors seeking unusual heritage rather than conventional tourist attractions. It is a place where scientific collections and cultural memory sit side by side. The result is less a collection of “oddities” and more a record of how people have explained the world around them.

    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
</script>
