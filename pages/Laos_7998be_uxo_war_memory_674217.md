## Introduction

The UXO museums and war memory collections of Laos are unusual because they do not preserve a distant conflict that ended neatly in the past. They document a danger that has remained part of everyday life for generations. Collections of bomb casings, unexploded ordnance (UXO), photographs, survivor accounts and rehabilitation stories show how the remains of war became objects of memory, education and recovery.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uxolao.gov.la/the-uxo-problem/uxo-lao-visitor-center/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uxolao.gov.la">[uxolao.gov.la]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uxolao.gov.la</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UX O Lao Visitor Center</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UXO Lao Visitor Center - Lao National Unexploded Ordnance Programme (UXO LAO)October 31, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 31, 2025</span></span></span>

Rather than displaying weapons as military trophies, Laos’s UXO museums generally treat them as evidence of human consequences. The focus is not only on what was dropped during the Second Indochina War, but on what happened afterwards: communities living with contaminated land, families affected by accidents, clearance teams removing explosives, and organisations helping survivors rebuild their lives.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.maginternational.org/laos-visitor-centres/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: maginternational.org">[maginternational.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">maginternational.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Laos Visitor Centres &#124; MAG</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Laos Visitor Centres &#124; MAG</span></span></span>

The best-known examples include the COPE Visitor Centre in Vientiane, the UXO Lao Visitor Center, and visitor centres operated by Mines Advisory Group (MAG). Together, these collections form one of Laos’s most distinctive forms of modern heritage: museums where the strange and unsettling objects on display are not curiosities for entertainment, but reminders of survival.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://copelaos.org/visit-us/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: copelaos.org">[COPE Laos]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">copelaos.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">COPE Laos Visit Us – COPE Laos</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">COPE Laos Visit Us – COPE Laos</span></span></span>

## What visitors see in UXO collections

Unlike traditional war museums built around uniforms, battles and commanders, Laos’s UXO collections are centred on ordinary objects with extraordinary consequences. A small cluster munition, a damaged farming tool or a prosthetic limb can carry more meaning here than a large military exhibit because these objects connect directly to civilian experience.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://copelaos.org/visit-us/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: copelaos.org">[COPE Laos]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">copelaos.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">COPE Laos Visit Us – COPE Laos</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">COPE Laos Visit Us – COPE Laos</span></span></span>

### Bomb remnants turned into memory objects

The UXO Lao Visitor Center presents deactivated bomb remnants, photographs, survivor stories and documentary material explaining the continuing problem of unexploded ordnance. The centre was established to show war remnants and explain the clearance work carried out by the Lao National Unexploded Ordnance Programme.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uxolao.gov.la/the-uxo-problem/uxo-lao-visitor-center/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uxolao.gov.la">[uxolao.gov.la]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uxolao.gov.la</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UX O Lao Visitor Center</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UXO Lao Visitor Center - Lao National Unexploded Ordnance Programme (UXO LAO)October 31, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 31, 2025</span></span></span>

The displays are deliberately straightforward. Visitors encounter the physical remains of weapons alongside explanations of why they are still dangerous decades after the fighting stopped. The result is less like a conventional military museum and more like a public education centre about risk, responsibility and recovery.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uxolao.gov.la/the-uxo-problem/uxo-lao-visitor-center/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uxolao.gov.la">[uxolao.gov.la]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uxolao.gov.la</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UX O Lao Visitor Center</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UXO Lao Visitor Center - Lao National Unexploded Ordnance Programme (UXO LAO)October 31, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 31, 2025</span></span></span>

In Vientiane, the COPE Visitor Centre takes a different but connected approach. It links the history of UXO with medical rehabilitation, showing prosthetic devices and sharing personal stories from people whose lives were changed by accidents. Its exhibitions explain both the historical causes of contamination and the practical work needed to help survivors regain mobility and independence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://copelaos.org/visit-us/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: copelaos.org">[COPE Laos]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">copelaos.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">COPE Laos Visit Us – COPE Laos</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">COPE Laos Visit Us – COPE Laos</span></span></span>

### The museums are small, but the subject is enormous

One reason these collections stand out in Laos’s museum landscape is the contrast between their modest size and the scale of the history they represent. They are not grand national monuments filled with thousands of artefacts. Instead, they often rely on carefully chosen objects, maps, films and testimony.

MAG’s visitor centres in Vientiane and Xieng Khouang use interactive maps, community stories, examples of recovered ordnance and clearance equipment to explain how contamination affects farming, education and local development.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.maginternational.org/laos-visitor-centres/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: maginternational.org">[maginternational.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">maginternational.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Laos Visitor Centres &#124; MAG</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Laos Visitor Centres &#124; MAG</span></span></span>

This approach changes how visitors understand war remains. A bomb fragment is not presented simply as an object from the past; it becomes part of a continuing story about land use, poverty, safety and rebuilding.

## The human stories behind unexploded ordnance

The most important feature of Laos’s UXO collections is that they move attention away from the machinery of war and towards the people who live with its consequences.

During the conflict period from 1964 to 1973, Laos experienced extensive bombing campaigns, and unexploded ordnance remained after the war ended. Official and humanitarian organisations describe UXO contamination as a continuing obstacle to safety and development, particularly in rural areas where communities depend on farming land.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.maginternational.org/laos-visitor-centres/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: maginternational.org">[maginternational.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">maginternational.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Laos Visitor Centres &#124; MAG</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Laos Visitor Centres &#124; MAG</span></span></span>

The museums preserve this history through personal narratives. At COPE, survivor stories sit alongside information about prosthetics, rehabilitation and disability support. This creates a different kind of war collection: one where the central artefact is often a person’s experience rather than the weapon itself.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://copelaos.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: copelaos.org">[COPE Laos]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">copelaos.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on copelaos.org.</span></span></span>

### From accident stories to recovery stories

A common misunderstanding about UXO collections is that they are only memorials of suffering. In reality, many are designed around recovery as much as tragedy.

COPE’s exhibitions explain how rehabilitation services support people with mobility-related disabilities, including those affected by UXO accidents. The organisation’s wider work includes prosthetic and orthotic support, physiotherapy and other forms of assistance aimed at improving independence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://copelaos.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: copelaos.org">[COPE Laos]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">copelaos.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on copelaos.org.</span></span></span>

This gives the collections a distinctive emotional balance. They acknowledge the scale of harm without presenting affected communities only as victims. The message is that war remnants have caused lasting damage, but people and institutions have also developed ways to respond.

## Museums as memorials and education centres

Laos’s UXO museums occupy an unusual space between memorial, classroom and humanitarian information centre. They preserve history, but they also have a practical purpose: teaching visitors why unexploded weapons remain dangerous and why clearance work continues.

The UXO Lao Visitor Center describes itself as an educational centre where visitors can learn about unexploded ordnance left after the Second Indochina War and the work of UXO clearance teams.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uxolao.gov.la/the-uxo-problem/uxo-lao-visitor-center/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uxolao.gov.la">[uxolao.gov.la]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uxolao.gov.la</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UX O Lao Visitor Center</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UXO Lao Visitor Center - Lao National Unexploded Ordnance Programme (UXO LAO)October 31, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 31, 2025</span></span></span> Similarly, MAG’s visitor centres connect historical displays with explanations of modern clearance operations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.maginternational.org/laos-visitor-centres/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: maginternational.org">[maginternational.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">maginternational.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Laos Visitor Centres &#124; MAG</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Laos Visitor Centres &#124; MAG</span></span></span>

### Why these collections matter in Laos’s strange-history landscape


<div class="content-callout content-callout--key content-callout--takeaway" markdown="1">

Among unusual museums and collections, UXO displays are distinctive because the objects themselves are ordinary and familiar: metal fragments, bomb casings and damaged equipment. Their unusual quality comes from their meaning. They are everyday objects transformed by history into symbols of danger and resilience.

</div>

This makes them different from collections built around mystery, folklore or rare artefacts. The curiosity comes not from uncertainty about what an object is, but from understanding how a simple object can continue affecting communities decades after it was created.

The collections also raise difficult questions about how societies remember conflict. A military museum may preserve weapons as symbols of power, while UXO museums preserve them as warnings. Their curatorial choice is significant: the bomb is not the story’s hero; the people living with its consequences are.

    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
