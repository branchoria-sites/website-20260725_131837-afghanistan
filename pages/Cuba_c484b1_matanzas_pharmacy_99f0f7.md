## Introduction

The Pharmaceutical Museum of Matanzas is one of Cuba's most remarkable historic interiors and one of the country's most unusual heritage attractions. Rather than reconstructing the past with collected antiques, it preserves an entire nineteenth-century French-style pharmacy almost exactly as it functioned during more than eighty years of business. Visitors walk through original dispensaries, laboratories and storage rooms lined with bottles, ceramic jars, balances, handwritten prescription books and medicines that were once part of everyday healthcare.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/6746/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: whc.unesco.org">[UNESCO World Heritage Centre]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">whc.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">World Heritage Centre Triolet French Drugstore (Pharmacy Museum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UNESCO World Heritage CentreTriolet French Drugstore (Pharmacy Museum) - UNESCO World Heritage CentreMarch 12, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 12, 2024</span></span></span>

For anyone interested in unusual collections, the museum stands out because its greatest treasure is not a dramatic medical instrument but the survival of an entire working medical world. Furniture, equipment, archives and even many pharmaceutical products remain together in the building for which they were originally designed. That completeness makes it an exceptional document of Cuban scientific, commercial and social history rather than simply a museum of old medicine.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/6746/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: whc.unesco.org">[UNESCO World Heritage Centre]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">whc.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">World Heritage Centre Triolet French Drugstore (Pharmacy Museum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UNESCO World Heritage CentreTriolet French Drugstore (Pharmacy Museum) - UNESCO World Heritage CentreMarch 12, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 12, 2024</span></span></span>

## The French Drugstore and its surviving rooms

The museum occupies the former French Drugstore established in Matanzas on 1 January 1882 by Cuban pharmacist Juan Fermín de Figueroa and the French pharmacist Ernesto Triolet Lelievre. The pharmacy continued operating until January 1964, when it was transformed into what is widely recognised as the first pharmaceutical museum in Latin America.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/6746/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: whc.unesco.org">[UNESCO World Heritage Centre]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">whc.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">World Heritage Centre Triolet French Drugstore (Pharmacy Museum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UNESCO World Heritage CentreTriolet French Drugstore (Pharmacy Museum) - UNESCO World Heritage CentreMarch 12, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 12, 2024</span></span></span>

What makes the building unusual is that it was purpose-built as a pharmacy and remained in continuous use rather than being assembled later from scattered collections. The ground floor still contains the original sales area and dispensing spaces. Upper floors housed the family residence, administrative offices, laboratory and rooms where medicinal herbs were dried before use. This continuity allows visitors to understand how a successful late nineteenth- and early twentieth-century pharmacy actually functioned as a complete workplace.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/6746/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: whc.unesco.org">[UNESCO World Heritage Centre]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">whc.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">World Heritage Centre Triolet French Drugstore (Pharmacy Museum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UNESCO World Heritage CentreTriolet French Drugstore (Pharmacy Museum) - UNESCO World Heritage CentreMarch 12, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 12, 2024</span></span></span>

The interior feels less like a conventional museum than a business whose staff have simply stepped away. Long wooden cabinets, marble counters, glass-fronted shelves and labelled containers remain in place. The collection includes:

* French porcelain apothecary jars.
* Bohemian and Baccarat glass bottles.
* Mortars, balances, presses and distillation equipment.
* Original laboratory instruments.
* Imported medicines from Europe, North America and elsewhere.
* Medicines produced by Cuban pharmaceutical laboratories.
* Original packaging, labels and commercial displays.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/6746/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: whc.unesco.org">[UNESCO World Heritage Centre]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">whc.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">World Heritage Centre Triolet French Drugstore (Pharmacy Museum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UNESCO World Heritage CentreTriolet French Drugstore (Pharmacy Museum) - UNESCO World Heritage CentreMarch 12, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 12, 2024</span></span></span>

## Prescription books, formulas and obsolete medicines

The museum's internationally significant collection is its archive of prescription and formula books rather than its shelves of medicines alone.

The surviving "Recipe Settlement Books" contain sixty-one manuscript volumes documenting more than half a million prescriptions prepared between 1873 and 1964 in three historic Matanzas pharmacies: San Rafael, Nuestra Señora de Regla and La Francesa. Each entry typically records the prescribing doctor, ingredients, preparation, date and price, with some also identifying the patient or institution receiving the medicine. Together they provide an unusually detailed record of everyday medical practice over nearly a century.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/27-new-inscriptions-unescos-memory-world-regional-register-latin-america-and-caribbean?hub=66924" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: unesco.org">[UNESCO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">27 new inscriptions on UNESCO´s Memory of the World Regional Register</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">27 new inscriptions on UNESCO´s Memory of the World Regional Register...</span></span></span>

These records reveal much more than changing drug formulas. They document:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* How physicians diagnosed and treated common illnesses.
* The transition from traditional botanical remedies towards more industrial pharmaceuticals.
* Commercial links between Cuba and European, American and other pharmaceutical suppliers.
* The economics of healthcare through recorded prices.
* The relationship between pharmacists and doctors in everyday Cuban society.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://media.unesco.org/sites/default/files/webform/mtd001/249_2024.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: media.unesco.org">[UNESCO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">media.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Formulario de postulación al Registro Memoria del</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Formulario de postulación al Registro Memoria del...</span></span></span>

## Medical curiosities that reward close attention

Although the museum is grounded firmly in documented history rather than medical spectacle, many individual objects have genuine curiosity value.

Shelves display ingredients that have disappeared from modern pharmacies, including preparations derived from plants, minerals and animals that reflected nineteenth-century pharmaceutical practice. Some substances once regarded as legitimate medicines now appear unusual or obsolete because scientific understanding has changed dramatically. The museum presents these items as historical evidence of evolving medical knowledge rather than as recommended treatments.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/6746/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: whc.unesco.org">[UNESCO World Heritage Centre]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">whc.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">World Heritage Centre Triolet French Drugstore (Pharmacy Museum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UNESCO World Heritage CentreTriolet French Drugstore (Pharmacy Museum) - UNESCO World Heritage CentreMarch 12, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 12, 2024</span></span></span>

Several objects also illuminate everyday urban life beyond medicine.

One is the city's earliest public telephone booth, installed outside the pharmacy and still preserved. Another is a medicine delivery bicycle dating from before 1890, complete with wooden wheels and solid tyres, used to deliver prescriptions to customers' homes. These details show how pharmacies operated as important community institutions rather than simply places to buy medicine.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/6746/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: whc.unesco.org">[UNESCO World Heritage Centre]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">whc.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">World Heritage Centre Triolet French Drugstore (Pharmacy Museum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UNESCO World Heritage CentreTriolet French Drugstore (Pharmacy Museum) - UNESCO World Heritage CentreMarch 12, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 12, 2024</span></span></span>

The museum also preserves an elaborate cabinet containing hundreds of beautifully hand-produced pharmaceutical labels. Their artistic quality reflects an era when packaging formed part of a pharmacy's professional identity, and many were created for products that won international recognition, including awards at the Paris Universal Exhibition of 1900.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/6746/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: whc.unesco.org">[UNESCO World Heritage Centre]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">whc.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">World Heritage Centre Triolet French Drugstore (Pharmacy Museum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UNESCO World Heritage CentreTriolet French Drugstore (Pharmacy Museum) - UNESCO World Heritage CentreMarch 12, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 12, 2024</span></span></span>

## Why the museum matters beyond medical history

Many historic pharmacies around the world display antique equipment. What distinguishes Matanzas is the exceptional completeness of its preservation.

UNESCO's nomination for World Heritage status argues that the building combines original architecture, furniture, documentation, laboratory equipment, products and business records in a way rarely found elsewhere. Rather than preserving isolated objects, it preserves the entire technological and commercial system of a nineteenth-century pharmacy.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/6746/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: whc.unesco.org">[UNESCO World Heritage Centre]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">whc.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">World Heritage Centre Triolet French Drugstore (Pharmacy Museum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UNESCO World Heritage CentreTriolet French Drugstore (Pharmacy Museum) - UNESCO World Heritage CentreMarch 12, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 12, 2024</span></span></span>

## What visitors can realistically expect today

Visitors should approach the museum as a preserved historic workplace rather than an interactive science centre. The appeal lies in authenticity and atmosphere rather than multimedia displays or dramatic demonstrations.

Many rooms remain densely furnished with original cabinets and equipment, creating the impression of stepping directly into a nineteenth-century pharmacy. Those interested in architecture, conservation and the history of medicine are likely to find more reward than visitors expecting theatrical displays or collections of surgical oddities.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/6746/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: whc.unesco.org">[UNESCO World Heritage Centre]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">whc.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">World Heritage Centre Triolet French Drugstore (Pharmacy Museum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UNESCO World Heritage CentreTriolet French Drugstore (Pharmacy Museum) - UNESCO World Heritage CentreMarch 12, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 12, 2024</span></span></span>

## A cornerstone of Cuba's unusual heritage

Within Cuba's landscape of unusual collections, the Pharmaceutical Museum of Matanzas occupies a distinctive place. Its fascination comes not from the macabre or mysterious but from the extraordinary survival of an entire professional world. Prescription ledgers, forgotten remedies, imported bottles, laboratory tools and original furnishings together preserve a complete picture of how medicine was prepared, prescribed and dispensed across generations.

For readers exploring Cuba's stranger and more distinctive heritage, the museum demonstrates that the country's most compelling curiosities are often found in places where everyday life has been preserved with exceptional fidelity. The result is less a cabinet of oddities than a rare historical time capsule of medicine, commerce and scientific practice.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/6746/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: whc.unesco.org">[UNESCO World Heritage Centre]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">whc.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">World Heritage Centre Triolet French Drugstore (Pharmacy Museum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UNESCO World Heritage CentreTriolet French Drugstore (Pharmacy Museum) - UNESCO World Heritage CentreMarch 12, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 12, 2024</span></span></span>
