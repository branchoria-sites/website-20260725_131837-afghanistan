## Introduction

[Lebanon]({{ 'where-does-lebanon-keep-its-strangest/' | relative_url }})'s most unusual visitor attractions are not always the products of national museums or archaeological authorities. Some of the country's most memorable collections began as intensely personal projects: a naturalist's lifetime of preserving animals, or one man's determination to build the medieval castle he had imagined since childhood. Together, the Lebanese Marine and Wildlife Museum near Jeita and Moussa Castle in the Chouf Mountains show how private obsession can become a public destination, while also raising questions about curation, authenticity and visitor expectations.

Unlike state museums, these places are shaped by the personalities of their founders. They mix education, spectacle and autobiography in ways that can be fascinating but also uneven. Visitors should approach them as private worlds rather than conventional museums, appreciating both their originality and their limitations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://tourism-lebanon.com/listing/jeita-lebanese-marine-and-wildlife-museum/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: tourism-lebanon.com">[Lebanon Travel Guide]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">tourism-lebanon.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Lebanon Travel Guide Lebanese marine and wildlife museum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lebanon Travel GuideLebanese marine and wildlife museum - Lebanon Tourism GuideAugust 11, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 11, 2023</span></span></span>

## Preserved wildlife near Jeita

The Lebanese Marine and Wildlife Museum, close to the famous Jeita Grotto, presents one of the country's largest collections of preserved animals. Founded around the private collection of Dr Jamal Younes, it contains thousands of marine and terrestrial specimens gathered over decades, with an emphasis on wildlife from Lebanon alongside examples from other parts of the world. The museum was officially inaugurated in 2015 with an educational mission focused on environmental awareness.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.lbcgroup.tv/news/news-bulletin-reports/241684/report-marine-and-wildlife-museum-inaugurated-in-jeita/en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lbcgroup.tv">[LBCIV7]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lbcgroup.tv</span><span class="citation-popover-title">LBCIV7REPORT: Marine and Wildlife Museum inaugurated in Jeita</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lebanon NewsDecember 4, 2015...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 4, 2015</span></span></span>

The museum deliberately emphasises visual impact. Large predators, oversized marine animals and dramatic display settings are used to capture attention, making it particularly attractive to families and casual tourists. This theatrical style differs markedly from the more research-oriented approach of institutions such as the American University of Beirut's Natural History Museum, whose collections are organised primarily for scientific documentation and biodiversity research.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aub.edu.lb/nhm/Pages/default.aspx" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aub.edu.lb">[American University of Beirut]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aub.edu.lb</span><span class="citation-popover-title">American University of Beirut Natural History Museum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">American University of Beirut Natural History Museum</span></span></span>

## Moussa Castle and the outsider-built attraction

If the museum near Jeita represents one person's fascination with wildlife, Moussa Castle represents one man's lifelong architectural dream.

Situated between Deir el-Qamar and Beiteddine, the castle was built almost entirely through the efforts of Moussa Abdel Karim Al Maamari. According to his widely repeated account, he was mocked at school after drawing his dream castle, left formal education, learned stone construction while working on historic buildings, and eventually spent decades building his own medieval-style fortress. The castle opened to visitors in 1969 and continued expanding for many years afterwards.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moussa_Castle" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Moussa Castle</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Moussa Castle</span></span></span>

Unlike a restored historical monument, Moussa Castle is itself the exhibit. Visitors encounter:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Towers, battlements and stone halls inspired by medieval architecture.
* Wax figures arranged in historical scenes.
* Displays of antique weapons and everyday objects.
* Reconstructed village interiors.
* Personal stories explaining the builder's life and ambitions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moussa_Castle" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Moussa Castle</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Moussa Castle</span></span></span>

</div>

The result occupies an unusual position between architecture, autobiography and folk art. It is neither an authentic medieval fortress nor a conventional history museum. Instead, it is an example of outsider creativity becoming a recognised tourist destination.

Its appeal comes less from historical authenticity than from the extraordinary persistence required to realise such a personal vision. The building stands as a monument to individual determination as much as to Lebanon's medieval past.

## When private collections become public museums

Both attractions illustrate how private museums differ from state institutions.

In national museums, collections are generally acquired through archaeological excavation, scientific research or public heritage programmes, with interpretation developed by professional curators. Private attractions often begin with a single collector's interests, and the narrative follows that person's vision rather than an academic framework.

This has several consequences.

**The displays are highly personal.** Visitors encounter the founder's tastes, priorities and storytelling style instead of a strictly balanced historical interpretation.

**Entertainment and education overlap.** Dramatic presentation is often used to sustain visitor interest, even when the educational content remains genuine.

**Collections can be eclectic.** Scientific specimens, decorative objects, historical recreations and family history may all appear within the same attraction.

**Interpretation varies in depth.** Some exhibits include substantial explanatory material, while others rely mainly on visual impact or guided tours.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://tourism-lebanon.com/listing/jeita-lebanese-marine-and-wildlife-museum/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: tourism-lebanon.com">[Lebanon Travel Guide]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">tourism-lebanon.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Lebanon Travel Guide Lebanese marine and wildlife museum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lebanon Travel GuideLebanese marine and wildlife museum - Lebanon Tourism GuideAugust 11, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 11, 2023</span></span></span>

These characteristics are not weaknesses in themselves. Rather, they define a different museum tradition in which personality is central to the visitor experience.

## Credibility, access and visitor expectations

Neither of these attractions should be judged by the standards of a national archaeological museum.

The Lebanese Marine and Wildlife Museum displays genuine preserved specimens, but its emphasis is educational spectacle rather than scientific research. Visitors interested in taxonomy or conservation science may find less detailed interpretation than they would expect from a university collection, although the sheer scale of the displays remains impressive.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://tourism-lebanon.com/listing/jeita-lebanese-marine-and-wildlife-museum/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: tourism-lebanon.com">[Lebanon Travel Guide]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">tourism-lebanon.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Lebanon Travel Guide Lebanese marine and wildlife museum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lebanon Travel GuideLebanese marine and wildlife museum - Lebanon Tourism GuideAugust 11, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 11, 2023</span></span></span>

Moussa Castle is even more unusual because its historical value lies largely in the modern story of its construction rather than in medieval origins. The castle intentionally recreates an imagined past through architecture, tableaux and collections assembled by its founder. It should therefore be understood as a work of personal heritage rather than an authentic medieval monument.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moussa_Castle" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Moussa Castle</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Moussa Castle</span></span></span>

Both sites remain established tourist attractions with public access, although opening times, admission policies and facilities can change. As with many privately operated attractions in Lebanon, checking directly before travelling is advisable, particularly during periods of political instability or changing tourism conditions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.tripadvisor.co.uk/Attraction_Review-g2624572-d8695806-Reviews-Lebanese_Marine_and_Wildlife_Museum-Jitta_Mount_Lebanon_Governorate.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: tripadvisor.co.uk">[Tripadvisor]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">tripadvisor.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Tripadvisor LEBANESE MARINE AND WILDLIFE MUSEUM</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">LEBANESE MARINE AND WILDLIFE MUSEUM - All You SHOULD Know Before You Go 2026 (w/ Reviews)...</span></span></span>

## Why these places matter in Lebanon's curiosity landscape

Private curiosity museums occupy an important place in Lebanon because they preserve forms of enthusiasm that larger institutions rarely accommodate. A collector's fascination with taxidermy or a builder's lifelong architectural fantasy might never become a government museum, yet these projects have attracted thousands of visitors and become recognised landmarks in their own right.

Together, the museum near Jeita and Moussa Castle demonstrate that curiosity in Lebanon is not confined to ancient archaeology or official heritage. It also emerges from individual passion, where personal collections and imaginative creations gradually evolve into public cultural attractions. Their value lies not only in the objects they display but also in the stories of the people who assembled them, revealing how private obsession can become part of a country's shared cultural landscape.

    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
</script>
