## Introduction

The [Jordan]({{ 'jordans-strange-collections-beyond/' | relative_url }}) Folklore Museum in Amman is a small but important collection of everyday heritage: clothing, household equipment, craft tools, musical instruments and objects connected with work, food preparation and social customs. Unlike archaeological museums that focus on ancient civilisations, this museum preserves the material culture of more recent generations, showing how ordinary objects can reveal how people lived, worked and expressed identity. It was established by Jordan’s Department of Antiquities and opened in 1975 inside the western side of the Roman Theatre complex in Amman.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://museums.visitjordan.com/en/Museum/12" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: museums.visitjordan.com">[museums.visitjordan.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">museums.visitjordan.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Jordan Folklore Museum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Jordan Folklore Museum</span></span></span>

Its unusual value lies in turning familiar items into historical evidence. A coffee-making tool, woven textile, tent object or farming implement may appear simple, but together these objects document different ways of life across Jordan, including desert communities, rural villages and urban households. The museum therefore acts less like a collection of rare curiosities and more like a preserved snapshot of changing social traditions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://museums.visitjordan.com/en/Museum/12" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: museums.visitjordan.com">[museums.visitjordan.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">museums.visitjordan.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Jordan Folklore Museum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Jordan Folklore Museum</span></span></span>

## Traditional objects and community life

The museum’s displays are built around objects from daily life, particularly from the nineteenth and early twentieth centuries. Rather than presenting folklore as only stories, songs or celebrations, the collection shows the physical systems behind those traditions: the things people wore, cooked with, worked with and used to organise domestic life.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://museums.visitjordan.com/en/Museum/12" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: museums.visitjordan.com">[museums.visitjordan.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">museums.visitjordan.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Jordan Folklore Museum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Jordan Folklore Museum</span></span></span>

### Clothing as a record of identity

Traditional clothing is one of the clearest examples of how the collection preserves social memory. The museum includes costumes representing different regions of Jordan, showing variations in design and decoration linked to local communities. These garments are not simply historic clothing items; they represent ideas about belonging, status, regional identity and craft skills.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://museums.visitjordan.com/en/Museum/12" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: museums.visitjordan.com">[museums.visitjordan.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">museums.visitjordan.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Jordan Folklore Museum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Jordan Folklore Museum</span></span></span>

The importance of costume collections in Jordan is also reflected in related heritage institutions, such as the Jordanian Museum of Traditional Costumes and Jewelry, which was created to preserve regional clothing and ornaments as part of national and folk identity. Together, these collections show how textiles can preserve stories about communities even when everyday practices change.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://museums.visitjordan.com/en/Museum/9" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: museums.visitjordan.com">[museums.visitjordan.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">museums.visitjordan.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Jordanian Museum of Traditional Costumes and Jewelry</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Jordanian Museum of Traditional Costumes and Jewelry</span></span></span>

### Household tools and the forgotten technology of daily life

Many of the museum’s most revealing objects are ordinary domestic tools. Items connected with preparing food, making bread, preparing coffee and tea, and managing household tasks show the practical knowledge that shaped everyday routines.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://museums.visitjordan.com/en/Museum/12" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: museums.visitjordan.com">[museums.visitjordan.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">museums.visitjordan.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Jordan Folklore Museum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Jordan Folklore Museum</span></span></span>

These objects are valuable because they preserve techniques that are often difficult to capture through written records. A tool used in a kitchen or workshop carries evidence of materials, skills and habits: what people produced locally, how labour was divided, and which customs surrounded hospitality and family life.

The museum also includes traditional craft examples such as rugs, tents, straw work, wool production and pottery. These displays connect objects with the skills required to make them, highlighting craftsmanship as part of cultural memory rather than simply as decoration.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://museums.visitjordan.com/en/Museum/12" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: museums.visitjordan.com">[museums.visitjordan.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">museums.visitjordan.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Jordan Folklore Museum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Jordan Folklore Museum</span></span></span>

## Bedouin and rural heritage displays

One of the museum’s defining features is its attempt to represent different environments and communities rather than a single version of Jordanian tradition. The official description divides the collection into three broad cultural settings: desert heritage, rural life and urban culture.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://museums.visitjordan.com/en/Museum/12" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: museums.visitjordan.com">[museums.visitjordan.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">museums.visitjordan.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Jordan Folklore Museum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Jordan Folklore Museum</span></span></span>

### Desert life preserved through objects

The desert displays focus on material culture associated with Bedouin communities. Visitors can see objects connected with tent life, movement, craftsmanship and hospitality. The collection includes examples of traditional instruments, equestrian equipment and other items linked with desert lifestyles.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://museums.visitjordan.com/en/Museum/12" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: museums.visitjordan.com">[museums.visitjordan.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">museums.visitjordan.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Jordan Folklore Museum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Jordan Folklore Museum</span></span></span>

A notable feature described by visitor guides is a Bedouin goat-hair tent display with associated tools, alongside objects such as a one-stringed musical instrument, weaving equipment, coffee grinders and traditional clothing. These exhibits help explain how mobility, animal resources and handmade production shaped life in desert environments.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.lonelyplanet.com/points-of-interest/folklore-museum/418191" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lonelyplanet.com">[Lonely Planet]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lonelyplanet.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Lonely Planet Folklore Museum &#124; Amman, Jordan &#124; Attractions</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lonely PlanetFolklore Museum &#124; Amman, Jordan &#124; Attractions - Lonely Planet...</span></span></span>

### Rural skills and agricultural traditions

The rural displays focus on the tools and objects that supported village life. Agricultural equipment and craft materials show how communities relied on local production, seasonal work and practical knowledge passed between generations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://museums.visitjordan.com/en/Museum/12" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: museums.visitjordan.com">[museums.visitjordan.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">museums.visitjordan.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Jordan Folklore Museum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Jordan Folklore Museum</span></span></span>

For visitors, these objects provide a different view of heritage from the grand monuments usually associated with Jordan. Instead of focusing on rulers, battles or ancient cities, the collection highlights the experiences of farmers, craftspeople and families whose lives formed the everyday foundation of society.

## How folklore collections preserve memory

The Jordan Folklore Museum matters because it demonstrates how museums can preserve forms of history that are easily lost. Everyday objects often disappear because they are used until they wear out, replaced by modern alternatives or considered too ordinary to save. Collecting them changes their meaning: a cooking utensil becomes evidence of domestic traditions; a piece of clothing becomes a record of regional identity; a craft tool becomes a link to disappearing skills.

The museum’s approach is therefore based on preservation rather than mystery. Its “curious” quality comes from seeing familiar objects in an unfamiliar way.       return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
