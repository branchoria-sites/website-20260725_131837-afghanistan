## Introduction

Iranian doll collections preserve local identities by turning small handmade figures into records of clothing, occupations, rituals and everyday life. Unlike ordinary toy displays, these collections often work as miniature ethnographic archives: a doll may represent a village craftsperson, a regional costume, a seasonal ceremony or a story passed between generations. The best-known examples are found in private and community-led museums rather than large national institutions, where collectors and makers have used dolls to document traditions that are changing or disappearing.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://visitiran.ir/attraction/Kashan--Puppet--and--Toy--Museum--%28Isfahan-Province%29" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: visitiran.ir">[Visit Iran]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">visitiran.ir</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Visit Iran Kashan Puppet and Toy Museum (Isfahan Province) &#124; Visit iran</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Visit Iran Kashan Puppet and Toy Museum (Isfahan Province) &#124; Visit iran</span></span></span>

The unusual quality of Iran’s doll collections is that they sit between toy history and cultural preservation. A visitor is not simply looking at decorative figures; they are encountering attempts to preserve memory through fabric, stitching and storytelling. Collections in Tehran, Kashan and Badrood show how dolls can record regional diversity, from traditional clothing to forgotten occupations, while also raising questions about how accurately miniature figures can represent living communities.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://visitiran.ir/attraction/Kashan--Puppet--and--Toy--Museum--%28Isfahan-Province%29" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: visitiran.ir">[Visit Iran]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">visitiran.ir</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Visit Iran Kashan Puppet and Toy Museum (Isfahan Province) &#124; Visit iran</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Visit Iran Kashan Puppet and Toy Museum (Isfahan Province) &#124; Visit iran</span></span></span>

## Regional dolls and cultural identity

Iran’s regional dolls are often valuable because they preserve details of local life that are easily lost: patterns of dress, household work, rural occupations and community ceremonies. A handmade figure can capture a style of clothing or a way of life at a moment when younger generations may no longer experience those traditions directly.

The Kashan Puppet and Toy Museum is one of the clearest examples of this approach. Its collection was developed through years of travel and research by collector Amir Sohrabi, who gathered dolls and puppets from different parts of Iran. The museum displays local dolls alongside older puppets and toys, with guides explaining the stories connected to individual objects.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://visitiran.ir/attraction/Kashan--Puppet--and--Toy--Museum--%28Isfahan-Province%29" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: visitiran.ir">[Visit Iran]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">visitiran.ir</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Visit Iran Kashan Puppet and Toy Museum (Isfahan Province) &#124; Visit iran</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Visit Iran Kashan Puppet and Toy Museum (Isfahan Province) &#124; Visit iran</span></span></span>

The Kashan collection is particularly unusual because its displays are organised around named regional figures rather than anonymous objects. Different dolls are linked to local traditions, including figures associated with areas such as Khorasan, northern Iran and Kurdish communities. This approach changes the visitor experience: instead of seeing a costume as a museum specimen, visitors encounter a character connected to a place, a story and a social setting.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://visitiran.ir/attraction/Kashan--Puppet--and--Toy--Museum--%28Isfahan-Province%29" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: visitiran.ir">[Visit Iran]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">visitiran.ir</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Visit Iran Kashan Puppet and Toy Museum (Isfahan Province) &#124; Visit iran</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Visit Iran Kashan Puppet and Toy Museum (Isfahan Province) &#124; Visit iran</span></span></span>

### Clothing as a record of place

Traditional clothing is one of the strongest forms of identity preserved through Iranian dolls. The garments often provide clues about geography, climate, craft skills and social customs. For example, educational collections featuring Iranian dolls have used regional dress from areas such as Gilan to show how clothing reflected ceremonial occasions and local textile traditions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://exhibits.library.utoronto.ca/exhibits/show/maria-de-lourdes-orozco-cuautl/iranian-doll/clothing" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: exhibits.library.utoronto.ca">[exhibits.library.utoronto.ca]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">exhibits.library.utoronto.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Clothing · Learning from Dolls · Exhibits</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Clothing · Learning from Dolls · Exhibits</span></span></span>

This makes dolls useful as cultural teaching tools, especially for younger audiences. A full-sized costume in an archive may be difficult to interpret without context, but a dressed figure immediately connects clothing with a person and a story. The limitation is that a doll is still an interpretation: it selects certain features and may simplify differences within a community. Museums and collectors therefore have to balance celebration of heritage with careful research.

## Collectors preserving local stories

Many Iranian doll collections exist because individual collectors chose to document traditions that were not always represented in formal museums. These collectors often combine research, craft production and public education, creating small heritage spaces that feel closer to a living archive than a conventional exhibition.

The Dr Rahgoshay Museum in Badrood is a notable example. Its doll project, “History of Badrood through handmade traditional dolls”, aimed to reconnect younger generations with local history by creating figures based on people, occupations and customs from the region. The museum states that its dolls include identity cards, with figures representing individuals, professions and locations within the community.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.rahgoshaymuseum.com/en/museum-sections/500-dolls-museum-en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: rahgoshaymuseum.com">[rahgoshaymuseum.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">rahgoshaymuseum.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Dolls museum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Dolls museum</span></span></span>

The Badrood collection is distinctive because it treats dolls almost like miniature biographies. Rather than only displaying a generic farmer, artisan or village woman, the project attempts to attach names, roles and social histories to figures. The museum describes dolls representing occupations such as farming, herbal knowledge, metalworking, caravan transport and carpet weaving, showing how everyday work becomes part of heritage preservation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.rahgoshaymuseum.com/en/ethnic-dolls-en/dolls-museum-en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: rahgoshaymuseum.com">[rahgoshaymuseum.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">rahgoshaymuseum.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Dolls museum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">March 5, 2016...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 5, 2016</span></span></span>

This method reflects a wider shift in heritage collecting: preserving ordinary lives rather than only famous people or grand monuments. A small handmade doll can represent the memory of a craft that has declined, a local occupation replaced by modern technology, or a domestic skill that was traditionally passed down within families.

## Toys, puppets and social memory

Iranian doll collections are also connected to older traditions of puppetry, storytelling and children’s play. Puppets and dolls have long occupied a space between entertainment and cultural transmission, allowing communities to pass on stories, humour and values.

The Kashan Puppet and Toy Museum combines dolls with puppets and historic toys, including examples connected with the Qajar period and older forms of performance culture. The museum’s setting in a historic house adds to its unusual character: rather than a large institutional gallery, it resembles a collector’s cultural house where objects are tied closely to personal research and local memory.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://visitiran.ir/attraction/Kashan--Puppet--and--Toy--Museum--%28Isfahan-Province%29" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: visitiran.ir">[Visit Iran]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">visitiran.ir</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Visit Iran Kashan Puppet and Toy Museum (Isfahan Province) &#124; Visit iran</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Visit Iran Kashan Puppet and Toy Museum (Isfahan Province) &#124; Visit iran</span></span></span>

The Tehran-based International Doll Museum presents another side of doll collecting. Its collection includes dolls from many countries as well as figures showing Iranian ethnic clothing, placing Iranian traditions within a wider international context.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.toiran.com/index.php/en/city-tehran/museums/International-Doll-Museum/1905" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: toiran.com">[ToIran, Tourism Platform]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">toiran.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">To Iran, Tourism Platform International Doll Museum &#124; To Iran, Tourism Platform</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">To Iran, Tourism Platform International Doll Museum &#124; To Iran, Tourism Platform</span></span></span>

Dolls can therefore work as a bridge between private memory and public heritage. A child’s toy, a handmade village figure or a collector’s object can all become evidence of how people represented themselves and their communities.

## The challenge of preserving living traditions

The importance of Iranian doll collections comes partly from their fragile nature. Many regional traditions survive through makers, storytellers and families rather than through written records. When those practices change, dolls can provide a visible reminder of earlier ways of life.

However, these collections should be understood as interpretations rather than perfect replicas of the past. A doll cannot capture every variation within a region, and costume choices may reflect the perspective of the maker or collector. Their value lies less in being complete records and more in preserving conversations about identity, memory and belonging.

Iran’s wider heritage work has increasingly recognised the importance of intangible culture — traditions, skills and practices that are not limited to physical monuments.   function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
