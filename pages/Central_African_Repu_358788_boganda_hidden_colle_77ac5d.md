## Introduction

The Barthélémy Boganda National Museum in Bangui is often described as the [Central African Republic]({{ 'central-african-republic/' | relative_url }})'s hidden museum because much of its collection has spent more than a decade out of public view. Rather than disappearing entirely during the violence that engulfed the country in 2013, thousands of archaeological, historical and ethnographic objects survived through an improvised strategy: museum staff concealed and packed much of the collection into large wooden crates and protected what they could inside the damaged building. The result is an unusual heritage story. Instead of empty galleries signalling total loss, the museum became a repository of unseen treasures awaiting conservation, documentation and eventual redisplay.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aliph-foundation.org/en/projects/protecting-heritage-at-the-barthelemy-boganda-museum" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aliph-foundation.org">[Aliph Foundation]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aliph-foundation.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Aliph FoundationProtecting Heritage at the Barthélémy Boganda Museum &#124; Aliph Foundation - Protecting heritage to build peace...</span></span></span>

For visitors interested in unusual museums and endangered collections, the museum's hidden stores are more significant than its current exhibition spaces. They illustrate how cultural heritage can survive conflict through the determination of a small museum team, even when regular public access becomes impossible.

## What the national collection contains

The Barthélémy Boganda National Museum is the Central African Republic's principal national repository for archaeological, historical and ethnographic material. Unlike specialist museums devoted to a single theme, it brings together objects representing the country's diverse cultures and landscapes.

The surviving collection includes:

* Traditional masks and ceremonial objects.
* Musical instruments and performance artefacts.
* Hunting equipment and weapons.
* Pottery, coins and archaeological finds.
* Religious and [ritual objects]({{ 'ritual-objects-fdf1d8/' | relative_url }}).
* Ethnographic collections representing communities from across the country.
* Some natural-history specimens alongside historical collections.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boganda_National_Museum" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Boganda National Museum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Boganda National Museum</span></span></span>

This broad mixture is what makes the museum especially interesting within Central African Republic's curiosity landscape. A carved mask, for example, is not presented simply as an unusual object but as evidence of performance, belief, craftsmanship and social identity. Likewise, ritual artefacts gain meaning through their cultural context rather than being displayed merely as mysterious relics.

Because much of the collection has remained boxed for years, many objects have become known through conservation reports rather than gallery displays.

## How concealed crates helped the collection survive

The museum suffered extensive looting and damage during the civil conflict that erupted in 2013. Display cases were broken, buildings were damaged and many cultural institutions across the country were left vulnerable.

Yet the museum's losses were not total. Reports from conservation organisations and journalists indicate that staff managed to preserve the majority of the collection by rapidly removing objects from vulnerable displays and packing them into large wooden crates stored inside the museum. Although some objects were stolen or damaged, several thousand remained protected in storage rather than exposed in abandoned galleries.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boganda_National_Museum" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Boganda National Museum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Boganda National Museum</span></span></span>

This emergency storage was never intended as a long-term solution. Objects remained stacked in improvised conditions for years, wrapped in paper or packed into crates designed simply to keep them together rather than to provide proper conservation. Nevertheless, the strategy proved remarkably successful compared with the complete destruction experienced by some museums in conflict zones.

The hidden collection therefore became both a symbol of survival and a reminder that preservation often depends on practical decisions taken under extreme pressure rather than carefully planned emergency procedures.

## Recovery has focused on conservation rather than exhibition

Saving the collection was only the first step. Years of storage in unsuitable conditions created new conservation challenges.

The current recovery programme, supported by the International Alliance for the Protection of Heritage in Conflict Areas (ALIPH) and implemented with the École du Patrimoine Africain (EPA), concentrates on stabilising the hidden collection before expanding public display. The work includes:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Removing objects from deteriorating storage.
* Cleaning and preventive conservation.
* Installing suitable shelving and storage systems.
* Improving documentation and inventory records.
* Training museum staff in long-term collections management.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aliph-foundation.org/en/projects/protecting-heritage-at-the-barthelemy-boganda-museum" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aliph-foundation.org">[aliph-foundation.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aliph-foundation.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Aliph FoundationProtecting Heritage at the Barthélémy Boganda Museum &#124; Aliph Foundation - Protecting heritage to build peace...</span></span></span>

</div>

This emphasis reflects a common principle in museum conservation: objects cannot safely return to exhibition until they have first been stabilised, documented and rehoused.

In late 2025, EPA specialists and museum staff continued practical conservation work on the collections, showing that recovery remains an active, long-term process rather than a completed restoration.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.epa-prema.net/chantier-professionnel-des-collections-au-musee-national-barthelemy-boganda-republique-centrafricaine/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: epa-prema.net">[Ecole du Patrimoine Africain – EPA]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">epa-prema.net</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ecole du Patrimoine Africain – EPA...</span></span></span>

## Why visitor access remains uncertain

One of the most unusual aspects of the Boganda Museum is that its most important collection is largely invisible.

There have been occasional public events demonstrating that parts of the museum can be reopened temporarily, but these should not be mistaken for a fully functioning national museum with permanent exhibitions. Conservation organisations continue to describe the project primarily as one of safeguarding collections rather than expanding visitor facilities.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aliph-foundation.org/en/projects/protecting-heritage-at-the-barthelemy-boganda-museum" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aliph-foundation.org">[Aliph Foundation]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aliph-foundation.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Aliph FoundationProtecting Heritage at the Barthélémy Boganda Museum &#124; Aliph Foundation - Protecting heritage to build peace...</span></span></span>

As a result, travellers should not expect to see thousands of objects on permanent display. Instead, the museum is better understood as a recovering institution where preservation currently takes priority over exhibition.

This hidden status has also inspired recent cultural attention. A 2026 documentary described the museum as a "ghost museum", portraying galleries left largely empty while rescued artefacts remained stored in crates and wrapped for protection, with staff continuing to care for the collection despite the prolonged closure.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gazette-drouot.com/en/article/nothing-is-forgotten-documentary-about-a-ghost-museum-in/95339" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: gazette-drouot.com">[Drouot Gazette]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">gazette-drouot.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Drouot GazetteNothing is forgotten documentary... &#124; Gazette DrouotJanuary 6, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 6, 2026</span></span></span>

## Why the hidden collection matters

The concealed collection has become an important example of heritage survival during armed conflict.

Rather than representing a forgotten museum, the boxed collections demonstrate that preservation sometimes means deliberately removing objects from public view until conditions become safe enough for conservation and display. In this respect, the museum differs from conventional curiosity museums, where unusual objects are exhibited for visitors. Here, the hidden nature of the collection is itself part of the story.

Within the Central African Republic's cultural landscape, the museum preserves irreplaceable evidence of archaeological discoveries, artistic traditions, ritual practices and everyday life that might otherwise have disappeared during years of instability. Its recovery is therefore not simply about reopening galleries, but about ensuring that the country's historical memory survives for future generations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aliph-foundation.org/en/projects/protecting-heritage-at-the-barthelemy-boganda-museum" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aliph-foundation.org">[aliph-foundation.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aliph-foundation.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Aliph FoundationProtecting Heritage at the Barthélémy Boganda Museum &#124; Aliph Foundation - Protecting heritage to build peace...</span></span></span>

    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
