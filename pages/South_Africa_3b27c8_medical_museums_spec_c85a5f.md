## Introduction

[South Africa]({{ 'where-south-africa-keeps-its-strangest/' | relative_url }})’s medical museums are unusual because they transform places of treatment into places of interpretation. Instead of presenting surgery as a triumphal tale or a display of anatomical curiosities alone, they preserve operating theatres, obsolete instruments, pharmacies and consulting rooms to show how medical knowledge changed over time—and how scientific breakthroughs were inseparable from ethical debate. The result is a form of museum theatre in which visitors step into reconstructed clinical spaces, encounter the equipment that once seemed cutting-edge, and confront the human stories behind landmark operations.

The country's best-known examples are the Adler Museum of Medicine in Johannesburg and the Heart of Cape Town Museum at Groote Schuur Hospital. Together they demonstrate how South African medical collections balance dramatic presentation with historical explanation, turning surgery into spectacle without losing sight of the patients, practitioners and moral questions involved.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.wits.ac.za/health/adlermuseum/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: wits.ac.za">[Wits University]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">wits.ac.za</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Wits University Adler Museum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Wits UniversityAdler Museum - Wits University...</span></span></span>

## The Adler Museum turns medical history into a walk through changing ideas

At the University of the Witwatersrand in Johannesburg, the Adler Museum of Medicine is less a cabinet of horrors than a carefully organised history of healthcare in southern Africa. Founded in 1962 from the collections of Dr Cyril and Esther Adler, it now preserves thousands of objects covering medicine, dentistry and pharmacy, alongside archives, rare books and photographs.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.wits.ac.za/health/adlermuseum/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: wits.ac.za">[Wits University]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">wits.ac.za</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Wits University Adler Museum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Wits UniversityAdler Museum - Wits University...</span></span></span>

Rather than placing instruments behind glass with minimal explanation, the museum uses reconstructed interiors to immerse visitors in earlier forms of medical practice. Among its most memorable displays are:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--comparison" markdown="1">

* An early twentieth-century Johannesburg pharmacy filled with bottles, scales and dispensing equipment.
* A period doctor's consulting room showing how diagnosis and patient care once took place.
* A reconstructed dental surgery displaying equipment that now appears intimidating but was once routine.
* Historical optometry displays and collections illustrating changing diagnostic technology.
* Displays comparing Western medicine with traditions such as acupuncture, Ayurveda and Unani (Tibb), placing different healing systems into historical context rather than presenting medicine as a single linear story.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://joburg.org.za/play_/Pages/Play%20in%20Joburg/Culture%20and%20Heritage/Links/Museums/Specialist%20museum/Adler-Museum-of-Medicine.aspx" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: joburg.org.za">[joburg.org.za]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">joburg.org.za</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Adler Museum of Medicine</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Adler Museum of Medicine</span></span></span>

</div>

Much of the fascination comes from seeing familiar medical practices stripped of modern design. Brass syringes, hand-powered instruments, glass containers and heavy steel surgical tools remind visitors that many procedures regarded as ordinary today were once physically demanding, uncertain and often painful. The museum therefore creates drama not through sensational specimens but through the material culture of medicine itself.

## Walking through the world's first human heart transplant

The most theatrical medical museum experience in South Africa is found inside Groote Schuur Hospital in Cape Town, where the Heart of Cape Town Museum preserves the original spaces associated with the world's first successful human-to-human heart transplant, performed by Professor Christiaan Barnard and his team on 3 December 1967. The museum occupies the original operating theatre complex rather than a purpose-built exhibition hall, allowing visitors to move through the actual locations where one of medicine's defining moments unfolded.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://heartofcapetown.co.za/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: heartofcapetown.co.za">[Heart of Cape Town Museum]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">heartofcapetown.co.za</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on heartofcapetown.co.za.</span></span></span>

The guided route deliberately follows the chronology of the operation. Visitors encounter recreated scenes from the donor's story, research laboratories where transplantation techniques were developed, Barnard's office, sterilisation rooms and finally the adjoining operating theatres where donor and recipient surgeries occurred simultaneously. Lifelike figures, original equipment and preserved layouts recreate the atmosphere of the night while guides explain the medical decisions step by step.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://heartofcapetown.co.za/guided-tour-program/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: heartofcapetown.co.za">[Heart of Cape Town Museum]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">heartofcapetown.co.za</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on heartofcapetown.co.za.</span></span></span>

Unlike many science museums that separate objects from place, the preserved theatres themselves are the principal artefacts. Their authenticity allows visitors to appreciate how much of the procedure depended not only on Barnard but on anaesthetists, nurses, technicians, laboratory researchers and dozens of other specialists whose work is often overshadowed by the famous surgeon. The museum explicitly presents the transplant as a collective achievement rather than the accomplishment of a single individual.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://heartofcapetown.co.za/about/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: heartofcapetown.co.za">[Heart of Cape Town Museum]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">heartofcapetown.co.za</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on heartofcapetown.co.za.</span></span></span>

## Why the operation remains compelling

The museum's power lies partly in the fact that the operation was never simply a surgical milestone. It forced society to confront new questions about the definition of death, organ donation and the ethics of transplantation.

Tours therefore move beyond technical achievement to discuss:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Denise Darvall, whose fatal accident made the transplant possible.
* Louis Washkansky, the recipient whose survival demonstrated that transplantation could succeed, even though he died eighteen days later from complications related to immunosuppression.
* The evolving concept of brain death, which became increasingly important in transplantation medicine.
* International admiration alongside religious and ethical criticism that followed the operation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heart_of_Cape_Town_Museum" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[wikipedia.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Heart of Cape Town Museum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Heart of Cape Town Museum</span></span></span>

</div>

By preserving newspaper reports, correspondence and contemporary reactions, the museum shows that spectacular surgery generated controversy as well as celebration. Visitors encounter the uncertainty surrounding the procedure rather than a simple narrative of inevitable scientific progress.

## Balancing spectacle with ethics

Medical museums face an unusual challenge. The operating theatre naturally attracts attention because it is dramatic, visually distinctive and associated with life-and-death decisions. Yet excessive theatricality risks reducing patients into supporting characters for famous surgeons.

South Africa's leading medical museums largely avoid that trap through interpretation rather than shock value.

At the Adler Museum, obsolete equipment is contextualised within changing medical knowledge, encouraging visitors to ask why earlier practitioners worked as they did instead of inviting ridicule of outdated techniques. The emphasis falls on education, research and preservation rather than spectacle for its own sake.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.wits.ac.za/health/adlermuseum/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: wits.ac.za">[Wits University]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">wits.ac.za</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Wits University Adler Museum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Wits UniversityAdler Museum - Wits University...</span></span></span>

At the Heart of Cape Town Museum, reconstructed scenes undoubtedly create emotional impact, but guides consistently return to broader themes: teamwork, scientific experimentation, donor generosity and the ethical debates that surrounded transplantation. The preserved operating rooms become settings for discussing how medicine evolves through both technical innovation and public trust.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://heartofcapetown.co.za/guided-tour-program/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: heartofcapetown.co.za">[Heart of Cape Town Museum]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">heartofcapetown.co.za</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on heartofcapetown.co.za.</span></span></span>

## Why these museums matter in South Africa's curiosity landscape

Among South Africa's unusual collections, medical museums occupy a distinctive position because they preserve moments when scientific practice became public drama. Unlike folklore museums or outsider-art environments, their fascination comes from authentic places and instruments that once served urgent practical purposes.

The Adler Museum demonstrates how everyday healthcare accumulated its own material culture, preserving objects that reveal changing attitudes towards diagnosis, pharmacy and surgery. The Heart of Cape Town Museum captures a single historic event that reshaped modern medicine while acknowledging the human cost, ethical uncertainty and collaborative effort behind it.

Together they show that South Africa's strangest museum experiences are not necessarily built around the bizarre or macabre. Sometimes the most compelling exhibits are ordinary clinical rooms preserved exactly as they were, where visitors can stand in the space between scientific achievement and human vulnerability.

    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
